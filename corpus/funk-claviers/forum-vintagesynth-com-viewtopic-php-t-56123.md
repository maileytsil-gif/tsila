 Post 
 by LWG » Sat May 08, 2010 9:19 am

 Hello,

I believe the wiki info to be inaccurate.

Even after drum machines came to be used in funk/dance, sequencing was never a dominant element, 

because the music was still mainly R&B-based. This was especially true of bands such as this that

started out in the early/mid 70s as funk bands. 

One of Cameo's lead singers at the time, Larry Blackmon was also the band's drummer (a good one at that).

What you often hear is a Simmons kit being played. 

Cameo - "She's Strange" (1984)

Cameo - "Hanging Downtown" (1984) Lovely ballad. Electronic percussion, but everything still played.

Even after the emergence of New Wave, such as with the material from the Minneapolis bands, you had a 

stripped down, largely keyboard-based sound, but there was still a drummer, often playing an electronic kit.

Andre Cymone - "Everything" (Live 84)
