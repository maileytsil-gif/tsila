# stevie-clavinet

Anonymously sourced Clavinet, improved from musical-artifacts.

Source: //https://musical-artifacts.com/artifacts/646

Named to honor Stevie Wonder.

Modifications by Jeff Learman

## Changes

- Format changes:
  - Normalized all samples
  - converted from 24 bits to 16 bits
  - converted from WAV to FLAC
  - removed redundant files
  - reduced from 500M to 60M
- Functional changes
  - set fast release time, as I recall from playing actual clavinets back in the 70's
  - extended to full keyboard (using highest and lowest samples)
  - fixed dynamic response (using `veltrack`)

## From musical-artifacts.com:

The clavinet is an electrically amplified clavichord that was invented by Ernst Zacharias and manufactured by the Hohner company of Trossingen, West Germany from 1964 to the early 1980s. Hohner produced seven models over the years, designated I, II, L, C, D6, E7 and Duo. Its distinctive bright staccato sound has featured most prominently in funk, jazz-funk, reggae, rock, and soul songs.
