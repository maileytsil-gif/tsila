## 
 Original Audio

 Herbie Hancock’s 1983 electro-funk masterpiece “Rockit” features a distinctive lead synthesizer sound that emerges prominently at 0:27, driving the track’s rhythmic foundation through syncopated 16th-note patterns. Originally performed on the Rhodes Chroma, this sound combines a piercing bright timbre to cut through the mix, creating a dynamic interplay with the track’s scratching and robotic visuals.
