 false 
 Rick Beato goes over track elements       04/08/21

 Rick Beato has worked as a musician, songwriter, audio engineer and record producer - in this video he goes over each of the elements in Bruno Mars' massive hit 24K Magic. There are some nice tips within the video, with each track being isolated and explained. Alongside the drums and synths, you'll hear subtle bass and vocal elements added low in the mix which really help thicken up the sound.
