## 1. Ableton Live 12 — instruments (manuel, chapitre 30)

Source officielle : https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/live-instrument-reference/ (bloquée en lecture directe). Texte lu sur le miroir : https://raw.githubusercontent.com/djaboxx/iron-static/main/docs/api/ableton/30-live-instrument-reference.md (pages PDF 649–768).

### 1.1 Electric (§30.5, p. 681–685) — Rhodes / Wurlitzer

- [DOC] « Electric is a software electric piano developed in collaboration with Applied Acoustics Systems. It is based on the classic instruments of the seventies; each component has been modeled using cutting edge physical modeling technology. […] No sampling or wavetables are used in Electric ». Le manuel ajoute que ses paramètres « can be tweaked to values not possible with the real instruments ».
- [DOC] Mécanique modélisée : « A note played on the keyboard activates a hammer that hits a fork. The sound of that fork is then amplified by a magnetic coil pickup […] The fork is made of two parts, called the tine bar and tone bar. The tine bar is where the hammer hits the fork while the tone bar is a tuned metal resonator ». Quatre sections : **Hammer, Fork, Damper/Pickup, Global**. (Le manuel 12 nomme la section « Hammer », pas « Mallet » ; l'extrait WebSearch d'une page de pack Ableton parle encore de « Mallet ».)
- [DOC] **Hammer** : *Stiffness* (dureté de la surface de frappe ; plus haut = plus brillant ; modulable par *Vel* et *Key*) ; *Noise* (bruit d'impact) avec sous-paramètres *Pitch* (fréquence centrale du bruit), *Decay*, *Key* ; *Force* (« intensity of the hammer's impact on the fork », amount + Vel + Key).
- [DOC] **Fork** : *Tine* = « the portion of the fork that is directly struck by the hammer » avec *Color* (« relative amplitude of high and low partials in the tine's spectrum »), *Decay*, *Key* (niveau selon la hauteur) ; *Tone* = « the secondary resonance of the fork » avec *Decay* ; *Release* commun Tine+Tone (« decay time of the fork's sound after a key is released »).
- [DOC] **Pickup** : *Symmetry* = position verticale du micro ; « At 50%, the pickup is directly in front of the tine, which results in a brighter sound » ; *Distance* = éloignement ; « the sound becomes more overdriven as the pickup approaches the tine ». **Type R / W** : « In the R position, Electric simulates electro-dynamic pickups, while W is based on an electro-static model » — c'est la seule formulation du manuel 12 ; **il ne nomme pas Rhodes ni Wurlitzer**. L'association R = Rhodes (électromagnétique) / W = Wurlitzer (électrostatique) est explicitée sur la page du pack Electric : « Two pickup models: electromagnetic (Rhodes®) and electrostatic (Wurlitzer®) » [DOC-extrait, https://www.ableton.com/en/packs/electric/ via WebSearch]. *Input* (part du signal de la fourche envoyée au micro → **quantité de distorsion**) et *Output* : « a low amount of input with a high amount of output will produce a cleaner sound than a high input with a low output ». *Key* module la sortie.
- [DOC] **Damper** : *Tone* (dureté des étouffoirs, plus haut = plus brillant), *Level* (niveau du bruit d'étouffoir), *Att/Rel* : « At -100, damper noise will only be heard during the note's attack phase. At 100, the noise is present only during the release phase. In the center, an equal amount ».
- [DOC] **Global** : *Volume* ; *Voices* (polyphonie, coût CPU par voix) ; *Semi* et *Detune* (± 50 cents) ; *Stretch* = accord étiré (« At 0%, Electric will play in equal temperament ») ; la suite du paragraphe (Stretch Center) est sur la page 686 du PDF, non incluse dans le miroir — voir §8.
- [RELEVÉ-Set] Les 34 paramètres exposés à l'API Live, relevés dans le Set de l'utilisateur (`vst-sound-design/references/instruments-natifs.md`) : Voices, PB Range, Note PB Range, Volume, Semitone, Detune, KB Stretch, M Stiffness, M Stiff < Key, M Stiff < Vel, M Force, M Force < Key, M Force < Vel, Noise Pitch, Noise Decay, Noise Amount, Noise < Key, F Release, F Tine Decay, F Tine Vol, F Tine < Key, F Tine Color, F Tone Decay, F Tone Vol, P Symmetry, P Distance, Pickup Model, P Amp In, P Amp Out, P Amp < Key, Damp Tone, Damp Amount, Damp Balance. (L'API garde le préfixe « M » de *Mallet* et « P Amp » pour Input/Output.)
- [HEUR] Un atlas tiers (LivePilot, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dreamrec/LivePilot/main/livepilot/skills/livepilot-core/references/device-atlas/synths-native.md) propose des plages de départ : Stiffness 50–70 % « naturel », > 80 % « métallique » ; Mallet Noise 10–20 % ; Tine Decay court = « clav-like ». Non constructeur, à traiter comme [TEST].

### 1.2 Analog (§30.1, p. 649–657) — Minimoog / Odyssey / Prophet

- [DOC] « Analog is a virtual analog synthesizer, created in collaboration with Applied Acoustics Systems. With this instrument, we have not attempted to emulate a specific vintage analog synthesizer but rather to combine different features of legendary vintage synthesizers ». Architecture : 2 oscillateurs + générateur de bruit → 2 filtres multimodes (série ou parallèle) → 2 amplis ; 2 LFO ; une enveloppe par filtre et par ampli.
- [DOC] **Oscillateurs** : formes *sine, sawtooth, rectangular, white noise* ; « When rectangular is selected, the Pulse Width parameter is enabled […] At 100%, the waveform is a perfect square, resulting in only odd harmonics. The pulse width can also be modulated by an LFO » (PWM disponible seulement si le LFO correspondant est actif). *Octave/Semi/Detune* (± 300 cents). *Key* (tracking clavier, 100 % = tempérament égal). *Pitch Env* (Initial + Time). **Sub/Sync** : mode *Sub* = « an additional oscillator, tuned an octave below the main oscillator. The sub-oscillator produces a square wave when the main oscillator's Shape control is set to rectangle or sawtooth and a sine wave when the main oscillator is set to sine » ; mode *Sync* = hard sync par oscillateur interne, *Ratio* (« For maximum analog nastiness, try mapping a modulation wheel […] to the Sync ratio »). *F1/F2* : balance de routage vers les deux filtres.
- [DOC] **Noise** : bruit blanc avec passe-bas −6 dB/oct (*Color*).
- [DOC] **Filtres** : « 2nd and 4th order low-pass, band-pass, notch, high-pass and formant filters » ; « When a formant filter is chosen […] the Reso control cycles between vowel sounds » ; *To F2* (part de F1 envoyée à F2), *Follow* (F2 suit la coupure de F1 avec offset) ; **Drive** : « three Sym options apply symmetrical distortion […] The Asym modes result in asymmetrical saturation. For both mode types, higher numbers result in more distortion ».
- [DOC] **Enveloppes** : ADSR avec *Att < Vel*, *Env < Vel*, *S.Time* (le sustain décroît même touche tenue), *Slope* lin/exp, **Legato** (« a new note that is played while another note is already depressed will use the first note's envelope, at its current position »), *Free* (mode trigger, « ideal for percussive sounds »), *Loop* Off / AD-R / ADR-R / ADS-R.
- [DOC] **LFO** : sine, triangle, rectangle, deux bruits (pas / rampes) ; *Width* (pulse width du tri/rect), *Delay*, *Attack*, *Retrig*, *Offset* ; Rate en Hz ou synchronisé.
- [DOC] **Global** : **Vibrato** = « essentially an additional LFO, but is hardwired to the pitch of both oscillators » avec Rate, Delay, Attack, *Error* (déviation aléatoire par voix), *Amt < MW* ; **Unison** (*Uni* + *Detune*, *Voices* 2 ou 4, *Delay*) ; **Glide** (*Gli* + *Time*, **Legato** : « the sliding will only occur if the second note is played before the first note is released », mode *Const* ou *Prop*) ; **Quick Routing** (4 boutons : parallèle, split, tout dans F1/Amp1, série F1→F2) ; Keyboard : Octave/Semi/Detune (± 50 cents), *PB Range*, *Stretch*, *Error*, *Voices*, *Priority* High/Low/Last.
- [DOC-extrait, https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/live-instrument-reference/] « Unison mode uses 4 voices per note and has a maximum of 8-note polyphony » — cette phrase appartient en fait à la section Drift (voir 1.4) ; le moteur l'a rattachée à Analog. Pour Analog, le manuel dit « two or four stacked voices ».
- [HEUR] Pour un mono-synth funk (Minimoog-like) : Voices 1, Legato, Glide Const court, Sub on, Drive Asym — combinaison déduite des paramètres ci-dessus, non prescrite par le manuel. [TEST]

### 1.3 Tension (§30.12, p. 747–758) — Clavinet

- [DOC] « Tension is a synthesizer dedicated to the emulation of string instruments, and developed in collaboration with Applied Acoustics Systems […] four types of exciters (two types of hammer, a pick and a bow), an accurate model of a string, a model of the fret/finger interaction, a damper model and different types of soundboards ». Chaîne : Exciter → String (avec Damper, Termination) → Filter → Body ; Pickup prélève sur la corde. « Each section (with the exception of String and the global Keyboard section) can be enabled or disabled independently. Turning off a section reduces CPU usage. »
- [DOC] **Exciter** : *Bow*, *Hammer* (« located below the string and strikes it once before falling away […] found in a piano »), *Hammer (bouncing)* (au-dessus, peut rebondir — « hammered dulcimer »), *Plectrum* (« associated with instruments such as guitars and harpsichords. It can be thought of as an angled object placed under the string that snaps the string into motion »). Paramètres : Bow → *Force*, *Friction* ; Hammer → *Mass*, *Stiffness* ; Plectrum → *Protrusion* (« Lower values result in a "thinner," smaller sound »), *Stiffness* ; universels → *Velocity*, *Position* (0 % = point de terminaison, 50 % = milieu), *Damping*, *Fix. Pos* (« similar to that of a guitar, where the picking position is always basically the same […] On a piano, the exciter position is relative — the hammers normally strike the string at about 1/7th of their length — and so is best modeled with Fix. Pos turned off »), *Vel*/*Key*.
- [DOC] **Damper** : *Mass* (« the string will mute more quickly »), *Stiffness* (« Lower values simulate soft materials such as felt, while higher values model a metal damper »), *Velocity* (appliqué à la relâche — « very high Velocity values can cause the damper to hit the string extremely hard, which can result in a very loud sound on key release »), *Gated* (off = l'étouffoir reste toujours sur la corde), *Position*, *Fix. Pos*, *Damping* (« becomes a bit less predictable as the Damping value goes over 50% […] bounces against the string »).
- [DOC] **String** : *Decay* (+ *< Key*), *Ratio* (rapport decay onset / release), *Inharm*, *Damping* (« Higher values result in more upper partials (less damping) »).
- [DOC] **Vibrato** : LFO sur la hauteur, *Delay*, *Attack*, *Rate*, *Amount*, *< Mod*, *Error*.
- [DOC] **Termination** : « models the interaction between the fret, finger and string » ; *Finger Mass* (+ Vel/Key), *Finger Stiff*, *Fret Stiff*.
- [DOC] **Pickup** : « models the effect of an electromagnetic pickup, similar to the type found in an electric guitar or electric piano […] The only control here is the Position slider […] Lower values generally result in a brighter, thinner sound, while higher values have more fullness and depth ».
- [DOC] **Body** : *Body Type* (« modeled after physical instruments » ; la liste des types n'est pas donnée dans le texte), *Body Size* XS→XL (« as you increase the body size, the frequency of the resonance will become lower »), *Decay*, *Str/Body* (mix direct / corps), *Low Cut*, *High Cut*.
- [DOC] **Filter/Global** : filtre 2e/4e ordre LP/BP/Notch/HP/Formant (Res = voyelles en mode formant), enveloppe ADSR (Vel sur Attack et Sustain), LFO (sine, tri, rect, 2 random ; Rate Hz/sync ; Attack, Delay) ; Keyboard : Octave/Semi/Detune, *Voices*, *Stretch*, *Error*, *Priority* ; **Portamento** : *Time*, *Legato*, *Prop.* ; **Unison** : 2 ou 4 voix, *Detune*, *Delay*.
- [DOC] Avertissement : « it's very easy to find parameter combinations that produce no sound at all. It's also very easy to create extremely loud sounds, so be careful when setting levels! »
- [DOC-extrait, https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/live-instrument-reference/] « Plectrum for guitars, harpsichords or picked bass » (formulation légèrement différente, page en ligne).
- **Clavinet** : le manuel **ne mentionne pas** le Clavinet. [HEUR] La recette « Plectrum + Damper Gated + Pickup Position bas (brillant) + Body petit ou bypass (Str/Body à gauche) » est une déduction du mécanisme du Clavinet (corde frappée par un marteau en caoutchouc, étouffée par fil, captée par micro) ; à valider [TEST]. L'alternative documentée par Ableton est le pack **Clav** (sampled) — voir 1.10.

### 1.4 Drift (§30.3, p. 667–674) — basse synthé mono

- [DOC] « Drift is a versatile synthesizer with intuitive controls and a simple interface that is fully MPE-capable. Based on subtractive synthesis […] while using minimal CPU resources. » Six sections : oscillateurs, filtre dynamique, enveloppes, LFO, Mod, global.
- [DOC] Osc 2 : « Sine, Triangle, Saturated, Saw, and Rectangle » ; *Oct*, *Detune* (demi-tons). Pitch Mod : 2 sources parmi Env 1, Env 2/Cyc, LFO, Key, Velocity, Modwheel, Pressure, Slide (± 100 %) ; « When applying pitch modulation using an LFO that uses the Ratio time mode, it is possible to generate FM tones ».
- [DOC] Mixer : Osc 1, Osc 2, Noise (bruit blanc) ; « As the oscillator gain values are increased from the default -6.0 dB, the first saturation point will become activated, and the second will be triggered when gain values are above 0.0 dB » (deux points de saturation dans le circuit de filtre) ; *R* = Retrigger de phase.
- [DOC] Filtre : passe-bas **Type I (12 dB/oct, DFM-1, « feeds back more of its distortion internally ») / Type II (24 dB/oct, Cytomic MS2, « Sallen-Key design and soft clipping to limit resonance »)** ; *Key* 0–1 ; passe-haut ; deux modulations de fréquence.
- [DOC] **Voice Modes** : *Poly* (« up to 32 voices ») ; *Mono* (« plays one note at a time, but the note is rendered using four voices to produce a unison effect depending on the Mono Thickness value […] A new note will choke the previously played note ») ; *Stereo* (2 voix pannées, *Stereo Spread*) ; *Unison* (4 voix désaccordées, *Unison Strength*). Avec 32 voix : Poly 32 notes, Stereo 16, Unison 8.
- [DOC] **Drift** : « adds slight variation to each voice, affecting different aspects of the voice's sound, such as pitch and filter cutoff. Every voice in Drift has a different randomization for the oscillators and filter frequency ».
- [DOC] **Legato / Glide** : « When the Voice Mode is set to Mono, you can enable the Legato switch so that triggering a new voice will change its pitch without resetting its envelopes. The Glide slider lets you adjust the time overlapping notes take to slide their pitch to the next incoming pitch when notes are played legato. » (Confirmé mot pour mot par l'extrait WebSearch de la page officielle.)
- [DOC] Cibles de modulation : Osc 1 Gain, Osc 1 Shape, Osc 2 Gain, Osc 2 Detune, Noise Gain, LP Frequency, LP Resonance, HP Frequency, LFO Rate, Cyc Env Rate, Main Volume [DOC-extrait page officielle].

### 1.5 Operator (§30.9, p. 700–709) — DX7 E.Piano et basse FM

- [DOC] « Operator is an advanced and flexible synthesizer that combines the concept of "frequency modulation" (FM) with classic subtractive and additive synthesis. It uses four multi-waveform oscillators that can modulate each other's frequencies […] Operator offers eleven predefined algorithms ». « Typically, FM synthesis makes use of pure sine waves […] we designed Operator to produce a variety of other waveforms, including two types of noise. »
- [DOC] Formes utilisateur : dessin de 16/32/64 harmoniques, *Repeat*, édition pair/impair, *Normalize*, export `.ams` (importable dans Simpler/Sampler).
- [DOC] *Coarse*/*Fine* ; **Fixed** (« Fixed Mode would be useful, for example, in creating live drum sounds. Fixed Mode also allows producing very low frequencies down to 0.1 Hz » ; alors *Freq* + *Multi*) ; *Osc < Vel* et *Q* (quantize).
- [DOC] Polyphonie jusqu'à 32 voix ; **« If Voices is set to 1, another effect occurs: Overlapping voices will be played legato, which means that the envelopes will not be retriggered from voice to voice, and only pitch will change. »** Glide « polyphonic glide function » avec *Glide Time* ; **Spread** = « rich stereo chorus by using two voices per note and panning one to the left and one to the right. The two voices are detuned » (CPU-intensif).
- [RELEVÉ-Set] 195 paramètres exposés à l'API (Algorithm, Osc-A…D Coarse/Fine/Level/Wave/Feedb/Fix, enveloppes Ae…De, Pe, Filter, LFO…) — liste dans `instruments-natifs.md`.
- **DX7 E.Piano** : le manuel ne donne **aucune recette** E.Piano. [MÉMOIRE, non vérifié] Le classique « E.PIANO 1 » du DX7 repose sur des porteuses en ratio 1 et 14 (cloche) avec modulateurs à decay court ; à reconstruire et valider [TEST].

### 1.6 Wavetable (§30.13, p. 759–768)

- [DOC] 2 oscillateurs wavetable + sub + 2 filtres modélisés (Cytomic). Oscillateurs « perfectly band-limited » sans modulation ; import WAV/AIFF par glisser-déposer, mode *Raw*. Effets d'oscillateur : **FM** (Tune 50 % = une octave, 100 % = deux), **Classic** (*PW* « the pulse width can be adjusted for all wavetables », *Sync*), **Modern** (*Warp*, *Fold*).
- [DOC] **Sub** : *Tone* (0 % = sinus pur), *Octave* −1/−2.
- [DOC] Filtres : LP/HP/BP/Notch/Morph, 12 ou 24 dB ; circuits **Clean** (= EQ Eight), **OSR** (« state-variable type with resonance limited by a unique hard-clipping diode […] a somewhat rare British monosynth »), **MS2** (« Sallen-Key design and soft clipping […] a famous semi-modular Japanese monosynth », LP/HP), **SMP** (hybride MS2/PRD), **PRD** (« ladder design and has no explicit resonance limiting […] a legacy dual-oscillator monosynth from the United States », LP/HP) ; *Drive* sauf en Clean ; routage Serial / Parallel / (Split).
- [DOC] Global : *Poly/Mono* (« a single voice with legato envelopes (Mono) »), *Poly Voices*, *Glide* (mono seulement), **Unison** : Classic, Shimmer, Noise, Phase Sync, Position spread, Random note ; *Voices*, *Amount*. Hi-Quality off par défaut depuis Live 11.1 (jusqu'à −25 % CPU).

### 1.7 Meld (§30.8, p. 690–699)

- [DOC] « combines two independent macro oscillator engines into one device […] two oscillator-dependent macro knobs ». Glide : *Portamento* ou *Glissando* (par degrés de gamme si scale awareness), *Glide Time* actif en Mono et en Poly ; *Stacked Voices* ; *Mixer Drive*.
- [DOC] **17 types de filtres** dont : SVF 12/24 (macro *L-B-H-N*), MS2 LP/HP, OSR BP, LP Crunch 12dB, LP Switched Res (*Lofi*), Filther, Eq Peak/Notch, **Phaser** (6 étages, *Feedback*, *Spread*), Redux (*Crush*, *Lofi*), **Vowel** (« a formant filter that mimics the characteristics of vowels being pronounced […] Morph macro knob »), **Comb +/−**, Plate Resonator, Membrane Resonator.

### 1.8 Collision (§30.2, p. 658–666)

- [DOC] « simulates the characteristics of mallet percussion instruments. Created in collaboration with Applied Acoustics Systems […] a pair of oscillators called Mallet and Noise, which feed a pair of independent (or linked) stereo resonators ». Avertissement : « very easy to program resonances that are much more sensitive to input than any physical resonator could be […] keep output levels low ».

### 1.9 Sampler / Simpler (§30.10–30.11, p. 718–745)

- [DOC] Sampler : « multisampling instrument […] handle multi-gigabyte instrument libraries […] imports most common library formats » ; définition du multisampling (« capturing the instrument at different pitches as well as different levels of emphasis ») ; Zone tab avec key/velocity/sample-select zones et **Round Robin** ; formats tiers importables : **REX (Standard/Suite), ACID Loops, Soundtrack Loops** ; les imports vont dans *User Library/Sampler/Imports*, parfois convertis en Instrument Racks de plusieurs Sampler. (Le manuel 12 ne cite plus les formats Kontakt/EXS/GigaStudio des versions antérieures — [MÉMOIRE, non vérifié] qu'ils étaient listés en Live 9/10.)
- [DOC] Simpler : trois modes — **Classic** (« optimized for creating "conventional" melodic and harmonic instruments using pitched samples […] complete ADSR envelope and supports looping […] polyphonic by default »), **One-Shot** (« strictly monophonic », *Trigger*/*Gate*, Fade In/Out), **Slicing** (Transient/Beat/Region/Manual ; *Sensitivity*) ; warping conservé depuis les clips.

## 2. Ableton Live 12 — effets audio (manuel, chapitre 28)

Source officielle : https://www.ableton.com/en/manual/live-audio-effect-reference/ (bloquée). Miroir lu : https://raw.githubusercontent.com/djaboxx/iron-static/main/docs/api/ableton/28-live-audio-effect-reference.md (pages PDF 501–633).

### 2.1 Auto Filter (§28.2, p. 503–510) — auto-wah / envelope filter

- [DOC] « Auto Filter is a filtering effect that modifies the incoming sound by applying frequency-selective filters. It offers various filter types and filter circuit types that emulate classic analog filters. The device's LFO modulation and envelope follower help create dynamic, tone-sculpting effects. The envelope follower can respond to the filtered signal itself or to an external sidechain input. » (Version « All-New Auto Filter » de Live 12.2, cf. blog Ableton dans les résultats de recherche.)
- [DOC] **Dix types** : Low-pass, High-pass, Band-pass, Notch (12/24 dB), **Morph** (6/12/24/48 dB, LP→BP→HP), **DJ** (contrôle bipolaire, résonance croissante vers ± 100 %), **Comb** (« flanger-like effects when modulated »), **Resampling**, **Notch + LP**, **Vowel** (« shapes the sound to resemble the human voice by emphasizing certain formants » ; contrôles *Pitch* (décalage des formants en demi-tons) et *Formant* (« position within the vowel spectrum corresponding to the vowels a, e, i, o, and u ») ; *Morph* normalise le gain des formants).
- [DOC] **Envelope follower** : « The Envelope Attack slider […] sets how the envelope responds to rising input signals. Low values result in a fast response to input levels, whereas high values integrate any changes gradually, creating a looser, slower response. Think of it as adding inertia » ; *Envelope Hold* (termine l'attaque avant la relâche) ; « The Envelope Release slider allows you to specify how quickly the envelope responds to falling input levels » ; *Envelope S&H* + *Rate* (quantification rythmique de l'enveloppe) ; « You can control the direction, as well as the extent to which the envelope modulation affects the filter using the Envelope control below the display » (quantité bipolaire).
- [DOC] LFO : stéréo *Phase*/*Spin*, *Phase Offset* (sync), *Quantization Mode* Steps / S&H, formes Sine, Triangle, Saw, Square, Ramp Up/Down, Wander, S&H, *Morph*/*Smooth*.
- [DOC] **Drive** + **circuits** : « SVF, DFM, MS2, and PRD […] only have an effect when used with the Low-pass, High-pass, Band-pass, Notch, and Morph filters » ; SVF = propre (distorsion si Drive > 0) ; DFM = « feeds back more of its distortion » ; MS2 = Sallen-Key (« famous semi-modular Japanese monosynth ») ; PRD = « ladder design […] legacy dual-oscillator monosynth from the United States ». (Le circuit « OSR » et « Clean » de l'ancien Auto Filter n'apparaissent plus dans ce texte.)
- [DOC] Global : *Clip* (soft-clip de sortie pour maîtriser la résonance) ; sidechain externe, **Mono Sidechain** par défaut (menu contextuel).
- [DOC] « Quantize Beat » : l'extrait WebSearch de la page officielle mentionne encore « The Quantize Beat control applies quantized modulation to the filter frequency » ; dans le miroir 12.3, la fonction équivalente est l'*Envelope S&H* / *LFO Quantization*. Différence de version à vérifier dans Live 12.4 [TEST].
- [HEUR] Auto-wah funk = filtre BP ou LP 12 dB, circuit MS2/PRD, Envelope positif, Attack bas, Release moyen ; à régler à l'oreille [TEST].

### 2.2 Phaser-Flanger (§28.29, p. 586–589)

- [DOC] Trois modes : **Phaser** (« creates wandering notch filters by feeding a phase-shifted version of the input signal back into the input […] modulated all-pass filters ») ; **Flanger** (« continuously changing comb filter effect by adding a time-modulated delayed signal with feedback ») ; **Doubler** (« the effect of doubled tracks […] by adding time-modulated delayed signals »).
- [DOC] Phaser : *Notches* (nombre d'all-pass), *Center*, *Spread* (Q des all-pass), *Blend* (routage de la modulation entre Center et Spread). Flanger/Doubler : *Time* (Doubler = modulation bipolaire).
- [DOC] LFO principal : Hz ou sync ; formes Sine, Triangle (défaut), Saw Up/Down, Rectangle, Random, Random S&H, **Triangle Analog** (« a low-pass filtered rectangular waveform »), Triangle 8, Triangle 16 ; *Phase*/*Spin* ; *Duty Cycle* ; LFO2 (triangle, *Mix*, *Freq/Rate*).
- [DOC] Global : *Amount*, *Feedback* (+ inversion Ø « hollow »), *Output*, *Warmth* (« adds slight distortion and filtering »), *Dry/Wet* ; **Envelope follower** (*Env Fol*, *Envelope Amount* bipolaire, *Attack*, *Release*) ; **Safe Bass** = passe-haut 5 Hz–3 kHz sur l'effet (« can make mixing certain bass-heavy material easier »). Avertissement sur les feedbacks élevés.

### 2.3 Chorus-Ensemble (§28.8, p. 529–530)

- [DOC] « classic two-delay line chorus with an optional third delay line mode ». **Classic** (« two time-modulated delayed signals ») ; **Ensemble** (« inspired by a thick three-delay line chorus pedal used in the '70s […] three delayed signals with evenly split modulation phase offsets ») ; **Vibrato** (« stronger modulation than a chorus to create pitch variation […] sine to a triangle […] "police siren" »). *High-Pass* 20 Hz–2 kHz ; *Width* 0–200 % (mid/side ; remplacé par *Offset*/*Shape* en Vibrato) ; *Rate*, *Amount*, *Feedback* (+ Ø), *Output*, *Warmth*, *Dry/Wet* (désactivé en Vibrato). Tip : « Use Ensemble mode at a rate between 1 Hz and 1.8 Hz and 100% Amount on dry guitars ».
- [HEUR] Pour le chorus stéréo d'un Rhodes Suitcase / Dimension D : mode Ensemble, Rate ≈ 0,5–1 Hz, Width 100 %. [TEST]

### 2.4 Auto Pan-Tremolo (§28.3, p. 511–514) — tremolo / rotary

- [DOC] Nouveau device (Live 12.3) : « two modes available: Panning and Tremolo. In Panning mode, two LFOs modulate the placement […] between the left and right channels. In Tremolo mode, a single LFO modulates the signal's amplitude ». Formes : Sine, Triangle, Shark Tooth, Saw Up, Saw Down, Square, Random, Wander, S&H ; modes de temps Rate/Time (100 ms–200 s)/Synced/Dotted/Triplet/16th ; *Phase*/*Spin* (180° = opposition) ; *Phase Offset* ; *Invert* (« ramp-shaped waveforms […] Invert produces a ducking effect, while disabling it creates a gating effect »).
- [DOC] **Tremolo** : options **Harmonic** (« the high and low frequencies are modulated in an alternating pattern […] A fixed crossover of 600 Hz splits the signal into two frequency bands ») et **Vintage** (« shaped by a non-linear curve, which adds warmth and grit ») ; *Amount*, *Rate*, *Shape* (« At its maximum and minimum values, the Shape control can create ramps for gating effects (with Invert off) and pumping effects (with Invert on) »).
- [DOC] Le tremolo « stéréo » du Rhodes Suitcase se fait donc en mode **Panning** (alternance G/D), le tremolo Wurlitzer (amplitude mono) en mode **Tremolo** ; le mode *Harmonic* correspond au tremolo harmonique d'ampli à deux bandes. Le manuel ne mentionne pas « rotary » ; [HEUR] une cabine Leslie s'approche par Panning + Vibrato (Chorus-Ensemble) + Cabinet, sans device dédié dans Live [TEST].

### 2.5 Pedal (§28.28, p. 583–585)

- [DOC] « Pedal is a guitar distortion effect […] can also be used […] on vocals, synths or drums ». *Gain* (« 0% does not mean zero distortion »), *Output* ; types **Overdrive « warm and smooth », Distortion « tight and aggressive », Fuzz « unstable, with a "broken amp" sound »** ; EQ 3 bandes **adaptative** : *Bass* peak 100 Hz, *Mid* commutable 500 Hz / 1 kHz / 2 kHz, *Treble* shelf 3,3 kHz ; **Sub** = low shelf < 250 Hz ; *Dry/Wet* ; Hi-Quality. Tip « Sub Warmer » : « choose the OD pedal, turn on the Sub switch and turn up the Bass control ».

### 2.6 Amp / Cabinet (§28.1, 28.6)

- [DOC] Amp : « emulates the sound and character of seven classic guitar amplifiers. Developed in collaboration with Softube » ; « While Amp and Cabinet sound great with guitars, you can get very interesting results by feeding them with drums, synthesizers or other sound sources. For example, try using Amp with Operator or Analog ». Cabinet : « five classic guitar cabinets », *Speaker* (ex. 4x12), *Microphone* Near On-Axis / Near Off-Axis / Far, dynamique/condensateur. La liste des sept amplis n'est pas dans le miroir (page 502 manquante).

### 2.7 Roar (§28.33, p. 596–603)

- [DOC] « saturation and coloration device with up to three processing stages […] Roar's feedback controls and built-in compressor ». Input : *Drive*, *Tone Amount*/*Frequency* (shelf), *Color Compensation* (« saturating drums without affecting low end frequency impact »). Routages : Single, Serial, Parallel, Multi Band (2 crossovers), Mid Side, Feedback, Delay. **Douze shapers** : Soft Sine, Digital Clip, Bit Crusher, Diode Clipper, Tube Preamp, Half/Full Wave Rectifier (« Full Wave Rectifier shifts the signal up by an octave »), Polynomial, Fractal, Tri Fold, Noise Injection, Shards ; *Shaper Bias* ; neuf filtres LP/BP/HP/Notch/Peak/Morph/Comb/Resampling/Dispersion, *Pre*. Feedback : modes Time/Synced/Triplet/Dotted/Note, avec compresseur dans la boucle ; Global : *Compression Amount*, *Compressor Sidechain HP Filter*, *Output Gain* (suivi d'un hard clip), *Dry/Wet*.

### 2.8 Vocoder (§28.42, p. 632–634) — talkbox

- [DOC] « combines the frequency information of one audio signal (called the carrier) with the amplitude contour of another audio signal (called the modulator) […] Live's Vocoder should be inserted on the track that contains the audio material you plan to use as your modulator ». **Carrier** : *Noise* (X-Y downsampling/densité), *External* (« the option you'll want for classic "robot voice" applications »), *Modulator* (auto-vocoding), *Pitch Tracking* (oscillateur mono sawtooth ou 3 pulses, *High/Low*, *Pitch*).
- [DOC] *Enhance* (« normalizing the spectrum and dynamics of the carrier »), *Unvoiced* + *Sens.* + Fast/Slow, **Bands** (« Using more bands results in a more accurate analysis […] but requires more CPU » — nombre max non indiqué dans le texte), *Range*, *BW* (« 100% is the most accurate »), **Precise/Retro** (« In Retro mode, bands become narrower and louder at higher frequencies »), *Gate*, *Level*, **Depth** (« At 0%, the modulator's envelope is discarded. At 200%, only high amplitude peaks will be used. 100% results in "classic" vocoding »), *Attack/Release*, Mono/Stereo/L/R, **Formant** (« shifted up or down […] small Formant changes can alter the apparent gender »), *Dry/Wet*.
- [HEUR] Talkbox funk (Zapp/Roger, Daft Punk) = Vocoder carrier External depuis une piste de synthé (scie, Analog ou Serum), peu de bandes (8–12) + Retro + Formant léger ; Ableton n'a pas de talkbox dédié. Auto Filter Vowel (2.1) et Meld Vowel (1.7) sont les alternatives sans voix. [TEST]

### 2.9 Multiband Dynamics (§28.26, p. 577–581) et le preset « OTT »

- [DOC] « Designed primarily as a mastering processor, Multiband Dynamics allows for upward and downward compression and expansion of up to three independent frequency bands, with adjustable crossover points and envelope controls for each band. Each frequency range has both an upper and lower threshold ». Théorie des quatre traitements (downward/upward compression, downward/upward expansion). Interface : blocs *Above*/*Below* par bande (« lowering the volume in the block above the Above threshold applies downward compression […] raising the volume in the block below the Below threshold applies upward compression »), boutons T/B/A, *Soft Knee*, **RMS/Peak**, *Output*, **Time** (« scales the durations of all of the Attack and Release controls ») et **Amount** (« adjusts the intensity of the compression or expansion applied to all bands. At 0%, each compressor/expander has an effective ratio of 1 »). Tips : compression multibande simple, de-essing (> 5 kHz), « Uncompression » par expansion ascendante.
- [HEUR] « OTT » (« Over The Top ») est le nom d'un preset d'usine de Multiband Dynamics (compression ascendante + descendante forte sur 3 bandes) : cité par un fil du forum Ableton dans les résultats de recherche, **non confirmé par le manuel**. Le « Amount » joue alors le rôle du « Depth » de l'OTT Xfer [MÉMOIRE, non vérifié].

### 2.10 Compressor (§28.9, p. 531–535)

- [DOC] *Threshold*, *Ratio*, *Knee* (0 dB = hard ; ex. « with a 10 dB knee and a -20 dB threshold, subtle compression will begin at -30 dB »), *Attack*, *Release*, *Auto Release*, *Makeup*, *Dry/Wet*, vues Collapsed/Transfer Curve/Activity. **Lookahead** : « three different Lookahead times: zero ms, one ms and ten ms ». **Modes** : *Peak* (« more aggressive and precise […] limiting tasks »), *RMS* (« less sensitive to very short peaks […] closer to how people actually perceive loudness »), *Expand* (« a ratio of 1 to 2 means that for every 1 dB of input above the threshold level, the output level will increase by 2 dB »). **Lin/Log** : « In logarithmic (Log) mode, sharply compressed peaks will have a faster release time ». Sidechain externe + EQ.
- [DOC] Le manuel sur le pompage : « Short release times can cause "pumping" as the compressor tries to figure out whether to compress or not; while generally considered an undesirable effect, some engineers use it on full drum kits to give unusual "sucking" effects ».

### 2.11 Glue Compressor (§28.21, p. 561–563)

- [DOC] « analog-modeled compressor created in collaboration with Cytomic, and is based on the classic bus compressor from a famous 80's mixing console ». Pas de knee réglable (« the knee becomes more sharp as the ratio increases ») ; *Attack* en ms, *Release* en s, *Auto* (« two times - a slow one as a base compression value, and a fast one to react to transients ») ; **Range** (« Values between about -60 and -70 dB emulate the original hardware, while values between -40 and -15 dB can be useful as an alternative to the Dry/Wet ») ; *Makeup* ; **Soft clip** (« fixed waveshaper […] maximum output level is -.5 dB […] not a transparent limiter, and will distort your signal ») ; Oversampling ; sidechain.

### 2.12 Saturator (§28.34, p. 605–608)

- [DOC] « waveshaping effect that can add that missing dirt, punch or warmth ». Huit courbes : **Analog Clip, Soft Sine, Bass Shaper, Medium Curve, Hard Curve, Sinoid Fold, Digital Clip, Waveshaper**. « Sinoid Fold is a wavefolding type » ; **Bass Shaper** « ideal for processing low-end signals like 808 kick drums and synth basslines » avec *Bass Shaper Threshold* 0 à −50 dB ; *Post Clip Mode* Soft/Hard ; **Color** (deux filtres, « remove bass frequencies before the shaper, so that only mid/high frequencies are saturated ») avec *Amt Lo*, *Amt Hi*, *Frequency*, *Width* ; *Drive*, *Output*, *Dry/Wet* ; Waveshaper : *Drive, Curve* (3e harmonique), *Depth, Linear, Damp, Period* ; menu : Hi-Quality, Pre-DC Filter. (L'extrait WebSearch qui parle de « three saturation stages […] built-in compressor » décrit en fait Roar, pas Saturator.)

### 2.13 Erosion, Redux, Vinyl Distortion, Drum Buss, Shifter, Spectral Resonator

- [DOC] **Erosion** (§28.17) : « degrades the input signal by modulating a short delay with filtered noise or a sine wave. This adds noisy artifacts or aliasing/downsampling-like distortions that sound very "digital" » ; modes Noise / Wide Noise (stéréo) / Sine ; *Frequency*, *Width*, *Amount*.
- [DOC] **Redux** (§28.30) : downsampling (*Rate*, *Jitter*, filtres *Pre*/*Post* avec *Post-Filter Octave*) et bit reduction (*Bits*, *Shape* — « Higher values produce a finer resolution for smaller amplitudes », *DC Shift*), *Dry/Wet*.
- [DOC] **Vinyl Distortion** (§28.41) : « emulates some of the typical distortions that occur on vinyl records during playback » ; *Tracing Model* = harmoniques paires (*Drive*, *Freq*, Q) ; *Pinch* = harmoniques impaires à 180° ; modes **Soft (« dub plate ») / Hard (« standard vinyl record »)** ; *Crackle* (*Density*, *Volume*).
- [DOC] **Drum Buss** (§28.12) : « analog-style drum processor » ; *Trim*, *Comp* (fixe : « fast attack, medium release and moderate ratio »), distorsion Soft/Medium/Hard, *Drive*, *Crunch*, *Damp*, **Transients** (« emphasizes or de-emphasizes the transients of frequencies above 100 Hz »), *Boom*/*Freq*/*Force To Note*, *Decay*.
- [DOC] **Shifter** (§28.35) : « multi-purpose pitch and frequency-shifting audio effect that can be used to add ring modulation ». Modes Pitch / Freq / Ring ; *Coarse*, *Fine*, *Spread* + *Wide*, *Window*, *Tone*, *Delay* + *Feedback* ; LFO (Sine, Triangle, Triangle Analog/8/16, Saw, Rectangle, Random, Random S&H ; Phase/Spin/Width) ; envelope follower (*Amount* en demi-tons ou Hz). Tips : « In Ring mode, frequencies below the audible range (about 20 Hz or slightly lower) create a tremolo effect » ; « minimally adjust (no more than 2 Hz or so) the Fine knob in Freq mode » pour du phasing.
- [DOC] **Spectral Resonator** (§28.36) : « transforms an audio signal by applying tuned resonances to its spectrum […] Choose which frequencies are affected through an internal resonator or via an external MIDI sidechain. Similar to a vocoder […] polyphonically with up to 16 voices » ; modes Internal / **MIDI** (External Source, Mono/Poly, Polyphony 2–16, MIDI Gate, Glide, PB 0–24) ; *Freq*/*Transp.* (± 48), *Stretch* (« At 100%, only odd harmonics are produced »), *Shift* (± 48), *Decay*, *HF/LF Damp*, *Harmonics*, *Quantize* (gamme), *Unison* + *Uni. Amt*, *Input Send* avec limiteur, *Dry/Wet*.

### 3.1 Moteurs d'oscillateur

- [DOC-extrait] « Serum 2 offers five primary oscillator modes: Wavetable, Multisample, Sample, Granular, and Spectral. » Sample : « sample playback with FM/PD/Distortion types, looping with snap loop detection, flexible loop modulation, a Rate control for tape stop effects, and sample slicing ». Spectral : « realtime resynthesis of samples at the harmonic level, with transient detection processing that enables you to shape time and frequencies ». Multisample : « You can create or import your own multisample recordings using the open standard and human-readable sfz file format. »
- [MESURE-tiers] Structure : slots Oscillator0..2 portent les cinq moteurs (`kOsc_WT, kOsc_MultiSample, kOsc_Sample, kOsc_Granular, kOsc_Spectral`), Oscillator3 = **NoiseOsc**, plus un Sub. Répartition dans les 626 presets d'usine : Wavetable 528, Multisample 155, Sample 72, Granular 56, Spectral 43 (un preset peut en cumuler).
- [MESURE-tiers] **Multisample** : le preset embarque le texte SFZ complet (`embedded_sfz`, « // SFZ Generated by libSFZ ») et une carte de fichiers **FLAC** (4 à 234 fichiers, 44,1–88,2 kHz, mono ou stéréo) ; paramètres `kParamTimbreShift` (−18…64), `kParamVelTrack` (+ override), enveloppe propre AHDSR (`EnvOverride`), warp menus (FM/PD/RM/AM depuis Sub/Osc/Noise, distorsions Tube/TapeSat/StompBox/Diode/SoftClip…), `RandomPhase`. Bibliothèque d'usine `Multisamples/Factory/` avec dossiers **Keys** (« Baby Grand Piano.sfz », « Elec.Piano Suitcase.sfz », « RDE88_V Samples »), Synth (« SuperJX 4 Chorus Pad », « Arp Solina »), Strings, Choir, Mallet (Balafon, Marimba), Plucked, Winds, Drums.
- [MESURE-tiers] **Sample** : `baseNote`, `trimHead/Tail`, warp menus ; échantillons d'usine dont un dossier **Bass** (« Alu Slap », « Clean 808 », « JB Fingerstyle », « PB Fingerstyle », « Round Comforting », « Samo Op »).
- [MESURE-tiers] **Spectral** : `kParamFreqLo/Hi`, `SpecFltShift`, `SpecFltWetDry` ; warps spectraux `kSpectralComb, kGate, kAddharmonics, kAddsubharmonics, kSmear, kDetune, kSpread, kSpectralPitchShift, kSpectralShift, kSpectralPhaseTwist, kMirror, kShepardFilter, kVocode_OSC/NOISE, kMask_OSC/NOISE, kPeakOctaveUp/Down`. Un fichier tiers précise que « Spectral is a MODE, not a separate content kind » (même dossier `Samples/`) [HEUR, https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rich6feet/serum/main/docs/serum2-content-setup.md].
- [MESURE-tiers] **Noise** : `kParamColor`, `kParamNoiseType` = White | Pink | Brown | Geiger, `kParamOneShot`, phase initiale/aléatoire.
- [DOC-local] Spectral est « CPU intensive » (avertissement Xfer cité dans `moteurs-synthese.md`) ; granulaire p. 98–103 du manuel.
- [HEUR] Rhodes échantillonné dans Serum 2 : soit le multisample d'usine « Elec.Piano Suitcase », soit un SFZ maison (les points de boucle embarqués dans les WAV **ne sont pas importés** — extrait d'un fil du forum Xfer [DOC-extrait, https://xferrecords.com/forums/general/sfz-file-import-issue]). Resynthèse d'un Rhodes : mode Spectral sur un échantillon, puis warps « kSmear / kSpectralComb » [TEST].

## 6. Goodhertz Vulf Compressor — ce que dit la documentation, et l'approche native

Pages bloquées : https://goodhertz.com/vulf-comp/, https://goodhertz.com/vulf-comp/manual/, https://manuals.goodhertz.com/3.13/vulf-comp/, miroir S3 (503).

- [DOC-extrait, texte Goodhertz repris intégralement dans https://raw.githubusercontent.com/existential-engineering/catalog/main/data/software/goodhertz-vulf-compressor.yaml] « Sometime around the turn of the century, an unknown Japanese DSP engineer engineered a radically weird compression algorithm for the Boss SP-303 Dr. Sample Sampler. Years passed, and this "Vinyl Sim" compression languished in obscurity, a dark art known only to practitioners of instrumental beatmaking, producers like J Dilla and Madlib. But then Vulfpeck's Jack Stratton read about the compression algorithm on a forum and enlisted two friends, Devin Kerr and Rob Stenson (co-founders of Goodhertz), to bring that algorithm to life in a digital form. But Devin did more than just bring it to life — he expanded the algorithm, fine-tuned the sound, perfected its oddity. » Même texte confirmé par l'extrait WebSearch de goodhertz.com.
- [HEUR, même catalogue, spécifications v3.13] Contrôles principaux : **Input Gain, Compression Amount, Wow/Flutter Amount, Lo-Fi Amount, Output Gain, Master Mix, Master On/Off** ; avancés : **Attack Time Constant, Release Time Constant, Stereo Link** ; section Wow & Flutter : Mix, Phase, **Speed 33.3 / 45 / 78 RPM** ; section Lo-Fi : **Noise Gain, Crunch, Type (Analog, 1990s Digital, 1980s Digital)** ; Sidechain : Listen, External, **Tilt** ; Digital Ref Level, HQ Mode (≈ 50 ms de latence, 2–4× CPU), Pre/Post-process Gain ; VCME = édition mastering.
- [MESURE-tiers, zone CSI générée depuis le VST3 : https://raw.githubusercontent.com/FunkybotsEvilTwin/CSI_Install/main/CSI/Surfaces/SSL_Nucleus_2/FXZones/AutoGeneratedFXZones/VST3__Ghz_Vulf_Compressor_3__Goodhertz_.zon] Libellés exposés : **In, Comp, Wow, Lo-Fi, Out, Mix, Noise, Master** (paramètres 0–6, 13, 22). Cohérent avec le catalogue.
- [DOC-extrait goodhertz manual via WebSearch] « smaller Comp Release Time Constants cause Vulf Compressor to release more quickly, often sounding louder and causing more audible pumping or even distortion ».
- **« DX style », « Pumping », « Reel », « Zap »** : **aucun de ces termes n'apparaît** dans le texte Goodhertz lu, dans les spécifications, ni dans les noms de paramètres VST3. Ils ne sont pas des contrôles du Vulf Compressor 3 ; il peut s'agir de noms de presets (par ex. du pack « Presets from the Golden Age of Samplers », https://goodhertz.com/tonal/golden-age-of-samplers/, non lu) ou d'un souvenir inexact. **[NON VÉRIFIÉ]** — à ne pas écrire dans le skill comme des paramètres.
- **Approche avec les natifs Live** (déductions à partir des définitions du manuel Live, toutes [HEUR]/[TEST]) :
  1. *Comp* (amount, algorithme SP-303) → **Compressor** en mode *Peak*, ratio élevé, *Release* court (le manuel : « Short release times can cause "pumping" ») ou *Log* pour un relâchement dépendant du niveau ; **Drum Buss** *Comp* fixe + *Crunch* comme raccourci.
  2. *Lo-Fi* Type « 1980s / 1990s Digital » + *Crunch* → **Redux** (*Rate* + *Bits* + *Shape* + *DC Shift*) ; Type « Analog » → **Saturator** *Analog Clip* ou *Bass Shaper*, ou **Vinyl Distortion** (Tracing/Pinch) ; *Noise Gain* → **Vinyl Distortion** *Crackle* ou **Erosion** Noise.
  3. *Wow/Flutter* (33/45/78 RPM) → aucun device natif de wow ; approximation par **Chorus-Ensemble** mode *Vibrato* à Rate très bas (0,5–1,3 Hz ≈ 33–78 tr/min) et faible Amount, ou **Shifter** mode *Freq* avec LFO lent [TEST].
  4. *Tilt* de sidechain → EQ de sidechain du **Compressor** / **Glue** (passe-haut) ; *Mix* → *Dry/Wet* du Compressor ou *Range* du Glue.
