# Bio

 Cory Wong is a grammy nominated artist who is renowned the world over for laying down some of the finest grooves in rock, jazz, and funk. He is a heavy hitter but with a precise clean tone that commands you to get up and dance – his playing is so infectious it has been classified as dangerous in the wrong hands. You can now grab a slice of that hot buttered tone with the new Wampler Cory Wong Compressor. Cory probably first hit our radar when we saw his legendary jams with Vulfpeck and since then has conquered the world with his dynamic rhythmic playing and pure positivity.

 The Wampler EGO has been a big part of Cory Wong’s signature tone for a long time, providing the light touch required to subtly drive the best from his tone, so it was a natural starting point for this pedal. But he needed some very specific features. He needed it to have an XLR out so he could go straight into a mixing desk or House PA for those signature bright and clean tones. Cory also wanted to add a boost that allowed him to choose either a flat volume boost or a rich, saturated boost that could push his tone further. The Wampler Cory Wong Compressor delivers all of this.
