# Here Are The Full Credits For Thundercat’s Drunk Album

## The album features contributions from Kendrick Lamar, Wiz Khalifa, Michael McDonald, Kenny Loggins, Flying Lotus, Kamasi Washington, and more. 

 By Ben Dandridge-Lemco 

 February 24, 2017

 Share -->

 Tweet -->

 On Thursday night, Thundercat released his third studio album, Drunk . The 23-track record features Michael McDonald and Kenny Loggins on "Show You the Way," vocals from Wiz Khalifa on "Drink Dat, and a contribution from past collaborator Kendrick Lamar on "Walk On By." Additional production and instrumentation on the album comes from Flying Lotus, Kamasi Washington, and more. See the full credits for the album below.
