# Signal To Noise, Episode 182: Chris Rabold, FOH Engineer, Bruno Mars & Silk Sonic

 November 30, 2022 

 PSW Staff 

 Discusses his recent work with both artists and how his approach is different for each show as well as listening to music on different mediums and translating it to the live realm, and more. 

Noted front of house engineer Chris Rabold returns to the show in Episode 182 to talk about his recent work with Bruno Mars and Silk Sonic over the past year and how his approach is different for each show. He and the hosts also discuss listening to music on different mediums and translating it to the live experience — and vice versa — specifically with cell phones. Chris also details how he continues to improve and evolve as an engineer, what the Pooch & Rabold YouTube series experience was like, and more.
