#### A MESSAGE FROM JACK 
 Over the past decade, I have dedicated myself to developing what is now known as the 'Vulf Sound.' It’s a joy for me to teach it in this Masterclass. Mixing can be a thrilling and creative process. Rather than obsess, we compress. We'll dissect sessions like ‘Dean Town’ and ‘Back Pocket,’ examine the delicate ratios between instruments that ensure your mix cracks , and understand how creative mixing can be the difference between a ‘bop’ and a ‘flop.’ My aim is for you to not just acquire technical skills but to absorb the ethos of creative freedom and innovation that defines the ‘Vulf Sound.’ Let's begin!
