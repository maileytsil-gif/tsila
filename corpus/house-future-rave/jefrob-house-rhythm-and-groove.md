# Rhythm & Groove Patterns

## Deep House

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Shuffle/swing feel on hi-hats (human groove)

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Standard four-on-the-floor:
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4
```
- Warm, round kick — NOT clicky or aggressive
- Fundamental at 50–60 Hz, minimal transient above 3 kHz
- Provides steady anchor for groove

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Swung 16ths with velocity variation:
|x . x x | . x x . | x x . x | x . x x |
(velocity: 90,50,70,60 — accents on off-beats)

Simpler variant (shuffled 8ths):
|x . . x | . x . x | . x . x | . x . x |
  1   &     2   &     3   &     4   &
```
- Swing amount: 57–62% (medium shuffle)
- Open hat on offbeat accents (not every offbeat)
- Velocity variation essential — mechanical hats sound wrong

#### Clap/Snare
```
Standard backbeat:
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | X . . . |
            2                   4
```
- Soft, wide clap (reverb tail)
- NOT snappy or aggressive — warm and cushioned
- Sits back in the mix

#### Percussion (Congas/Shakers)
```
Shaker (constant 16ths):
|x x x x | x x x x | x x x x | x x x x |
(velocity variation: 80,50,70,40)

Conga pattern:
|. . x . | . . . x | . x . . | . . x . |
      &           a     e           &
```
- Light touch — groove support, not lead
- Pan slightly off-center for width
- Ghost notes at very low velocity for depth

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 57–62% (medium, groovy)
- **Quantize**: Not fully quantized — slight human drift (3–8ms)
- **Overall feel**: Warm, laid-back, comfortable
- **Groove pocket**: Slightly behind the beat — never rushing

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Kick = steady four-on-the-floor (anchor)
- Hats = swung 16ths (primary groove)
- Shaker = constant 16ths (groove glue)
- Congas/percussion = syncopated against both
- Bass = locked to kick with passing notes

## Tech House

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Tight, driving groove — less swing than deep house

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Four-on-the-floor with accent:
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4

Variant with ghost kick:
|X . . . | X . x . | X . . . | X . x . |
1         2   &     3         4   &
```
- Punchy, present kick (clicky at 3–5 kHz)
- Sub at 50–70 Hz, body at 100 Hz
- Drives the track forward

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Standard 16th notes:
|x x x x | x x x x | x x x x | x x x x |

With open hat on offbeats:
|x x x X | x x x X | x x x X | x x x X |
        &         &         &         &
(X = open hat)
```
- Tighter swing than deep house (52–55%)
- Open hat on offbeat is signature
- Mechanical but with subtle velocity humanization

#### Clap/Snare
```
Standard:
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | X . . . |
            2                   4

With ghost snares:
|. . . . | X . . x | . . . . | X . . x |
            2     a             4     a
```
- Short, snappy clap (less reverb than deep house)
- Ghost snares for groove detail
- Tight and punchy

#### Percussion Layers
```
Ride cymbal:
|. x . x | . x . x | . x . x | . x . x |
    e   a     e   a     e   a     e   a

Conga:
|. . x . | . x . . | . . x . | x . . . |
      &     e           &   1

Rimshot:
|. . . x | . . . . | . . . x | . . . . |
        a                 a
```
- Multiple percussion layers (5+ channels)
- Each with distinct rhythmic identity
- Panned for width

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 52–55% (tight, driving)
- **Quantize**: Tighter than deep house — more mechanical
- **Overall feel**: Driving, relentless, hypnotic
- **Groove pocket**: On the beat — forward momentum

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Kick = strict four-on-the-floor (driving)
- Hats = 16th grid (mechanical pulse)
- Open hat = offbeat accent (groove definition)
- Congas/rimshot = syncopated (human element)
- Vocal chops = rhythmic accents on unexpected positions

## Progressive House

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Straight feel (less swing than deep/soulful house)

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Standard four-on-the-floor:
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4
```
- Clean, tight kick with sub extension
- Not too clicky, not too boomy — balanced
- Consistent, unwavering throughout

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Straight 16ths (mechanical):
|x x x x | x x x x | x x x x | x x x x |

Evolving pattern (adds open hats over time):
Bar 1:  |x x x x | x x x x | x x x x | x x x x |
Bar 33: |x x x X | x x x x | x x x X | x x x x |
Bar 65: |x x x X | x x x X | x x x X | x x x X |
```
- Minimal swing (50–52%)
- Elements EVOLVE — new patterns add gradually
- Subtle rides and cymbals layer in over time

#### Clap/Snare
```
Standard:
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | X . . . |
            2                   4
```
- Layered clap (multiple samples for texture)
- Medium reverb tail (not too dry, not too wet)
- May be filtered/processed differently per section

#### Percussion
```
Shaker (enters mid-track):
|x x x x | x x x x | x x x x | x x x x |

Ride (very subtle):
|. . . x | . . . x | . . . x | . . . x |
        a         a         a         a
```
- Minimal percussion — focus is on melodic evolution
- Elements enter one at a time over 16–32 bars
- Never cluttered — space is the production

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 50–52% (nearly straight)
- **Quantize**: Grid-locked — precision is the aesthetic
- **Overall feel**: Mechanical but emotional through arrangement
- **Groove pocket**: On the beat — steady, metronomic

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Minimal polyrhythm — focus is on layering over time
- Kick = steady constant
- Hats = evolving complexity over 64+ bars
- Percussion = sparse accents, not complex patterns
- Groove grows through addition, not syncopation

## Funky House

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Strong disco-influenced shuffle/swing

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Four-on-the-floor (disco weight):
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4
```
- Punchy, present kick (disco weight)
- More body/mid than deep house kick
- Drives the dance floor

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Open hat on every offbeat (disco signature):
|x . X . | x . X . | x . X . | x . X . |
1   &   2   &   3   &   4   &
(x = closed, X = open)
```
- THE signature: open hat on EVERY offbeat
- Creates the "disco house" rhythmic feel
- Consistent, driving offbeat pulse

#### Clap/Snare
```
Snare on 2 and 4:
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | X . . . |
            2                   4
```
- Snappy snare with slight reverb
- More present than deep house (funkier)
- May be layered with clap for width

#### Bass Pattern (Rhythmic — Part of Groove)
```
Funky octave bass:
|X . . x | . X . . | x . . X | . . x . |
1       a     2     e       3         a

Syncopated disco bass:
|X . . . | x . X . | . . x . | X . . x |
1         e   2           e   3       a
```
- Bass IS part of the groove (not just harmony)
- Octave jumps, syncopation, chromatic runs
- Percussive attack on bass notes

#### Percussion
```
Tambourine (constant 16ths):
|x x x x | x x x x | x x x x | x x x x |

Congas:
|. x . . | x . . x | . . x . | x . . . |
  e       1       a       &   1
```
- Tambourine on 16ths (disco essential)
- Congas for Latin/funk flavor
- Handclaps on anticipations

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 55–60% (disco groove)
- **Quantize**: Humanized — band feel
- **Overall feel**: Energetic, bouncy, danceable
- **Groove pocket**: On or slightly ahead — uplifting energy

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Kick = four-on-the-floor (foundation)
- Open hat = constant offbeat (disco identity)
- Bass = syncopated (primary groove element)
- Tambourine = 16th constant (energy)
- Congas = counter-rhythm (Latin flavor)

## Afro House

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Complex polyrhythmic layering implies multiple time signatures

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Four-on-the-floor:
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4
```
- Deep, rounded kick
- Sub-heavy (40–60 Hz fundamental)
- Steady anchor for complex percussion above

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
16ths with velocity variation:
|x x x x | x x x x | x x x x | x x x x |
(v: 80,40,70,30,80,40,70,30...)

With open hat accents:
|x x x X | x x x x | x x x X | x x x x |
        &                   &
```

#### Djembe/Conga Pattern (Signature)
```
Layer 1 — Djembe (tone + slap):
|T . S . | . T . . | S . . T | . . S . |
(T=tone, S=slap)

Layer 2 — Conga:
|. x . . | x . . x | . . x . | . x . . |
  e       1       a       &     e

Layer 3 — Shaker:
|x x x x | x x x x | x x x x | x x x x |
(constant 16ths, light)
```
- 5–8 percussion layers interlock
- Each layer syncopated differently
- Ghost notes and flams on every layer

#### Talking Drum / Misc Percussion
```
|. . . x | . x . . | . . x . | x . . . |
        a   e           &   1
```
- Sparse accents, deep tones
- Pan different positions for immersion
- Appear/disappear through arrangement

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 52–57% (less swing than deep house — tighter for polyrhythm)
- **Quantize**: Tight on kick/hat, looser on hand percussion
- **Overall feel**: Tribal, driving, hypnotic
- **Groove pocket**: On the beat — forward energy

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Kick = four-on-the-floor (constant anchor)
- Hi-hat = 16th grid (reference pulse)
- Djembe = 3/4 over 4/4 (implied polyrhythm)
- Conga = syncopated against djembe (interlocking)
- Shaker = constant (glue between all layers)
- Result: 5+ layers creating dense polyrhythmic web

## Jackin House

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Chicago shuffle — swung, raw, jacking

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Four-on-the-floor (steady):
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4
```
- 909-style kick: punchy, round
- Drives the "jacking" body movement
- No variation — steady, relentless

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Swung 16ths with off-beat accent:
|x . x X | x . x X | x . x X | x . x X |
1   e & a   ...
(X = open hat on every &)
```
- Chicago shuffle feel (55–60% swing)
- Open hat on offbeats (Chicago signature)
- Tight closed hats between

#### Clap
```
On 2 and 4 (dry):
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | X . . . |
            2                   4
```
- Dry, short clap (minimal reverb)
- No-nonsense — functional

#### Bass Pattern
```
Syncopated jack-bass:
|X . . x | . . X . | . x . . | X . . x |
1       a       &     e       1       a
```
- Short, punchy bass hits
- Syncopated against the kick
- Creates the "jacking" motion

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 55–60% (Chicago shuffle)
- **Quantize**: Slightly loose — human feel
- **Overall feel**: Raw, physical, dance-inducing
- **Groove pocket**: On the beat with forward push

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Kick = steady four-on-the-floor
- Hats = swung 16ths (groove definition)
- Open hat = offbeat accent
- Bass = syncopated against kick (creates jack motion)
- Vocal samples = rhythmic accents

## Soulful House

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Organic, band-like groove feel

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Four-on-the-floor (warm):
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |

With ghost kick:
|X . . . | X . . x | X . . . | X . x . |
                  a               e
```
- Warm, acoustic-feeling kick
- Rounder than tech house, softer than funky house
- Provides gentle foundation

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Swung pattern with dynamics:
|x . x x | . x . x | x . x x | . x . x |
(v: 80,60,90,40,70,90,50,80)
```
- Heavy swing (58–63%)
- Dynamic variation (band/drummer feel)
- Rides and cymbals layer in for sections

#### Snare/Clap
```
Backbeat with ghost notes:
|. . x . | X . . . | . . x . | X . . . |
      &     2           &     4
(x = ghost, X = accent)
```
- Soft ghost snares around the backbeat
- Warm, wide snare/clap on 2 and 4
- Reverb for space and warmth

#### Percussion
```
Tambourine (swung):
|. x . x | . x . x | . x . x | . x . x |
  &   a     &   a     &   a     &   a

Congas:
|x . . x | . x . . | . . x . | x . . . |
1       a   e           &   1
```
- Organic percussion (congas, shakers, tambourine)
- Band-feel dynamics
- Ghost notes everywhere for groove depth

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 58–63% (heavy, soulful shuffle)
- **Quantize**: Human — NOT grid-locked (band feel)
- **Overall feel**: Warm, organic, soulful
- **Groove pocket**: Behind the beat (laid-back, relaxed)

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Kick = four-on-the-floor with ghost kicks (human)
- Hats = swung, dynamic (drummer feel)
- Percussion = organic layers (congas, tambourine, shaker)
- Bass = walks between notes (chromatic, melodic)
- Vocals = rhythmically free (not grid-locked to drums)

## UK Garage

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- 2-step rhythm (NOT four-on-the-floor)

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern (2-Step — The Signature)
```
Classic 2-step kick:
|X . . . | . . . . | . . X . | . . . . |
1                       &

Variant 2:
|X . . . | . . x . | . . X . | . . . . |
1             e         &

Skippy variant:
|X . . . | . . . x | X . . . | . x . . |
1               a   3           e
```
- NOT four-on-the-floor — syncopated, "skipping" kick
- Ghost kicks fill gaps (low velocity)
- Pattern changes between bars (not static loop)

#### Snare/Clap
```
On beat 2 (NOT on 2 and 4 like house):
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | . . . . |
            2

Variant with ghost:
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | . . x . |
            2                       e
```
- Snare/clap primarily on beat 2
- Beat 4 often empty or ghost level
- Tight, short snare (not boomy)

#### Hi-Hat Pattern (Shuffled/Skippy)
```
Classic garage shuffle:
|. x . x | x . x . | . x x . | x . . x |
  e   a   1   e       e &     1       a

Faster variant:
|x . x x | . x . x | x x . x | . x x . |
1   e &     e   a   1 e   a     e &
```
- Complex, syncopated, NEVER straight
- Swing: 60–66% (heavy shuffle)
- Constant movement — hats carry the energy
- Open and closed hats mixed in complex patterns

#### Bass Pattern
```
Deep sub with syncopation:
|X . . . | . . . . | . X . . | . . . X |
1                     &             a

Wobble variant:
|X ~ ~ . | . . X ~ | ~ . . . | X . . ~ |
(~ = sustained/wobbling note)
```
- Deep sub bass (30–60 Hz)
- Syncopated with the 2-step pattern
- Pitch bends and wobbles for movement
- Reese stabs for mid-bass texture

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 60–66% (heavy shuffle — definitive)
- **Quantize**: Tight but shuffled (precise shuffle, not sloppy)
- **Overall feel**: Skippy, bouncy, light on its feet
- **Groove pocket**: Complex — parts behind, parts ahead

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Kick = 2-step syncopation (NOT steady — creates space)
- Hats = complex shuffle (primary groove carrier)
- Snare = on beat 2 (anchor point)
- Bass = syncopated, deep, with the kick
- Vocals = sit on top, rhythmically free
- Result: "Skipping" feel — every element dances independently

## Electro House

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Straight, powerful groove (minimal swing)

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Four-on-the-floor (powerful):
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4

In builds (kick removed):
|. . . . | . . . . | . . . . | . . . . |
(builds often strip the kick entirely)
```
- Heavy, punchy kick (sub + click)
- Dominant in the mix on drops
- Removed in breakdowns for contrast

#### Clap/Snare
```
Heavy on 2 and 4:
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | X . . . |
            2                   4

Build (snare roll):
|. . . . | x . x . | x x x x | X X X X |
(accelerating snare roll into drop)
```
- Big, layered clap/snare
- Builds use snare rolls (accelerating 8ths → 16ths → 32nds)
- Impact hit on drop beat 1

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Standard 8ths or 16ths:
|x . x . | x . x . | x . x . | x . x . |
(straight, minimal variation)
```
- Straight, mechanical (no swing)
- Serves the groove but doesn't dominate
- Offbeat open hat occasional

#### Sidechain Rhythm (Implied)
```
Sidechain pump pattern:
|↓ ↑ ↑ ↑ | ↓ ↑ ↑ ↑ | ↓ ↑ ↑ ↑ | ↓ ↑ ↑ ↑ |
(↓ = ducked by kick, ↑ = release/pump)
```
- Sidechain creates rhythmic pumping on pads/synths
- The "pump" IS a rhythmic element
- Defines the groove feel on drops

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 50% (straight — no swing)
- **Quantize**: Grid-locked, mechanical, tight
- **Overall feel**: Powerful, driving, heavy
- **Groove pocket**: On the beat — impact and power

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Minimal polyrhythm — power comes from alignment
- Kick = heavy four-on-the-floor
- Sidechain pump = rhythmic breathing
- Lead synth rhythm = aligned with or contrasting kick
- Everything serves the DROP moment

## Bass House

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Groove comes from bass rhythm, not percussion

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Four-on-the-floor:
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4

With ghost kicks (G-house style):
|X . . x | X . . . | X . x . | X . . . |
1       a 2         3   e     4
```
- Tight, punchy 909-style
- Sub at 50 Hz, click at 3–4 kHz
- Clean — bass needs frequency space below

#### Bass Pattern (THE GROOVE)
```
Wobble bass rhythm (drop):
|X ~ ~ x | . X ~ ~ | x . X ~ | ~ x . . |
1     &     2         e   3         a

G-house bass (simpler):
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . X | . . . . |
1         2         3     a
```
- Bass rhythm IS the primary groove
- LFO rate changes create rhythmic variation
- Pattern often 2 bars before repeating
- Syncopated against the kick

#### Hi-Hat
```
Simple 8ths or 16ths:
|x . x . | x . x . | x . x . | x . x . |
```
- Simple — doesn't compete with bass
- Provides time reference
- Minimal variation

#### Clap
```
On 2 and 4:
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | X . . . |
            2                   4
```
- Short, tight clap
- Not dominant — bass is the star
- May have reverb for width

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 50–53% (nearly straight)
- **Quantize**: Tight, grid-locked
- **Overall feel**: Heavy, bouncy (from bass LFO)
- **Groove pocket**: Bass LFO creates the "groove" more than drum timing

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Kick = four-on-the-floor (foundation)
- Bass wobble = primary groove element (LFO-driven rhythm)
- Hats = simple time reference
- Bass rhythm vs. kick creates "bounce"
- Sidechain pumping adds rhythmic interplay

## Chicago House

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Straight, mechanical, "jacking" feel

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Four-on-the-floor (mechanical, relentless):
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4
```
- TR-707/808/909 kick (raw, unprocessed)
- Steady, never varies (hypnotic repetition)
- The "jack" groove — body-moving tempo

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Open hat on every offbeat (Chicago signature):
|. . X . | . . X . | . . X . | . . X . |
      &         &         &         &

Closed hat on 16ths:
|x x x x | x x x x | x x x x | x x x x |
(when both present: closed 16ths + open on &)
```
- Open hat on offbeat = ESSENTIAL Chicago house
- Machine-feel — no humanization
- Raw drum machine output

#### Clap/Snare
```
On 2 and 4:
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | X . . . |
            2                   4
```
- 707/808/909 clap
- Dry, short, mechanical
- No processing — straight from the machine

#### Cowbell (Dance Mania Signature)
```
|. x . x | . x . x | . x . x | . x . x |
  e   a     e   a     e   a     e   a
(8th notes)
```
- Cowbell pattern common (Dance Mania era)
- Adds metallic, driving energy
- Simple, repetitive

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 50% (STRAIGHT — no swing)
- **Quantize**: Machine-perfect (drum machine output)
- **Overall feel**: Mechanical, hypnotic, jacking
- **Groove pocket**: Perfectly on the beat — machine-timed

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- MINIMAL polyrhythm — simplicity is the point
- Kick = steady quarter notes
- Open hat = steady offbeats
- Clap = beats 2 and 4
- Everything aligned, mechanical, repetitive
- Hypnosis through repetition, not complexity

## Ghetto House

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Fast, aggressive, relentless

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Four-on-the-floor (aggressive):
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4

Variant with syncopation:
|X . . X | . . X . | X . . X | . . X . |
1     a       &   1       a       &
```
- 808 kick — heavy, deep
- Fast tempo (125–135 BPM) makes it relentless
- May double-up or syncopate for energy

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Fast 16ths (machine-gun):
|x x x x | x x x x | x x x x | x x x x |

Triplet variant (faster feel):
|x x x x x x | x x x x x x | x x x x x x | x x x x x x |
(triplet 16ths)
```
- Fast, mechanical, constant
- No swing — pure machine precision
- At 135 BPM, 16ths are very fast

#### Clap/Snare
```
On 2 and 4 (hard):
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | X . . . |
            2                   4
```
- 808 snare/clap — dry, hard
- May layer for impact
- Repetitive, driving

#### 808 Tom Rolls
```
Fill pattern:
|. . . . | . . . . | T T T T | T T T T |
                    (fill into next section)

Accent pattern:
|. . T . | . . . . | . T . . | . . . . |
      &               e
```
- 808 toms for fills and accents
- Rolls into section changes
- Pitched toms (high, mid, low)

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 50% (STRAIGHT — no swing)
- **Quantize**: Machine-perfect, grid-locked
- **Overall feel**: Fast, aggressive, relentless
- **Groove pocket**: On the beat — driving forward

### Polyrhythmic Relationships

## UK Funky

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Broken, syncopated, tribal-influenced groove

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern (Broken/Syncopated)
```
Syncopated UK funky kick:
|X . . . | . . x . | X . . . | . x . . |
1             &   3           e

Variant 2 (more broken):
|X . . x | . . . . | . . X . | . . . x |
1       a               &           a
```
- NOT standard four-on-the-floor
- Syncopated, broken pattern
- Garage-influenced with tribal energy

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Complex syncopated hats:
|x . x x | . x . x | x . x . | x x . x |
1   e &     e   a   1   e       1 e   a

Triplet influence:
|x . x . x . | x . x . x . | x . x . x . | x . x . x . |
(implied triplet feel over 4/4)
```
- Complex, broken patterns (never straight 16ths)
- Heavy swing (60–65%)
- Soca/carnival rhythmic influence

#### Percussion (Tribal Layers)
```
Conga pattern:
|. x . . | x . . x | . . x . | . x . . |
  e       1       a       &     e

Shaker/Woodblock:
|x . x x | x . x . | x x . x | . x x . |
```
- 3–5 percussion layers
- Tribal: congas, bongos, djembe, woodblocks
- Each layer syncopated differently (polyrhythmic)
- Pan wide for immersive percussion field

#### Bass Pattern
```
Syncopated, heavy bass:
|X . . . | . . X . | . . . X | . . . . |
1             &           a

Sub drop:
|X ~ ~ ~ | . . . . | X ~ ~ ~ | . . . . |
(sustained sub hits)
```
- Deep sub bass (UK bass culture)
- Syncopated with the broken kick
- May wobble or pitch-bend

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 60–65% (heavy shuffle, tribal feel)
- **Quantize**: Loose on percussion, tighter on kick/bass
- **Overall feel**: Bouncy, syncopated, percussive, tribal
- **Groove pocket**: Complex — pushes and pulls

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Kick = broken, syncopated (NOT steady anchor)
- Hats = complex shuffle (soca/carnival influence)
- Congas = tribal polyrhythm (3 against 4 implied)
- Bass = heavy accents, syncopated
- Result: Dense, percussive, danceable complexity

## French Touch

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout
- Disco groove filtered through house production

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Four-on-the-floor (disco-house weight):
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4
```
- 909-style kick (tight, punchy)
- Triggers the extreme sidechain compression
- Steady, constant, driving

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Open hat on offbeats (disco):
|. . X . | . . X . | . . X . | . . X . |
      &         &         &         &

With closed 16ths:
|x x X x | x x X x | x x X x | x x X x |
      &         &         &         &
```
- Disco-style open hat on offbeats
- 16th closed hats for drive
- Machine-precise (909 patterns)

#### Clap
```
On 2 and 4:
|. . . . | X . . . | . . . . | X . . . |
            2                   4
```
- Tight, snappy clap
- Sits behind the pumping sidechain effect

#### Sidechain Pump Pattern (THE SIGNATURE)
```
Everything ducks on kick:
Element: | ↓ ↑ ↑ ↑ | ↓ ↑ ↑ ↑ | ↓ ↑ ↑ ↑ | ↓ ↑ ↑ ↑ |
Kick:    | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
(↓ = heavy duck 8-12dB, ↑ = release creates "pump")
```
- Extreme sidechain (8–12 dB ducking)
- Creates the iconic "pumping" rhythm
- Applied to EVERYTHING except kick and hats

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 50–53% (straight to slight swing)
- **Quantize**: Machine-tight (disco precision)
- **Overall feel**: Pumping, driving, disco-filtered
- **Groove pocket**: On the beat — sidechain creates the groove feel

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Kick = four-on-the-floor (sidechain trigger)
- Sidechain pump = primary rhythmic feel
- Open hat = disco offbeat (counter to kick)
- Sampled disco content = original groove underneath
- Filter position reveals/hides underlying disco rhythms

- Classic house groove with degraded/imprecise timing

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern
```
Four-on-the-floor (slightly unquantized):
|X . . . | X . . . | X . . . | X . . . |
1         2         3         4
(±3-8ms timing drift from perfect grid)
```
- Classic house kick but slightly imprecise
- Saturated/distorted character
- Timing drift is intentional (human/tape feel)

#### Hi-Hat Pattern
```
Swung 16ths (slightly off-grid):
|x . x x | . x x . | x x . x | x . x x |
(timing drifts ±5-10ms from grid)
```
- Classic deep house pattern but imperfect
- Timing drift from quantize disabled or tape recording
- Warm, dark character (filtered/saturated)

#### Clap
```
On 2 and 4 (slightly behind beat):
|. . . . | . X . . | . . . . | . X . . |
            2+                  4+
(hits 5-10ms behind the beat)
```
- Laid-back placement (behind the beat)
- Lo-fi character (saturated, crunchy)
- Short, not huge reverb

#### Chord Rhythm (Part of Groove)
```
Filtered chord stabs:
|. . X . | . . . . | . . X . | . . . X |
      &                   &           a
```
- Filtered/muffled chord stabs
- Part of rhythmic texture (not just harmony)
- Tape warble adds pitch drift

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 55–60% (medium, natural feel)
- **Quantize**: Deliberately OFF — unquantized, drifting
- **Overall feel**: Warm, imprecise, intimate, human
- **Groove pocket**: Slightly loose — bedroom feel

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Same as classic deep house patterns
- Imprecision creates micro-polyrhythm unintentionally
- Kick = slightly drifting four-on-the-floor
- Hats = swung but imprecise
- Chord stabs = rhythmic element adding groove texture
- Tape speed variation = constant micro-drift

## Future Garage

### Time Signature
- 4/4 throughout (implied)
- BUT feels like half-time or ambiguous tempo
- 2-step rhythm implied but broken/deconstructed

### Core Groove Components

#### Kick Pattern (Sparse, Unquantized)
```
Sparse 2-step influenced:
|X . . . | . . . . | . . x . | . . . . |
1                       &
(ghost kick at very low velocity)

More broken:
|X . . . | . . . . | . . . . | . . X . |
1                               &
```
- SPARSE — not on every beat
- Unquantized (±10–20ms from grid)
- Feels like half-time (kick every 2+ beats)
- Deep, soft kick (not aggressive)

#### Snare/Clap (Deconstructed)
```
Off-grid placement:
|. . . . | . . X . | . . . . | . . . . |
              ~2.5   (not exactly on beat 2)
```
- Placed by feel, not grid
- May fall between beats (intentional)
- Soft, reverbed, distant

#### Hi-Hat (Shuffled, Unquantized)
```
Broken shuffle:
|. x . . | x . . x | . . x . | . . . x |
  e       1       a       &           a
(each hit ±5-15ms from grid position)
```
- Complex shuffle inspired by UKG but LOOSENED
- Timing drifts — not metronomic
- Low velocity, soft, distant
- Ghost hats create atmosphere

#### Vinyl Crackle / Atmosphere (Rhythmic Texture)
```
Constant:
|c c c c | c c c c | c c c c | c c c c |
(c = continuous crackle layer underneath)
```
- Vinyl crackle as constant texture bed
- Field recordings (rain, ambience)
- These provide rhythmic micro-detail

### Swing & Feel
- **Swing amount**: 55–65% (heavy but inconsistent — changes per beat)
- **Quantize**: UNQUANTIZED — placed by feel
- **Overall feel**: Drifting, melancholic, spacious, atmospheric
- **Groove pocket**: Ambiguous — simultaneously half-time and garage-speed

### Polyrhythmic Relationships
- Kick = sparse, unquantized (anchor is implied, not stated)
- Hats = broken shuffle (UKG DNA but deconstructed)
- Vocal chops = rhythmic fragments adding complexity
- Reverb/delay = creates ghost-rhythms from other elements
- Vinyl crackle = constant textural bed
- Result: Floating, atmospheric rhythm — more "felt" than "counted"
