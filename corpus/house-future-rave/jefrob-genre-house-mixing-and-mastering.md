# Mixing & Mastering Benchmarks

## Deep House

### Frequency Spectrum Profile
| Range | Content | Level |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 20–60 Hz | Kick sub, bass fundamental | Present but controlled |
| 60–200 Hz | Bass body, kick body, warmth | WARM — genre character lives here |
| 200–500 Hz | Chord low-mids, vocal warmth | Moderate — avoid mud |
| 500 Hz–2 kHz | Rhodes/keys, vocal body | Clear, warm presence |
| 2–6 kHz | Vocal presence, hat transients | Present but never harsh |
| 6–12 kHz | Hat air, shaker shimmer | Smooth, warm |
| 12–20 kHz | Air, subtle sparkle | Gentle — rolled off slightly |

### Stereo Field
- **Mono center**: Kick, bass, lead vocal
- **Narrow stereo**: Chord stabs (slight width), congas
- **Wide stereo**: Pads, shakers, reverb returns, atmospheric elements
- **Overall width**: Moderate-wide — spacious and warm
- **Mono compatibility**: Important (club systems)

### Dynamics & Loudness
- **Target LUFS (streaming)**: -14 LUFS integrated
- **Target LUFS (club)**: -10 to -11 LUFS
- **Dynamic range**: 8–12 dB (preserve groove dynamics)
- **Bus compression**: Light (2 dB GR max), glue only
- **Limiter**: Gentle — avoid killing the groove
- **Philosophy**: Deep house BREATHES — never over-compress

### EQ Signature Moves
- High-pass bass at 35 Hz (let kick own sub)
- Warmth boost at 100–150 Hz on bass (genre character)
- Cut Rhodes/keys at 200–300 Hz (prevent mud with bass)
- Gentle presence boost on vocals at 2–3 kHz
- Low-pass master gently at 16 kHz (smooth top end)

### Common Mix Mistakes
- Too bright/harsh (deep house should be warm)
- Over-compression killing groove dynamics
- Bass and kick competing (need sidechain or EQ carving)
- Too many elements (space IS the genre)
- Pads too loud/forward (should be background warmth)

## Tech House

### Frequency Spectrum Profile
| Range | Content | Level |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 20–60 Hz | Kick sub | Tight, controlled |
| 60–150 Hz | Kick body, bass | Punchy, present |
| 150–500 Hz | Percussion body, bass upper | Moderate |
| 500 Hz–2 kHz | Vocal chops, percussion mid | Clear, dry |
| 2–6 kHz | Kick click, hat body, vocal presence | Crisp, present |
| 6–12 kHz | Hat air, ride shimmer | Bright, open |
| 12–20 kHz | Air | Present but not harsh |

### Stereo Field
- **Mono center**: Kick, bass, vocal chop (main)
- **Narrow stereo**: Clap, main percussion
- **Wide stereo**: Rides, shakers, congas, reverb returns
- **Overall width**: Moderate — functional for DJing
- **Mono compatibility**: Critical (club systems)

### Dynamics & Loudness
- **Target LUFS (DJ tool)**: -8 to -9 LUFS integrated
- **Target LUFS (streaming)**: -14 LUFS
- **Dynamic range**: 5–8 dB (punchy, consistent)
- **Bus compression**: Medium (3–4 dB GR), parallel percussion compression
- **Limiter**: Moderate — punchy loudness
- **Philosophy**: Punchy and clean — functional DJ tool

### EQ Signature Moves
- Tight HPF on kick at 30 Hz (control sub rumble)
- Boost kick click at 3–5 kHz (presence)
- Cut bass at 200–300 Hz (avoid muddiness with kick)
- High-pass percussion at 300–500 Hz (clarity)
- Notch resonances in vocal chops (500 Hz–1 kHz area)

### Common Mix Mistakes
- Kick and bass fighting (need clear frequency separation)
- Percussion layers masking each other (need panning and EQ)
- Too much reverb (tech house is DRY)
- Vocal chops too loud (texture, not lead)
- Not enough click on kick (needs presence to cut through)

## Progressive House

### Frequency Spectrum Profile
| Range | Content | Level |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 20–60 Hz | Kick sub, bass fundamental | Clean, extended |
| 60–200 Hz | Bass body, pad low-mids | Warm, controlled |
| 200–500 Hz | Pad body, arpeggio low-mids | Full but clear |
| 500 Hz–2 kHz | Arpeggios, pads, vocal body | Lush, present |
| 2–6 kHz | Arpeggio presence, vocal air | Clear, smooth |
| 6–12 kHz | Hat air, shimmer, FX | Bright, airy |
| 12–20 kHz | Air, sparkle, reverb tails | Present, smooth |

### Stereo Field
- **Mono center**: Kick, bass, main arpeggio
- **Narrow stereo**: Clap, mid pads
- **Wide stereo**: High pads, reverb returns, FX, atmospherics
- **Overall width**: WIDE — immersive, cinematic
- **Mono compatibility**: Check but less critical (headphone/speaker listening)

### Dynamics & Loudness
- **Target LUFS (streaming)**: -12 to -14 LUFS
- **Target LUFS (club)**: -9 to -11 LUFS
- **Dynamic range**: 8–12 dB (preserve builds and breakdowns)
- **Bus compression**: Light (2 dB GR), transparent glue
- **Limiter**: Gentle — dynamics ARE the genre
- **Philosophy**: Dynamics create the journey — builds need headroom

### EQ Signature Moves
- HPF everything except kick/bass at 80–100 Hz (clean low end)
- Scoop 200–400 Hz on pads (prevent mud buildup from layers)
- Gentle boost at 8–12 kHz on pads (shimmer, air)
- Notch competing frequencies between arpeggio and pads
- Presence dip at 2 kHz on pads (make room for arpeggios)

### Common Mix Mistakes
- Pad buildup creating muddy 200–400 Hz (too many layers)
- Low end too boomy (needs tight sub, not rumble)
- Losing dynamics in mastering (squashed builds lose impact)
- Reverb tails causing phase issues in low end
- Not enough contrast between sections (breakdowns should breathe)

## Funky House

### Frequency Spectrum Profile
| Range | Content | Level |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 20–60 Hz | Kick sub, bass fundamental | Present, punchy |
| 60–200 Hz | Bass body, kick body | Strong, funky |
| 200–500 Hz | Guitar/keys body, vocal warmth | Warm, full |
| 500 Hz–2 kHz | Vocal presence, horn body, guitar | Energetic, forward |
| 2–6 kHz | Vocal clarity, brass bite, hat body | Bright, energetic |
| 6–12 kHz | Open hat, tambourine, air | Bright, disco sparkle |
| 12–20 kHz | Air, cymbal shimmer | Present |

### Stereo Field
- **Mono center**: Kick, bass, lead vocal
- **Narrow stereo**: Guitar, keys
- **Wide stereo**: Strings, backing vocals, open hats, tambourine, reverb
- **Overall width**: Wide — vibrant, energetic
- **Mono compatibility**: Important for club systems

### Dynamics & Loudness
- **Target LUFS (streaming)**: -14 LUFS
- **Target LUFS (club)**: -9 to -10 LUFS
- **Dynamic range**: 6–9 dB (energetic but groovy)
- **Bus compression**: Medium (3–4 dB GR), adds glue and punch
- **Limiter**: Moderate — punchy and loud
- **Philosophy**: Energetic but never squashed — must groove

### EQ Signature Moves
- Boost bass at 80–120 Hz (funky presence)
- Cut at 250–350 Hz (prevent muddiness between bass and keys)
- Presence boost on vocals at 3–5 kHz (cut through the mix)
- Boost open hat at 8 kHz (disco sparkle)
- High-pass strings at 200 Hz (keep them airy, not boomy)

### Common Mix Mistakes
- Bass too subby (funky house bass needs MID presence, not just sub)
- Open hat too loud/harsh (should be present but not piercing)
- Vocals buried (should be clear and upfront in funky house)
- Mix too clean/sterile (needs warmth and analog character)
- Losing the groove dynamics to over-compression

## UK Garage

### Frequency Spectrum Profile
| Range | Content | Level |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 20–60 Hz | Sub bass, kick sub | HEAVY — massive sub |
| 60–150 Hz | Bass body, reese mid | Strong |
| 150–500 Hz | Reese bass upper, vocal warmth | Moderate |
| 500 Hz–2 kHz | Vocal chops, pad body | Clear, present |
| 2–6 kHz | Vocal presence, hat detail | Crisp, detailed |
| 6–12 kHz | Hat shimmer, pad air | Bright, detailed |
| 12–20 kHz | Air, sparkle | Present |

### Stereo Field
- **Mono center**: Kick, sub bass, lead vocal
- **Narrow stereo**: Reese bass, clap
- **Wide stereo**: Super saw pads, hats (shuffled), reverb, backing vocals
- **Overall width**: Wide — pads and hats create space
- **Mono compatibility**: Essential (club systems need mono sub)

### Dynamics & Loudness
- **Target LUFS (club)**: -9 to -11 LUFS
- **Target LUFS (streaming)**: -14 LUFS
- **Dynamic range**: 7–10 dB (shuffled groove needs dynamics)
- **Bus compression**: Medium (3–4 dB GR)
- **Limiter**: Moderate — preserve groove shuffle
- **Philosophy**: Sub-heavy but clear — skip and groove must be dynamic

### EQ Signature Moves
- Sub bass: HPF at 25 Hz, low-pass at 80 Hz (clean sub)
- Reese: HPF at 100 Hz, focused 200–500 Hz (mid-bass texture)
- Vocal chops: presence at 3–5 kHz, HPF at 200 Hz
- Hats: HPF at 5 kHz (thin, detailed, no muddiness)
- Pads: scoop at 300–500 Hz (prevent mud with bass layers)

### Common Mix Mistakes
- Sub bass too loud/unfocused (needs tight control)
- Reese and sub fighting (need clear frequency division)
- Hats lost in mix (shuffled groove must be clear)
- Vocal chops too quiet (should be present and clear)
- Not enough sub (UK garage IS sub-heavy — don't be shy)

## Electro House

### Frequency Spectrum Profile
| Range | Content | Level |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 20–60 Hz | Kick sub, bass sub | Tight, powerful |
| 60–200 Hz | Bass body, kick body | Heavy, punchy |
| 200–500 Hz | Lead synth low, bass mid | Full, aggressive |
| 500 Hz–2 kHz | Lead synth body | DOMINANT on drops |
| 2–6 kHz | Lead presence, kick click | Bright, cutting |
| 6–12 kHz | Lead air, hat, effects | Bright, aggressive |
| 12–20 kHz | Air, sparkle | Present |

### Stereo Field
- **Mono center**: Kick, bass sub layer
- **Narrow stereo**: Lead synth (centered but wide)
- **Wide stereo**: Lead width layers, pads, FX, risers
- **Overall width**: MAXIMUM on drops (wide leads)
- **Mono compatibility**: Check bass in mono; leads can be wide

### Dynamics & Loudness
- **Target LUFS (club/festival)**: -6 to -8 LUFS (LOUD)
- **Target LUFS (streaming)**: -14 LUFS (normalized)
- **Dynamic range**: 4–6 dB (heavily compressed)
- **Bus compression**: Heavy (4–6 dB GR on drops)
- **Limiter**: Aggressive — ceiling pushing
- **Philosophy**: LOUD, impactful, festival-ready

### EQ Signature Moves
- Tight sub bass (HPF at 30 Hz, LPF at 80 Hz for sub layer)
- Aggressive mid boost on lead (500 Hz–2 kHz for impact)
- Sidechain EVERYTHING to kick (including leads)
- Cut mid on bass during lead sections (frequency separation)
- Boost presence on kick at 4–5 kHz (punch through dense mix)

### Common Mix Mistakes
- Drop not loud/impactful enough (needs aggressive mastering)
- Sub bass too boomy (tight and controlled for impact)
- Lead synth and bass fighting for the same space
- Build not contrasting enough with drop (needs dynamic range between sections)
- Over-limiting creating distortion (use clipping before limiting)

## Bass House

### Frequency Spectrum Profile
| Range | Content | Level |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 20–60 Hz | Sub bass layer, kick sub | Deep, clean |
| 60–200 Hz | Wobble bass fundamental, kick body | DOMINANT — bass lives here |
| 200–500 Hz | Wobble harmonics, bass mid | Strong, aggressive |
| 500 Hz–2 kHz | Wobble top layer, vocal chops | Present, cutting |
| 2–6 kHz | Kick click, hat, bass presence | Bright, clear |
| 6–12 kHz | Hat air, effects | Bright |
| 12–20 kHz | Air | Less important |

### Stereo Field
- **Mono center**: Kick, sub bass, wobble bass center
- **Narrow stereo**: Wobble mid (slight width)
- **Wide stereo**: Wobble top/effects, pads, reverb
- **Overall width**: Moderate — bass-focused mixes are center-heavy
- **Mono compatibility**: Critical (bass must work on mono systems)

### Dynamics & Loudness
- **Target LUFS (club)**: -7 to -9 LUFS
- **Target LUFS (streaming)**: -14 LUFS
- **Dynamic range**: 5–7 dB (compressed bass, dynamic arrangement)
- **Bus compression**: Heavy on bass bus (4–6 dB GR), moderate on master
- **Limiter**: Moderate-heavy — bass needs loudness
- **Philosophy**: Bass-forward, punchy, impactful

### EQ Signature Moves
- Sub layer: HPF at 25 Hz, LPF at 80 Hz (clean sub)
- Wobble mid: HPF at 80 Hz, focused 100–500 Hz
- Wobble top: HPF at 300 Hz (presence/character layer)
- Kick: cut at 80–100 Hz if fighting with bass sub
- Sidechain all bass layers to kick (tight ducking)

### Common Mix Mistakes
- Bass layers fighting each other (need clear frequency separation)
- Sub bass undefined (too muddy, needs tight HPF/LPF)
- Kick lost behind bass (needs sidechain and EQ separation)
- Bass too wide in low end (mono below 150 Hz)
- Not enough contrast between drops and breaks

| 2–6 kHz | Hat attack, vocal clarity | Bright, raw |
| 6–12 kHz | Open hat, cymbal | Bright, metallic |
| 12–20 kHz | Air | Less relevant (lo-fi aesthetic) |

### Stereo Field
- **Mono center**: EVERYTHING (or nearly everything)
- **Narrow stereo**: Minimal stereo width typical
- **Wide stereo**: Not characteristic — genre is mono/narrow
- **Overall width**: NARROW — mono-forward production
- **Mono compatibility**: Inherent (most recordings are mono/narrow)

### Dynamics & Loudness
- **Target LUFS (remastered)**: -10 to -12 LUFS
- **Target LUFS (original character)**: Variable — often quieter
- **Dynamic range**: 6–10 dB (raw, uncompressed character)
- **Bus compression**: Minimal or none (raw machine output)
- **Limiter**: Minimal (tape saturation provides limiting)
- **Philosophy**: RAW — don't over-process. Imperfection is the character

### EQ Signature Moves
- MINIMAL EQ — genre is about raw output
- Let drum machine speak without heavy processing
- Tape saturation provides natural frequency shaping
- If EQ needed: gentle warmth boost at 100–200 Hz
- Accept lo-fi character — don't try to "fix" it

### Common Mix Mistakes
- Over-processing (genre should sound RAW)
- Too much EQ/compression (removes character)
- Trying to make it sound modern (wrong approach)
- Stereo widening (genre is mono/narrow by nature)
- Removing tape hiss/imperfections (they ARE the sound)

## French Touch

### Frequency Spectrum Profile
| Range | Content | Level |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 20–60 Hz | Kick sub, bass sub | Tight, punchy |
| 60–200 Hz | Bass body, sample low | Strong (when filter open) |
| 200–500 Hz | Sample body, disco warmth | Full, warm, saturated |
| 500 Hz–2 kHz | Sample mid, vocal hooks | Rich, present |
| 2–6 kHz | Sample presence, hat body | Bright (when filter open) |
| 6–12 kHz | Hat air, sample shimmer | Variable (filter-dependent) |
| 12–20 kHz | Air | Variable |

### Stereo Field
- **Mono center**: Kick, bass
- **Narrow stereo**: Main disco sample (pumping)
- **Wide stereo**: Phased elements, reverb, atmospheric moments
- **Overall width**: Moderate — pumping compression narrows perceived width
- **Mono compatibility**: Important (club systems)

### Dynamics & Loudness
- **Target LUFS (club)**: -7 to -9 LUFS
- **Target LUFS (streaming)**: -14 LUFS
- **Dynamic range**: 4–7 dB (heavily compressed — sidechain creates perceived dynamics)
- **Bus compression**: Heavy (4–6 dB GR — genre signature)
- **Limiter**: Aggressive — saturated loudness
- **Philosophy**: Saturated, compressed, PUMPING — loudness through saturation

### EQ Signature Moves
- Resonant LP filter sweep (200 Hz → 10 kHz) is THE signature
- Boost sample warmth at 200–500 Hz (disco character)
- Sidechain creates frequency "pumping" (no EQ needed for that)
- Saturate master bus (tape/tube character)
- Mono bass below 120 Hz

### Common Mix Mistakes
- Sidechain too subtle (should be EXTREME — 8–12 dB ducking)
- Filter sweep not dramatic enough (should define the structure)
- Mix too clean/digital (needs analog saturation character)
- Not enough compression (genre is COMPRESSED by nature)
- Losing disco sample quality under too much processing

## Lo-Fi House / Outsider House

### Frequency Spectrum Profile
| Range | Content | Level |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 20–60 Hz | Kick sub, bass | Present, warm |
| 60–200 Hz | Bass body, kick body | Warm, saturated |
| 200–500 Hz | Chord body, vocal warmth | Warm, muffled |
| 500 Hz–2 kHz | Chord mid, sample content | Present but filtered |
| 2–6 kHz | Reduced — lo-fi character | Rolled off |
| 6–12 kHz | Tape hiss, vinyl crackle | Subtle texture |
| 12–20 kHz | Minimal content | Reduced — warm character |

### Stereo Field
- **Mono center**: Kick, bass
- **Narrow stereo**: Chord stabs, main elements
- **Wide stereo**: Reverb, crackle, atmosphere
- **Overall width**: Moderate — warm, intimate
- **Mono compatibility**: Usually fine (warm production is mono-friendly)

### Dynamics & Loudness
- **Target LUFS**: -11 to -13 LUFS (NOT loud — intimate)
- **Dynamic range**: 6–9 dB (tape compression limits range naturally)
- **Bus compression**: Tape compression character (natural)
- **Limiter**: Gentle or via tape saturation
- **Philosophy**: Warm, NOT loud — intimate bedroom listening feel

### EQ Signature Moves
- Low-pass master at 10–12 kHz (rolled-off highs — genre character)
- Tape saturation provides natural frequency shaping
- Boost warmth at 100–200 Hz (cozy bass presence)
- DON'T fix the darkness — it's intentional
- Vinyl noise sits at 2–6 kHz (don't cut it)

### Common Mix Mistakes
- Too bright/clean (genre is intentionally dark/warm)
- Over-processing to "fix" lo-fi character (it's intentional)
- Too loud (genre is intimate, not club-banger loud)
- Removing tape hiss/vinyl noise (it's essential texture)
- Drums too quantized/perfect (should drift slightly)

## Future Garage

### Frequency Spectrum Profile
| Range | Content | Level |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 20–60 Hz | Sub bass, kick sub | Deep, warm, controlled |
| 60–200 Hz | Reese bass body | Warm, dark |
| 200–500 Hz | Pad body, vocal warmth | Warm, spacious |
| 500 Hz–2 kHz | Vocal chops, pad mid | Present, atmospheric |
| 2–6 kHz | Vocal presence, percussion detail | Soft, not harsh |
| 6–12 kHz | Crackle, hi-hat air, reverb shimmer | Detailed, soft |
| 12–20 kHz | Air, reverb tail | Present, gentle |

### Stereo Field
- **Mono center**: Kick, sub bass
- **Narrow stereo**: Reese bass, snare
- **Wide stereo**: Vocal chops, reverb returns, crackle, pads, atmosphere
- **Overall width**: VERY WIDE — immersive, cinematic
- **Mono compatibility**: Less critical (headphone/speaker listening, not primarily club)

### Dynamics & Loudness
- **Target LUFS**: -12 to -14 LUFS (quiet, intimate, dynamic)
- **Dynamic range**: 10–14 dB (very dynamic — genre breathes)
- **Bus compression**: Minimal (1–2 dB GR max)
- **Limiter**: Barely touching (preserve all dynamics)
- **Philosophy**: DYNAMIC — quietness and space are the beauty

### EQ Signature Moves
- Warm HPF on reverb returns at 150–200 Hz (prevent muddy reverb)
- LPF reverb returns at 6–8 kHz (dark, warm reverb character)
- Gentle warmth on reese bass at 100–200 Hz
- Presence boost on vocal chops at 2–4 kHz (cut through reverb)
- Don't over-EQ — softness is the aesthetic

### Common Mix Mistakes
- Too loud/compressed (genre needs dynamics and quietness)
- Reverb causing mud (need to HPF/LPF reverb returns)
- Elements too present/forward (everything should be somewhat distant)
- Kick too aggressive (should be soft, supporting)
- Not enough space between elements (space IS the genre)
