# Sound Design Palette

## Deep House

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Warm, round, deep — NOT clicky or aggressive
- **Frequency**: Fundamental 50–60 Hz, body 80–120 Hz, minimal click above 3 kHz
- **Length**: Medium-long (slight tail for warmth)
- **Processing**: Gentle saturation, subtle compression, HPF at 30 Hz
- **Source**: 909-based samples or warm analog synthesis

#### Bass
- **Character**: Round, warm, smooth — sine/triangle with subtle harmonics
- **Synthesis**: Sine wave + low-pass filtered saw for harmonics
- **Frequency**: 40–120 Hz fundamental
- **Processing**: Light saturation, sidechain to kick (2–3 dB), mono below 120 Hz
- **Movement**: Subtle filter modulation for warmth
- **Pattern**: Follows root notes with chromatic passing tones

#### Rhodes/EP Chords
- **Character**: Warm, jazzy, electric piano — NOT bright acoustic piano
- **Synthesis**: Keyscape, Lounge Lizard, or FM synthesis (DX7-inspired)
- **Voicings**: 7ths, 9ths, 11ths — jazz voicings, shell chords
- **Articulation**: Staccato stabs on off-beats OR sustained pads
- **Processing**: Light chorus, plate reverb, gentle compression
- **Frequency**: 250 Hz–3 kHz (warm midrange)

#### Pads
- **Character**: Warm, evolving, analog — background atmosphere
- **Synthesis**: Analog-modeled (Juno-106 character), soft saw waves
- **Processing**: Long reverb (3–5s), subtle chorus, low-pass filter
- **Role**: Fill harmonic space without drawing attention
- **Stereo**: Wide, diffuse, sitting behind main elements

#### Hi-Hats & Percussion
- **Character**: Warm, shuffled, organic
- **Source**: 909 hats (classic) or sampled acoustic cymbals
- **Processing**: HPF at 3 kHz, gentle room reverb, slight compression
- **Shaker**: Egg shaker or maracas — constant 16ths, airy (6–14 kHz)
- **Congas**: Warm, medium decay, panned slightly off-center

## Tech House

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Punchy, present, clicky — drives the track forward
- **Frequency**: Sub at 50–70 Hz, body at 100 Hz, click at 3–5 kHz
- **Length**: Short-medium (tight low end)
- **Processing**: Transient shaping (enhance attack), saturation, HPF at 30 Hz
- **Source**: 909-based with layered click/top sample

#### Bass
- **Character**: Driving, mid-present, slightly gritty
- **Synthesis**: Saw or square wave, low-pass filtered with resonance
- **Frequency**: 60–200 Hz (more mid-presence than deep house)
- **Processing**: Distortion/saturation, sidechain to kick (fast), mono
- **Pattern**: 16th note arpeggios or rolling patterns
- **Movement**: Filter cutoff modulation

#### Vocal Chops
- **Character**: Short fragments (1–4 words), processed, rhythmic
- **Processing**: Pitch-shift, time-stretch, reverse, delay throws
- **Frequency**: 500 Hz–5 kHz (midrange presence)
- **Role**: Rhythmic texture, hook element
- **Effects**: Heavy reverb on throws, stutter effects

#### Percussion Ensemble
- **Congas/Bongos**: Warm, panned, multiple articulations
- **Rimshots**: Sharp, dry, midrange (2–5 kHz)
- **Shakers**: Constant 16ths, high-frequency (8–12 kHz)
- **Rides/Bells**: Metallic accents, offbeat placement
- **Processing**: Group compression (2–3 dB GR), room reverb for cohesion

#### Synth Stabs
- **Character**: Short, percussive hits — simple waveform
- **Synthesis**: Saw or square with fast envelope
- **Processing**: Reverb throw, distortion, filter sweep
- **Role**: Accent, transition marker, energy addition
- **Frequency**: 200 Hz–2 kHz

## Progressive House

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Clean, balanced, tight — not too heavy, not too clicky
- **Frequency**: Sub at 50–60 Hz, body at 100 Hz, subtle click at 3 kHz
- **Length**: Medium (slightly extended sub tail)
- **Processing**: Minimal — clean, uncolored
- **Source**: Layered: sine sub + acoustic-style body + click top

#### Layered Pads
- **Character**: Evolving, lush, wide — multiple layers at different octaves
- **Synthesis**: Wavetable (Serum, Phase Plant) or analog modeling
- **Layer 1**: Low pad (200–500 Hz, warm foundation)
- **Layer 2**: Mid pad (500 Hz–2 kHz, harmonic content)
- **Layer 3**: High pad (2–8 kHz, air and shimmer)
- **Processing**: Slow LFO on cutoff, long reverb, sidechain to kick
- **Movement**: Each layer filtered differently, revealed through automation

#### Arpeggiated Synths
- **Character**: Clean, crystalline, evolving over time
- **Synthesis**: Saw or pulse wave, detuned slightly
- **Pattern**: 16th note arpeggios, 4–8 notes
- **Processing**: Delay (dotted 8th), reverb, filter automation
- **Role**: Provides forward motion and harmonic interest
- **Evolution**: New notes added/removed over 32+ bars

#### Bass
- **Character**: Clean, sub-focused with evolving harmonics
- **Synthesis**: Sine sub + filtered saw for mid character
- **Processing**: Sidechain to kick, subtle distortion for harmonics
- **Movement**: Filter opens gradually over builds
- **Frequency**: 40–150 Hz (sub extends to mid during peaks)

#### FX and Atmospherics
- **Risers**: White noise sweeps, reversed reverb tails
- **Impacts**: Sub drops, layered hits for section starts
- **Textures**: Field recordings, granular processing, shimmer reverbs
- **Processing**: Heavy spatial effects (reverb, delay, granular)
- **Role**: Transitions, builds, creating anticipation

## Funky House

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Punchy, disco-weight, full-bodied
- **Frequency**: Sub at 55–65 Hz, body at 100–150 Hz, presence at 2–3 kHz
- **Length**: Medium (disco thump)
- **Processing**: Light compression, saturation for warmth
- **Source**: 909 or sampled acoustic disco kick

#### Funky Bass
- **Character**: Slap bass, octave jumps, percussive attack
- **Synthesis**: Bass guitar sample/VST or analog synth with fast envelope
- **Frequency**: 60–250 Hz (strong mid-bass presence)
- **Processing**: Compression (for consistent level), subtle saturation
- **Articulation**: Staccato, hammer-ons, slides, ghost notes
- **Pattern**: Syncopated, octave jumps, chromatic approaches

#### Disco Samples
- **Character**: Chopped strings, horns, guitar from vinyl records
- **Source**: 70s/80s disco, funk, boogie records
- **Processing**: EQ to fit modern mix, phaser/flanger for movement
- **Frequency**: Variable (depends on source — typically midrange)
- **Role**: Main melodic/harmonic hook

#### Open Hi-Hat (Signature)
- **Character**: Bright, consistent offbeat pulse
- **Frequency**: 6–12 kHz (bright, cutting)
- **Processing**: Minimal — raw, disco character
- **Pattern**: Every offbeat (8th note) — THE disco/funky house identifier
- **Source**: 909 open hat or sampled acoustic cymbal

#### Strings / Horns
- **Character**: Disco-style orchestral stabs
- **Synthesis**: Sample-based (Kontakt libraries) or sampled from records
- **Voicings**: Lush, full arrangements (strings) or punchy stabs (horns)
- **Processing**: Reverb, gentle EQ, subtle compression
- **Role**: Energy lifts, hook sections

## Afro House

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Deep, rounded, organic — less electronic than tech house
- **Frequency**: Fundamental 40–55 Hz, body 80–120 Hz
- **Length**: Medium
- **Processing**: Gentle saturation, HPF at 30 Hz, minimal click
- **Source**: Acoustic-influenced samples or analog synthesis

#### Percussion Ensemble
- **Djembe**: Warm bass tone + sharp slap articulations
- **Congas**: Open, muted, slap — multiple articulations per instrument
- **Shakers**: Light, airy, constant 16ths (6–12 kHz)
- **Talking drum**: Pitched, melodic percussion (200–800 Hz)
- **Bongos**: Higher-pitched fills (500 Hz–2 kHz)
- **Processing**: Group bus with room reverb, light compression

#### Kalimba / Marimba
- **Character**: Metallic, percussive, tuned — organic melody carrier
- **Frequency**: 500 Hz–4 kHz (bright, present)
- **Processing**: Light reverb, stereo widening on delays
- **Role**: Primary melodic element in many tracks
- **Pattern**: Repetitive interlocking patterns (African influence)

#### Sub Bass
- **Character**: Deep, simple, sine-wave-focused
- **Synthesis**: Sine or triangle wave, minimal harmonic content
- **Frequency**: 40–80 Hz fundamental
- **Processing**: Sidechain to kick (4–6 dB), mono, tight envelope
- **Pattern**: Simple — 1–2 notes, repetitive, groove-locked

#### Atmospheric Pads
- **Character**: Dark, spiritual, evolving
- **Synthesis**: Granular or wavetable — complex shifting timbres
- **Processing**: Long reverb (5+ seconds), subtle modulation
- **Role**: Background atmosphere, spiritual depth
- **Frequency**: 200 Hz–2 kHz (wide, diffuse)

## Jackin House

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Punchy, round, 909-style — drives the jack
- **Frequency**: Sub at 55 Hz, body at 100 Hz, moderate click at 3 kHz
- **Length**: Short-medium (tight for grooves)
- **Processing**: Minimal — raw drum machine character
- **Source**: 909 samples or faithful recreations

#### Sampled Loops
- **Character**: Lifted from disco/funk/soul records — raw, unpolished
- **Source**: Vinyl chops (bass loops, vocal hooks, string stabs)
- **Processing**: High-pass at 100–200 Hz, filtering, saturation
- **Frequency**: Variable (depends on source material)
- **Role**: Primary musical content — sample IS the track

#### Hi-Hats (Chicago Style)
- **Character**: Bright, open on offbeats — Chicago house DNA
- **Source**: 909 open/closed hat
- **Processing**: Minimal — raw machine output
- **Pattern**: Swung 16ths with open on every &
- **Frequency**: 6–12 kHz

#### Bass (Short, Punchy)
- **Character**: Short hits, syncopated — not sustained lines
- **Synthesis**: Simple saw/square, fast decay envelope
- **Frequency**: 60–150 Hz (punchy mid-bass)
- **Processing**: Saturation, short reverb, sidechain to kick
- **Pattern**: Syncopated against kick — creates the "jack" motion

## Soulful House

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Warm, acoustic-feeling, round — band aesthetic
- **Frequency**: Fundamental 50–55 Hz, warm body 80–150 Hz
- **Length**: Medium
- **Processing**: Gentle compression, room reverb for "live" feel
- **Source**: Acoustic drum samples or warm 909 variants

#### Vocals (Core Element)
- **Character**: Full soulful performance — gospel/R&B influence
- **Processing**: Compression (3–6 dB GR), de-essing, plate reverb
- **Frequency**: 200 Hz–8 kHz (full vocal range)
- **Style**: Runs, melisma, ad-libs, harmonized stacks
- **Position**: ON TOP of mix — vocal IS the focus

#### Rhodes / Wurlitzer
- **Character**: Warm, chorused, jazz voicings
- **Synthesis**: Keyscape, Lounge Lizard, Arturia Wurli
- **Voicings**: Full jazz chords (9ths, 11ths, 13ths)
- **Processing**: Chorus, tremolo, subtle overdrive, plate reverb
- **Role**: Harmonic foundation, fills between vocal phrases

#### Live Bass
- **Character**: Warm, round, fingerstyle bass guitar
- **Source**: Trilian, Scarbee, or live recording
- **Frequency**: 60–200 Hz (warm, not subby)
- **Processing**: Light compression, gentle saturation
- **Pattern**: Walking bass with chromatic passing tones

#### Strings
- **Character**: Lush, sustained pad arrangements
- **Source**: String libraries (Spitfire, CSS) or synth string pads
- **Processing**: Long reverb, gentle EQ roll-off above 8 kHz
- **Role**: Emotional depth, supporting vocal
- **Voicings**: Full orchestral arrangements (violins, violas, cellos)

## UK Garage

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Deep, sub-heavy — NOT the standard 909 four-on-the-floor
- **Frequency**: Heavy sub at 40–50 Hz, body at 80–100 Hz
- **Length**: Medium-short (tight)
- **Processing**: Layered (sub sine + body sample)
- **Pattern**: 2-step placement (syncopated, not on every beat)

#### Sub Bass
- **Character**: Massive, deep, powerful — sine wave dominance
- **Frequency**: 30–60 Hz fundamental (very deep)
- **Processing**: Mono, clean, envelope-shaped, pitch-bend automation
- **Movement**: Pitch bends, slides, and wobbles for expression
- **Pattern**: Syncopated with 2-step kick, not on every beat

#### Reese Bass
- **Character**: Dark, detuned sawtooth — growling, filtered
- **Synthesis**: 2–3 detuned saw waves, low-pass filtered
- **Processing**: Light chorus, dark reverb, filter modulation
- **Role**: Mid-bass texture (200–500 Hz), layered above sub
- **Usage**: Stabs and fills, not constant

#### Chopped Vocals
- **Character**: Fragmented syllables, pitched up, rhythmic
- **Processing**: Time-stretch, pitch-shift, reverse, granular
- **Frequency**: 500 Hz–5 kHz (present, cutting)
- **Technique**: Todd Edwards "micro-sampling" — tiny fragments form melody
- **Role**: Primary melodic hook in many tracks

#### Super Saw Pads
- **Character**: Thick, wide, JP-8000-inspired
- **Synthesis**: 5–7 detuned saw waves (super saw)
- **Processing**: Chorus, long reverb, sidechain to kick
- **Frequency**: 200 Hz–4 kHz (wide, lush)
- **Role**: Atmospheric pad, breakdown element

#### Orchestral Stabs
- **Character**: Dramatic hits (Korg Triton/M1 presets)
- **Processing**: Reverb, subtle delay
- **Usage**: Energy lifts, drop accents
- **Frequency**: 200 Hz–6 kHz (full, powerful)

## Electro House

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Heavy, punchy, layered — maximum impact on drops
- **Frequency**: Sub at 50 Hz, body at 100–150 Hz, hard click at 4–6 kHz
- **Length**: Short (tight, punchy)
- **Processing**: Transient shaping, clipping, parallel compression
- **Source**: Layered: sine sub + 909 body + click/noise top

#### Supersaw Lead
- **Character**: Wide, huge, anthem-level — 5–7 voices detuned
- **Synthesis**: Supersaw (Sylenth1, Serum) — heavy unison, spread
- **Processing**: OTT/multiband compression, sidechain, EQ sculpting
- **Frequency**: 200 Hz–8 kHz (fills the frequency spectrum)
- **Role**: DROP melody — the main hook of the track
- **Movement**: Filter automation (closed in builds, open on drops)

#### Distorted Bass
- **Character**: Aggressive, distorted, mid-heavy — NOT clean sub
- **Synthesis**: Saw waves through heavy distortion chains
- **Processing**: Waveshaping, saturation, multiband processing
- **Frequency**: 60–300 Hz (sub extends into mid with distortion)
- **Role**: Energy, aggression, physical impact
- **Movement**: Pitch bends, glides, LFO modulation

#### Build Elements
- **Risers**: White noise sweep (rising over 8–16 bars)
- **Snare rolls**: Accelerating (8ths → 16ths → 32nds)
- **Reverb swells**: Reversed reverb tails building tension
- **Processing**: Increasing volume, filter opening, compression
- **Impact**: Sub drop + layered hit on beat 1 of drop

#### FX Layers
- **Impacts**: Layered sub + noise + reverb tail hits
- **Transitions**: Sweeps, zaps, risers, downlifters
- **Processing**: Heavy reverb, delay, granular
- **Role**: Section markers, energy management

## Bass House

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Tight, punchy, 909-based — clean to let bass dominate
- **Frequency**: Sub at 50 Hz, click at 3–5 kHz, tight body
- **Length**: Short (tight — bass needs the sub space)
- **Processing**: HPF sub cut below 40 Hz, transient enhancement
- **Source**: 909-based, tight and clean

#### Wobble Bass (Primary Hook)
- **Character**: Moving, modulated, "wobbly" — bass IS the melody
- **Synthesis**: Wavetable (Serum) — LFO on cutoff creates wobble
- **Processing**: OTT multiband compression, distortion, sidechain
- **Layers**: Sub (clean sine, 30–60 Hz) + Mid (wobble, 80–500 Hz) + Top (presence, 500 Hz–3 kHz)
- **Movement**: LFO rate changes (1/4, 1/8, triplet) for rhythmic variation
- **Resampling**: Bounce and re-process for unique character

#### G-House Vocal Samples
- **Character**: Pitched-down rap/vocal samples — attitude, swagger
- **Processing**: Pitch down 2–4 semitones, compression, subtle reverb
- **Frequency**: 100–300 Hz (low, dark character)
- **Role**: Vibe/attitude setting, not lyrical content
- **Usage**: Intro, breakdowns, between drops

#### Sub Bass (Clean Layer)
- **Character**: Pure sine, clean, deep — foundation underneath wobble
- **Frequency**: 30–60 Hz (pure sub)
- **Processing**: Mono, sidechain to kick, high-pass at 25 Hz
- **Role**: Low-end weight that speakers can reproduce
- **Separation**: High-pass the wobble at 80 Hz, sub fills below

#### Percussion
- **Character**: Simple, supportive — NOT complex like tech house
- **Hats**: 8th or 16th notes, straight, minimal variation
- **Clap**: On 2 and 4, tight, short
- **Processing**: Minimal — drums support, bass dominates
- **Role**: Time-keeping reference, not groove-defining

## Chicago House

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum (TR-707/808/909)
- **Character**: Raw, unprocessed drum machine output
- **TR-707**: Crisper, more hi-fi, lighter sub — characteristic early Chicago
- **TR-808**: Deeper sub, more boom, rounder body
- **TR-909**: Punchier, more sub weight, metallic hat character
- **Processing**: NONE (or minimal tape saturation from recording)
- **Role**: Steady pulse — never varies, never stops

#### Open Hi-Hat (Signature Sound)
- **Character**: Metallic, consistent, on every offbeat
- **Source**: 707, 808, or 909 open hat (each has distinct character)
- **Processing**: None — raw drum machine output
- **Pattern**: EVERY offbeat — the defining sound of Chicago house
- **Frequency**: 5–12 kHz

#### Synth Bass
- **Character**: Simple, deep, repetitive — Juno-106 or SH-101
- **Synthesis**: Saw or square wave, single oscillator, low-pass filtered
- **Frequency**: 50–150 Hz
- **Processing**: Minimal — raw synth output
- **Pattern**: Simple root note, repetitive, groove-locked

#### Organ Stabs
- **Character**: Church organ or B3 Hammond style
- **Voicings**: Simple triads, offbeat stabs
- **Processing**: Raw (or slight overdrive for B3 character)
- **Role**: Harmonic content, gospel/spiritual flavor
- **Frequency**: 200 Hz–3 kHz

#### Vocal Chants
- **Character**: Raw, unprocessed, repetitive — "jack your body" style
- **Recording**: Basic microphone, minimal treatment
- **Processing**: Compression for consistency, that's all
- **Style**: Rhythmic chanting, preacher-style, hypnotic repetition
- **Role**: Primary hook — vocal IS the track in many Chicago house records

## Ghetto House

### Signature Sounds

#### 808 Kick
- **Character**: Heavy, aggressive, fast-tempo-appropriate
- **Frequency**: Sub at 45–60 Hz, click at 2–4 kHz
- **Length**: Short (fast tempo needs quick decay)
- **Processing**: Clipping, saturation for aggression
- **Source**: TR-808 or faithful sample

#### 808 Toms
- **Character**: Pitched, aggressive, used for fills and rolls
- **Frequency**: 80–400 Hz (high, mid, low toms)
- **Processing**: None or slight distortion
- **Role**: Fills, accents, rolls leading into sections
- **Pattern**: Rapid rolls (16ths or triplets)

#### Hi-Hats
- **Character**: Fast, constant, machine-gun 16ths
- **Source**: 808 closed hat
- **Processing**: None
- **Frequency**: 8–12 kHz (bright, cutting)
- **Pattern**: Constant 16ths at 125–135 BPM (very fast)

#### Cowbell
- **Character**: Metallic, constant — Dance Mania signature
- **Source**: 808 cowbell
- **Pattern**: 8th notes or offbeat accents
- **Processing**: None
- **Role**: Adds driving metallic energy

#### Vocal Samples (THE Element)
- **Character**: Explicit, repetitive, raw, rapid-fire chanting
- **Recording**: Basic quality — lo-fi is genre-appropriate
- **Processing**: Compression for loudness, that's all
- **Style**: Short phrases (2–4 words) looped every 2–4 bars
- **Role**: THE PRIMARY ELEMENT — defines the track

## UK Funky

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Deep, UK bass weight — sub-heavy
- **Frequency**: Heavy sub at 40–55 Hz, body at 80–100 Hz
- **Length**: Medium
- **Processing**: Layered sub + body, slight saturation
- **Pattern**: Syncopated (not four-on-the-floor)

#### Tribal Percussion
- **Congas**: Warm, multiple articulations, panned
- **Bongos**: Higher-pitched, fills and accents
- **Shakers**: Light, airy, providing groove thread
- **Woodblocks**: Sharp, percussive accent hits
- **Processing**: Room reverb for cohesion, group compression

#### Sub Bass
- **Character**: Deep, powerful UK bass (30–60 Hz)
- **Synthesis**: Sine/triangle sub with envelope shaping
- **Processing**: Mono, clean, pitch-bend automation
- **Movement**: Wobble, slides, drops
- **Role**: Major energy element — enters mid-track for step-up

#### Synth Hooks
- **Character**: Short, percussive, syncopated riffs
- **Synthesis**: Simple waveforms, fast envelopes
- **Processing**: Reverb throw, filter modulation
- **Role**: Hook element — rhythmic, not melodic complexity
- **Frequency**: 300 Hz–3 kHz

#### R&B Vocal Chops
- **Character**: Pitched, time-stretched, fragmented R&B vocals
- **Processing**: Granular, pitch shift, reverse, stutter
- **Role**: Textural hook, rhythmic element
- **Frequency**: 500 Hz–5 kHz

## Gqom

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: HEAVY, dark, dominant — foundation of the genre
- **Frequency**: Very deep sub (30–50 Hz), dark body (60–100 Hz)
- **Length**: Medium-long (sub extends)
- **Processing**: Saturation, slight distortion, HPF at 25 Hz
- **Role**: Primary rhythmic AND tonal element

#### Drone Bass
- **Character**: Sustained, dark, static frequency — NOT melodic
- **Synthesis**: Sine wave at 40–60 Hz, sustained, minimal movement
- **Processing**: Distortion, heavy low-pass filter, mono
- **Role**: Provides dark tonal weight underneath percussion
- **Movement**: NONE (static drone) or very slow pitch shift

#### Industrial Percussion
- **Character**: Metallic, fractured, dark, sparse
- **Source**: Metal hits, industrial impacts, processed recordings
- **Processing**: Distortion, low-pass filter (dark), reverb (eerie)
- **Frequency**: 200 Hz–4 kHz (filtered dark)
- **Spacing**: SPARSE — silence between hits is essential

#### Dark Atmosphere
- **Character**: Eerie, unsettling ambient textures
- **Source**: Dark pads, reversed sounds, distorted field recordings
- **Processing**: Long dark reverb, distortion, heavy filtering
- **Role**: Creates the dark, hypnotic mood
- **Frequency**: 100 Hz–2 kHz (no brightness)

## French Touch

### Signature Sounds

#### Disco Samples (Core Material)
- **Character**: Chopped disco/funk loops — filtered, phased
- **Source**: 70s/80s disco vinyl records
- **Processing**: Low-pass filter (sweepable), phaser/flanger, compression
- **Frequency**: Full range (but filtered dynamically)
- **Role**: THE compositional element — track IS the sample manipulation

#### Pumping Sidechain (THE Signature)
- **Character**: Extreme rhythmic ducking on everything
- **Settings**: 8–12 dB reduction, fast attack (<1ms), medium release (100–200ms)
- **Trigger**: Kick drum
- **Applied to**: EVERYTHING (pads, bass, samples, vocals)
- **Result**: Creates iconic "breathing" rhythmic pump

#### Filtered Bass
- **Character**: Funky, disco-influenced — filtered and pumping
- **Synthesis**: Saw bass or sampled disco bass
- **Processing**: Extreme sidechain, filter automation
- **Frequency**: 60–200 Hz (revealed through filter sweep)
- **Movement**: Filter opens/closes with arrangement

#### Phased/Flanged Elements
- **Character**: Moving, swirling disco textures
- **Processing**: MXR Phase 90 (or emulation), flanger, chorus
- **Applied to**: Guitar samples, string loops, vocal chops
- **Role**: Movement, disco authenticity, psychedelic quality
- **Rate**: Slow (1–4 Hz) for lush movement

## Lo-Fi House / Outsider House

### Signature Sounds

#### Degraded Drum Machine
- **Character**: Classic house drums through tape/degradation
- **Source**: TR-606, TR-707, TR-808 (or Volca equivalents)
- **Processing**: Tape saturation, bit-crushing, vinyl emulation
- **Timing**: Slightly unquantized (±5–10ms from grid)
- **Result**: Classic patterns but warm/fuzzy/imprecise

#### Tape-Warbled Chords
- **Character**: Sampled or synthesized chords through tape warble
- **Source**: Jazz/soul vinyl samples OR synth chords
- **Processing**: Cassette tape recording (or RC-20/Vinyl plugin), heavy low-pass filter
- **Movement**: Tape speed instability creates pitch wobble
- **Frequency**: 100–2 kHz (muffled, warm — high frequencies rolled off)

#### Saturated Bass
- **Character**: Deep but distorted — warmth from saturation
- **Synthesis**: Simple sine/saw with heavy tape/tube saturation
- **Processing**: Tape compression, tube saturation, low-pass filter
- **Frequency**: 40–150 Hz (distortion adds harmonics above)
- **Role**: Deep but textured — not clean sub

#### Vinyl Noise / Tape Hiss
- **Character**: Constant background texture
- **Source**: Actual vinyl surface noise, tape hiss, or emulated
- **Processing**: Part of the master bus texture (not removed)
- **Role**: Atmosphere, warmth, "bedroom" intimacy
- **Level**: Audible but not dominant (-15 to -20 dB below music)

## Future Garage

### Signature Sounds

#### Kick Drum
- **Character**: Soft, deep, distant — NOT aggressive or punchy
- **Frequency**: Sub at 40–55 Hz, soft body (no click)
- **Length**: Medium-long (soft tail fading out)
- **Processing**: Low-pass filter, reverb (distant placement)
- **Role**: Subtle rhythmic marker (not dominant)

#### Reese Bass
- **Character**: Warm, dark, filtered detuned sawtooths
- **Synthesis**: 2–3 saw waves detuned 5–15 cents, heavy low-pass filter
- **Processing**: Chorus (subtle), dark reverb, filter automation
- **Frequency**: 40–200 Hz (deep, warm, moving slowly)
- **Movement**: Very slow filter modulation (over 8–16 bars)
- **Character**: NOT aggressive — warm, enveloping, cinematic

#### Pitched Vocal Chops (SIGNATURE)
- **Character**: Fragments of vocals — pitched, reversed, granulated
- **Processing**: Pitch-shift (up or down), granular synthesis, heavy reverb
- **Frequency**: 300 Hz–5 kHz (present but distant)
- **Role**: Emotional core — melancholic, beautiful fragments
- **Technique**: 1–3 syllable fragments looped with reverb trails

#### Vinyl Crackle
- **Character**: Constant textural bed — warm, intimate
- **Source**: Sampled vinyl surface noise (or iZotope Vinyl plugin)
- **Processing**: Low-pass filter (not too bright), subtle volume automation
- **Level**: Always present, subtle (-18 to -22 dB below music)
- **Role**: Creates intimacy, nocturnal atmosphere

#### Atmospheric Reverb (SIGNATURE)
- **Character**: Cavernous, long tails — 5–10 second decay
- **Type**: Hall or convolution (large spaces — cathedral, warehouse)
- **Processing**: High-pass reverb return at 200 Hz, low-pass at 6 kHz
- **Applied to**: Vocals, snares, synth stabs — creates depth
- **Role**: Defines the "space" — cinematic, immersive

#### Field Recordings
- **Character**: Rain, urban ambience, static, distant sounds
- **Processing**: Low-pass filter, reverb, volume automation
- **Role**: Atmospheric texture, world-building
- **Placement**: Enters/exits through arrangement (not constant)
- **Level**: Subtle, background-level
