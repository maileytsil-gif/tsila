## Inter-plugin communication

### Fill out your iZotope plug-in ecosystem 
 VocalSynth 2 is part of iZotope’s collection of software with Inter-plugin Communication capabilities. By placing VocalSynth 2 on a track in your session, the plug-in can interact with instances of Neutron 5, Visual Mixer, Ozone 8, Tonal Balance Control, Insight 2, Relay, and Nectar 4 for numerous automated mixing and mastering processes.

## Auto mode

 Auto modeLock your vocals into key with real-time pitch correction, generate additional voices and harmonies, and seamlessly blend your module settings with VocalSynth’s intuitive mix panel.

## MIDI mode

 MIDI modeUse MIDI data to control your pitch and effortlessly create custom vocal harmonies as you play. Recreate the experience of playing a classic vocoder or talkbox!

## Sidechain mode

 Sidechain modePipe your own audio into VocalSynth 2 to use as your “carrier” signal. This lets you make any audio “talk” or create new outlandish sounds that would be impossible otherwise.

 VocalSynth 2 FAQ

 What is iZotope VocalSynth 2?

 VocalSynth 2 is a creative vocal synthesizer plugin that combines five powerful modules to create electronic vocal textures, robot voices, and computerized harmonies. It uses advanced processing to transform your voice into a unique instrument.

 Who is VocalSynth 2 for?

 VocalSynth 2 is for any creator on their production journey who wants to push the boundaries of vocal sound. It is ideal for electronic music producers, sound designers, and artists looking for iconic vocoder or talkbox effects.

 What are the five main engines in VocalSynth 2?

 VocalSynth 2 features five distinct engines: Biovox, Vocoder, Talkbox, Polyvox, and Compuvox. These can be blended and layered to create a vast range of synthetic and organic vocal textures.

 Does VocalSynth 2 support MIDI control?

 Yes, VocalSynth 2 features a MIDI Mode that allows you to play harmonies and melodies using your voice as the oscillator. This allows for expressive, playable vocal performances driven by your MIDI controller.
