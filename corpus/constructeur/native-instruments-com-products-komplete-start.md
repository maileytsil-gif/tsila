 Komplete Start includes Kontakt Player – the streamlined version of the industry’s powerhouse sampler and virtual instrument platform. It’s your gateway to thousands of sample libraries from both NI and leading-developers. Plus, you can kickstart your ideas with a free sampler and a layer of creative tools.

## The sampler that plays how you think

 A fast, flexible sampler built into Kontakt Player.

## Tools that help you build smarter

 Whether you’re building chord progressions, sketching melodies, or flipping loops into full tracks, Kontakt Player has nine tools made to accelerate your process.

### Chords Tool

 Generate harmonic progressions in one click.  
 The Chords Tool lets you trigger full chord sets with a single press – perfect for writing fast, exploring new inversions, or playing with progressions you wouldn’t think up yourself. Choose from a wide range of modes, customize voicings, humanize timing, and instantly create harmonies.
 
 [play type="video" url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BFBWadZxmuw" label="Watch demo"]

### Phrases Tool

 Instant melodies, filtered by genre and feel.  
 The Phrases Tool helps you explore musical ideas fast – just choose a style, play a note, and hear the result. With tempo-syncing, scale locking, and expressive variation built in, it’s a powerful way to move from idea to structure.
 
 [play type="video" url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RZ6scYWOJ_Q" label="Watch demo"]

### Patterns Tool

 Trigger customizable melodic patterns in real time.  
 The Patterns Tool is a polyphonic step sequencer and arpeggiator that lets you create dynamic musical phrases at the press of a key. With editable steps, scale locking, and over 40 included patterns, it’s perfect for crafting evolving melodies, chord runs, and rhythmic textures.
 
 [play type="video" url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fOa3NjYrxWo" label="Watch demo"]
