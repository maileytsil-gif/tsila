## What’s new in Maschine 3?

### Stem separation

 Import any track and Maschine will identify bass, drums, vocals, and other elements at the click of the button. Stem separation opens up a whole world of sampling possibilities, all powered by iZotope’s legendary RX technology.

### Tempo per scene

 Set custom tempos per scene to shape the energy in your arrangement with ease. Perfect for complex sound design and dramatic flows.

### Redesigned Modulation Editor

 Make fluid edits with expressive control thanks to smoother visuals and powerful new creative tools. Draw curves, sketch modulation, and edit multiple points at once with DAW-level precision.

### Modulation in the Arranger

 Fine-tune your tracks automation directly in the arranger view, using powerful new tools like curves, grid-snapping, drag-to-edit, marquee select, and updated shortcuts.

### User chords

 Easily create, save, and tweak your own chord sets right from your Maschine hardware. Explore unique harmonic structures, tailor your sound to genres, and much more.

### Play Series instruments on Maschine+

 Maschine 3 brings next-level standalone production to life - now with compatibility for a selection of Play Series instruments and effects on Maschine+.
 
 Outstanding sound quality. Easy-to-use instruments. Each with their own distinctive sound and character, heaps of pro presets, and focused controls. A high-speed SD card is recommended for the best standalone experience.

## How it works

 Sketch and arrange your ideas, use virtual instruments or samples to develop your track, mix it, and get it ready to share with the world – all in one place.

### Built-in instruments & effects

 In addition to the Maschine Central sound library, Maschine 3 comes with a complete set of built-in instruments and effects. From Drum Synths, Bass Synth, and Poly Synth to a versatile range of compressors, reverbs, delays, and modulation tools, everything you need to create and polish your sound is built right in, no extra plugins required to start out.

### The Maschine browsing experience

 Quickly find instruments, sounds, and samples with the tag-based browser in Maschine 3.
 
 Browse, preview, and load content without searching through long lists. Stay focused on creating instead of menu-diving.

### Explore and experiment in the Ideas view

 Collect patterns, test out arrangements, plan performances, and experiment in the Ideas view before committing to a final structure.

### Start arranging

 Build patterns into tracks, arrange audio, mix levels, and fine-tune your track in Song mode. This is where those first ideas become cohesive tracks.

### Go deeper

 There’s always more to explore as you develop your sound. Maschine lets you route different effects chains, bring in external instruments, and create your own sounds from scratch using its built-in synths and effects.

### Add any sound

 With thousands of authentic virtual instruments from NI and our third-party developers you’ll find whatever you need. Plus, Expansions offer incredible quality sound packs, tailored for a huge range of different genres.

## No hardware needed

 It just got easier to create a track from start to finish without additional gear, thanks to Maschine 3’s new and improved mouse MIDI editing tools. These include split, join, erase, mute, MIDI notes, keyboard shortcuts, computer keyboard, audible MIDI note previews, and more.

 System Requirements

 System requirements

 Mac macOS 13, 14, 15 (latest update)
 
 Win Windows 10 (64-bit, latest Service Pack) or Windows 11 (latest Service Pack)
 
 Intel Core i5 or equivalent X86 CPU, or Apple Silicon (native mode or using Rosetta 2), 4 GB RAM (8 GB recommended)

 Additional Requirements 
 9 GB free disk space for Maschine software
 USB 2.0 compatible port for using compatible Maschine and Kontrol Hardware
 An internet connection is required to download and activate Maschine software. Once installed and activated, all products can be used offline
 Graphics hardware support for Direct 3D 11.1 (Feature Level 11_0) or higher on Windows-based systems
 Supported Interfaces: Stand-alone, VST3, Audio Units (64-bit), AAX 64, ASIO, CoreAudio, and WASAPI.

 Compatibility 
 Maschine 3 functions without hardware but also works with the following controllers: Maschine Mikro MK3, Maschine MK3, Maschine Studio, and Maschine+ in controller and standalone mode
 Maschine Jam is supported by Maschine 3 but without an update to the browser interface
