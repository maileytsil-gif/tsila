# Brass Quartet

### by Spitfire Audio 
 Feature a rich blend of trumpet, flugelhorn, tenor horn and trombone in your music. The included instruments highlight the natural breathiness, range of expression and broad tonality of a brass quartet. Created in collaboration with Spitfire Audio.

 This collection of realistic multisampled instruments gives you access to the warmth and natural fluctuation of a small brass ensemble. Close-recorded for intimacy and detail, these blended horns can bring a rounded organic quality to productions when treated naturally, or give off a harsh metallic timbre when oversaturated. 
 Work with a variety of note lengths, dynamics, tones and performance techniques including staccatissimo, vibrato, pitch bend, hollow and flutter. Use these playing styles individually, or blend them and change them up on the fly using the technique Macro on the all-in-one instruments. 
 The Pack also includes custom Max for Live devices for added realism, most notably with delicately tuned crossfade curves for the all-in-one instruments. Plus, a selection of MPE presets offers expressive articulation, letting you change each note’s technique independently using an MPE controller. 
 A selection of MIDI clips in different melodic styles and chord progressions can provide inspiration, start you on a creative path, or serve as the basis for experimentation. 
 Watch demo videos and find in-depth manuals for the Brass Quartet, String Quartet and Upright Piano Packs on the Spitfire Audio website .

## Spitfire Audio
 Spitfire Audio is a world leader in instrumental multisampling. The company, which started as a collection of string samples recorded for the founders’ own compositions, has released over 60 sample libraries which can be heard in major Hollywood film scores and hit records.
