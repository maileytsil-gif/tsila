### Welcome

 Welcome to the Serum 2 User Guide . Serum 2 is an advanced virtual synthesizer that offers a unique blend of sound generation methods together with a powerful modulation system.

 Note: Throughout this manual, we’ll refer to Serum 2 (the latest version of Serum) simply as Serum.

 Serum combines wavetable, subtractive, multi-sampled, sampled, granular, and spectral synthesis into an intuitive and cohesive workflow. This manual helps you get the most out of your instrument.
