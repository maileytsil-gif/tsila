 This is session 14 of the YouTube Series with Francois PML called "Producer Notes". In Episode 014 we are  analysing the Melodic Afro House style of artists "keinemusik", Rampa, &Me and to a certain degree also "Moderat". 

 Watch and see what we can learn from this style sitting somewhere between Percussive House and Afro House. 

 The   key lessons   from this episode are:

 Main elements of this sound: 

 Clean low-end with Toms for groove 

 Shakers for groove and timing 

 One tonal Percussive Element is mixed louder and can be part of the Melody (Here: Bells and glass sounds) 

 Savannah-type element for vibe: whoop, elephant type sounds 

 Piano for harmonic core 

 Trippy, uplifting vocals
