## Summary

This document records the failed attempt to generate a working big band arrangement of **Beatrice (Sam Rivers)** using iterative AI-generated orchestration prompts.

Despite multiple regeneration passes (V1 → V16), none of the outputs produced a musically usable chart. The process consumed significant time and resulted in arrangements that were structurally unstable, orchestrationally incorrect, and often unplayable.

The decision has therefore been made to **abandon AI generation for the orchestration stage** and return to **manual scoring in Sibelius**, using Bora's opening bars as the structural foundation.

---

## Initial Goal

Create a **3-chorus big band arrangement** of Beatrice that could:

- align with Toshi Clinch's arranging course
- be technically solid enough to show Bora
- potentially be shown to Dan's London big band

The workflow was intended to be:

1. Create reharmonised **piano lead sheet**
2. Expand into **big band arrangement**
3. Refine orchestration
4. Reach **GCE ≥ 9**

---

## What Actually Happened

The process devolved into repeated regeneration cycles.

Each prompt attempted to "fix" the score but typically introduced new problems.

Common issues:

- runaway piano comping loops
- density explosions in the B section
- instruments written outside playable range
- no clear sectional architecture
- counterlines colliding with melody
- all sections playing simultaneously
- rhythm section overpowering the horns
- "shout chorus" without structural preparation

Listening to the rendered scores revealed the real problem:  
**they were not musically coherent.**

---

## Versions Attempted

V1 – Initial orchestration attempt  
V2 – Range corrections  
V3 – Density reduction attempt  
V4 – Reharm lead sheet creation  
V5–V8 – Counterline and orchestration experiments  
V9 – Architecture revision  
V10 – Added sax soli / shout chorus concept  
V11 – Range validation  
V12 – XML export correction for evaluation  
V13 – Counterpoint insertion  
V14 – Structural rewrite attempt  
V15 – Full orchestration pass (failed B section)  
V16 – Attempted repair of orchestration density

None of these versions produced a score that could be considered performance-ready.

---

## Core Lesson

AI systems can assist with:

- harmonic exploration
- reharmonisation ideas
- structural suggestions
- analysis

But **big band orchestration requires human musical judgement** regarding:

- density
- register balance
- ensemble roles
- rhythmic space
- phrase shaping

These elements repeatedly broke down in generated scores.

---

## Current Decision

The arrangement will now proceed using the following approach:

1. Keep **Bora's first 12 bars exactly as written**
2. Use the **reharmonised lead sheet as harmonic reference**
3. Continue orchestrating **manually in Sibelius**
4. Use AI only for:
   - theoretical consultation
   - orchestration analysis
   - arranging references

---

## Final Status

AI-generated orchestration attempts are **archived but abandoned**.

The working chart will now be **hand-written from bar 13 onward**.

This document exists as a record of the attempt and as a reminder that **musical listening and judgement must remain the primary authority in arranging work.**
