# Modern Big Band Arranging — Books

List of influential modern arranging texts with short summaries.

---

## Core Texts

| Title | Author | Summary |
|-------|--------|---------|
| *The Complete Arranger* | Sammy Nestico | Standard reference for big band voicings, section writing, and scoring. Covers 4-part and 5-part writing, brass and sax balance, and practical chart layout. |
| *The Complete Arranger-Composer* | Russell Garcia | Orchestration and colour. Emphasis on instrumental combinations, timbre, and arranging for film and studio. |
| *Inside the Score* | Rayburn Wright | Analytical approach to classic big band scores. Deconstructs Thad Jones, Bob Brookmeyer, and others. |
| *Modern Jazz Voicings* | Ted Pease, Ken Pullig | Jazz harmony and voicings. Drop-2, drop-3, upper-structure triads. Berklee-based. |

---

## Reharmonisation

| Title | Author | Summary |
|-------|--------|---------|
| *The Jazz Theory Book* | Mark Levine | Chord-scale theory, reharmonisation techniques, modal interchange. Widely used in jazz education. |
| *Reharmonization Techniques* | Randy Felts | Jazz reharm methods. Tritone substitution, chord substitution, passing chords. |

---

## Big Band Specific

| Title | Author | Summary |
|-------|--------|---------|
| *Arranging for Large Jazz Ensemble* | Various | Section writing, balance, and ensemble texture. Covers sax, brass, and rhythm section roles. |
| *The Study of Orchestration* | Adler | General orchestration. Reference for ranges, techniques, and scoring across instruments. |
