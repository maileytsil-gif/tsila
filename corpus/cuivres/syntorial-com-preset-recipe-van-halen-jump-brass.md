 Remaking the brass synth from the 1984 song "Jump" by Van Halen can be an exciting challenge for any synthesizer enthusiast. Here’s how you can create the "Jump" brass patch and play this iconic sound on most hardware or software subtractive synthesizers (Serum, Vital, Pigments, Prophet, Korg, Moog, etc). We've included the preset download directly for your convenience, but we strongly recommend you use our programming tutorial to recreate it yourself.

## 
 Original Audio

 Van Halen’s “Jump” is renowned for its iconic brass synthesizer sound, which dominates the track from the beginning to the end. The sound is characterized by a bright, piercing, fat quality achieved through the use of detuned sawtooth waveforms.
