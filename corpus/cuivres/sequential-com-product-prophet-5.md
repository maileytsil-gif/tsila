# Legendary analog polysynth 

## A timeless classic

 The Prophet-5 is revered by artists for its unique and influential sound that has shaped music for decades. Introduced in 1978 as the world’s first fully programmable polyphonic synthesizer, it revolutionized music production by enabling musicians to instantly save and recall sounds. Today’s Prophet-5 Rev4 remains faithful to the original design, recreating the analog circuit ry that produces its rich warm tone, while still adding a few new twists that modern musicians love.

 The Prophet-5 Rev1 and Rev2 featured SSM filters that were rich in character but could be temperamental, while the Rev3 switched to the Curtis filter for more stability and control. Today, reissues of both analog filter chips let you experience their unique qualities without worrying about reliability. The Rev4 offers the best of both worlds, allowing you to choose your ideal Prophet-5 sound.

## Origin story

 Dave Smith, the creator of the Prophet-5 and founder of Sequential, is no longer with us. But he shared the story of the creation of this era-defining product in this video. Powered by microprocessor control, the Prophet-5 was the first synthesizer that could save and recall sounds—an unprecedented feature at the time. This innovation combined with five-note polyphony transformed the landscape of electronic music, and made the Prophet-5 a model for nearly every synthesizer to come.

## Poly Mod

 With Poly Mod, you can easily explore exponential FM, extreme filter effects, and pulse width modulation by routing its sources to destinations like oscillator and filter frequencies. This simple yet versatile system continues to yield exciting new sonic discoveries.

## LFO

 The Prophet-5 features a global LFO that can be brought in using the mod wheel. Additionally, Oscillator B can be set to low frequency mode, allowing for per-voice LFO modulations, yet it can also produce a range of gritty timbral effects when set to pitched oscillator frequencies. 

## Wheel Mod

 Wheel Mod allows you to quickly assign modulation routes to the mod wheel where you can use them in performance.

## Wheels

 Prophet-5 offers a spring-loaded pitch wheel with selectable range per program, while the mod wheel can be easily set to a number of expressive modulations using the Wheel Mod section.

## Oscillators

 The iconic tone of the Prophet-5 oscillators comes from its simultaneously selectable wave shapes—sawtooth, pulse, and triangle—along with pulse width modulation, and also including the unmistakable hard sync sound, famously used in ‘Let’s Go’ by The Cars.

## Glide & Unison

 Glide Rate allows you to create smooth transitions between notes, adding a distinct, flowing character to bass lines, leads, and pads, as heard throughout electronic music, hip-hop, and beyond. 
 Unison mode transforms the Prophet-5 into a powerful monophonic synthesizer, allowing you to stack up to five voices on one note for a truly massive analog sound. 

## Keyboard

 The Rev4 offers a premium 5-octave keyboard with velocity and aftertouch, adding new dynamics and expression to classic Prophet-5 sounds.

## Program Select & Globals

 The Prophet-5 is packed with 200 user and 200 factory programs , including the iconic original preset bank that fueled countless hit records.
 Global settings are now available from the top panel, giving you full command of your Prophet’s behavior.

## Filter

 The rich character of all three vintage versions, or “Revs” of the Prophet -5 is captured in the two switchable filters of the new Rev4 . Select “1/2” for the SSM filter and “3” for the Curtis sound .   

## Envelopes

 The behavior of the Envelopes is crucial for capturing the feel of vintage Prophet-5s, including the slight timing variations between voices, which can now be controlled using the Vintage knob.

## Velocity & Aftertouch

 V elocity and aftertouch add new dimensions to this revitalized Prophet . Applying  them to the filter and envelopes gives you enhanced dynamics and expression, redefining your experience of the legendary Prophet-5 sound.   

## MIDI

 The addition of MIDI connectivity ensures the Prophet-5 now integrates effortlessly into any modern studio setup.

## Inputs

 On the Prophet-5, the Release jack accepts a momentary footswitch to control sustain behavior, interacting with the Prophet’s distinctive Release switch. Meanwhile, there are two inputs for standard expression pedals: one to control volume, and the other for filter cutoff.

## Control Voltage

 The CV and Gate I/O allows the Prophet-5 to integrate with other analog gear, expanding its capabilities and allowing for more complex and unique sound possibilities.

## Outputs

 Staying true to the vintage Prophet-5, the Rev4 features a single, unbalanced ¼ inch audio output jack for mono signals. (However, we’ve broken with tradition by adding a headphone output.)
