## Engine Architecture

### Section 1 — Bars 13–24: Wheeler Lyric Engine

**Purpose:** Preserve melodic lyricism, warm harmonic colour, restrained orchestration.

**Writing approach:**
- **Bars 13–16:** Trumpet 1 melody (from V4.5 lead sheet), sax pad (drop-2 voicings), trombone harmonic support
- **Bars 17–24:** Continuation of Wheeler lyricism; trumpet melody, sax pad, trombone sustains
- Minimal density; no more than two sections playing simultaneously
- Register: middle (Chorus 1)

### Section 2 — Bars 25–32: Polyphonic Labyrinth + Counterpoint Engine

**Purpose:** Polyphonic texture, harmonic exploration, independent inner voices.

**Writing approach:**
- **Sax soli:** 5-part harmonised sax section (bars 25–32) with stepwise inner voice motion
- **Contrapuntal passage (bars 25–28):** Trumpet guide-tone counterline vs trombone independent line; different rhythms; contrary motion where possible
- Inner voices move stepwise; bebop-style passing tones
- Register: mid-high (Chorus 2)

### Section 3 — Bars 33–40: Shorter Narrative + Scofield Rhythm Engine

**Purpose:** Rhythmic lift, development, modern brass writing.

**Writing approach:**
- Trumpet 1 melody; Trumpet 2 punctuations on odd bars
- Sax backgrounds (drop-2 pads)
- Trombone punches on bars 33, 35, 37, 39
- More active rhythm section
- Register: upper brass (Chorus 3)

### Section 4 — Bars 41–48: Thad Jones Brass Architecture

**Purpose:** Explosive shout chorus, trumpet dominance, rhythmic brass writing.

**Writing approach:**
- Full brass (drop-2-4 voicings)
- Sax rhythmic figures (hits on bars 44, 46, 48)
- Trombone punches (bars 42, 44, 46)
- Ensemble hits at peak moments
- Rising trumpet register
