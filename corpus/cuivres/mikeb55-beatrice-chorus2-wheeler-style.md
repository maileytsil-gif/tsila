# Chorus 2 — Wheeler Lyric Style

**Strategy:** Kenny Wheeler–inspired; maj9, maj7#11, minor9; floating, luminous; Lydian colour.

## 16-Bar Chord Progression (iRealPro Style)

```
Fmaj9 | Abmaj7#11 | Fmaj9 | Ebmaj9 |
Dm9 | Ebmaj9 | Dm9 B7alt | Bbm9 |
Am9 | Bbmaj9 | Eø A7sus | Dm9 |
Gm9 | Abmaj7#11 | Fm9 | Gbmaj7 |
```

## Reharmonisation Logic

- Abmaj7#11 for Gbmaj7: enharmonic Lydian colour; #11 adds luminosity.
- maj9 and minor9 extensions throughout; Wheeler lyricism.
- B7alt as side-slip before Bbm9; chromatic approach.
- A7sus for softer dominant; avoids hard resolution.
- Ebmaj9, Bbmaj9: sustained, floating quality.

## Horn-Section Orchestration Possibilities

- Trumpets: open voicings; 4ths and 5ths; sustained pads.
- Saxes: melody in soprano or alto; lush 3-part harmonisation on long notes.
- Trombones: pedal or counter-melody; avoid dense blocks.
- Section contrast: full ensemble on A sections; reduced on bridge; Wheeler-style transparency.
