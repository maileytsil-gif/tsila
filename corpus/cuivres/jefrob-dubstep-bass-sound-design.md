# Sound Design Palette

## Riddim

### Signature Sounds

#### Metallic Bass Stab
- **Character**: Short, percussive, metallic — like a pitched anvil hit or transformer sound
- **Synthesis approach**: FM synthesis (2–3 operator) with fast pitch envelope down; or wavetable with metallic wavetable position
- **Frequency range**: 80–500 Hz fundamental, harmonics extending to 2–4 kHz
- **Processing**: Distortion/saturation, tight OTT compression, high-pass sidechain to kick
- **Role in mix**: PRIMARY element — this IS the track

#### Sub-Bass Layer
- **Character**: Clean, powerful sine-wave sub underneath the metallic stabs
- **Synthesis approach**: Pure sine wave or triangle with minimal harmonics
- **Frequency range**: 30–80 Hz (below the stab fundamental)
- **Processing**: Mono, sidechained to kick, light saturation for system translation
- **Role in mix**: Foundation — felt in chest on sound system, not heard on headphones

#### Vocal Chop / Sample
- **Character**: Short, pitched vocal sample used as rhythmic accent
- **Synthesis approach**: Sampled vocal, pitch-shifted, time-stretched
- **Frequency range**: 500 Hz–4 kHz (mid-range presence)
- **Processing**: Bitcrushing, formant shifting, reverb throws on specific hits
- **Role in mix**: Accent — punctuates between bass stabs for variety

#### Riddim Kick
- **Character**: Short, punchy, clicks through dense bass — attack-focused
- **Synthesis approach**: Layered: sine sub (pitch envelope) + click sample + body sample
- **Frequency range**: Sub at 50–60 Hz, click at 3–6 kHz, body at 100–200 Hz
- **Processing**: Transient shaping (maximum attack), short decay, parallel distortion
- **Role in mix**: Anchor — must cut through bass stabs without competing for low-end

#### Clap / Snare Layer
- **Character**: Aggressive, layered, cracking — maximum impact on beat 3
- **Synthesis approach**: White noise burst + clap sample + pitched-down snare
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–8 kHz (wide presence)
- **Processing**: Heavy compression, saturation, short plate reverb
- **Role in mix**: Anchor beat 3 — headbang timing reference

#### Riser/Transition FX
- **Character**: White noise sweep building tension before drop
- **Synthesis approach**: White noise through rising high-pass filter; pitch-ascending synth
- **Frequency range**: Sweeps from 200 Hz to 10+ kHz
- **Processing**: Increasing reverb, delay, stereo width as tension builds
- **Role in mix**: Transition tool — builds anticipation, signals drop incoming

## Melodic Dubstep

### Signature Sounds

#### Supersaw Lead
- **Character**: Massive, wide, emotional — wall of detuned saw waves
- **Synthesis approach**: 7+ unison voices, detuned ±20–40 cents, layered octaves
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–8 kHz (fills the entire mid-range)
- **Processing**: OTT compression, stereo widening, sidechain to kick, reverb
- **Role in mix**: PRIMARY drop element — carries the melody with power

#### Warm Sub-Bass
- **Character**: Warm, round, sustaining — musical and melodic
- **Synthesis approach**: Saw wave low-passed, or sine with gentle saturation
- **Frequency range**: 30–120 Hz
- **Processing**: Mono, sidechain to kick, light saturation for harmonics
- **Role in mix**: Foundation — follows chord roots, provides weight under supersaws

#### Cinematic Pad
- **Character**: Lush, evolving, atmospheric — creates emotional space
- **Synthesis approach**: Multiple detuned oscillators, slow LFO modulation, wavetable
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–6 kHz (wide, enveloping)
- **Processing**: Long reverb (3–5s), chorus, gentle high-pass at 200 Hz
- **Role in mix**: Atmosphere — fills breakdowns, creates emotional depth

#### Piano Layer
- **Character**: Intimate, emotional, acoustic — human touch in electronic context
- **Synthesis approach**: Sampled grand piano (Keyscape, Noire, Una Corda)
- **Frequency range**: 100 Hz–4 kHz
- **Processing**: Light reverb, gentle compression, EQ presence at 2–3 kHz
- **Role in mix**: Emotional anchor in breakdowns and intros

#### Vocal (Processed)
- **Character**: Ethereal, reverbed, emotional — the human heart of the track
- **Synthesis approach**: Recorded vocal, pitched, harmonized, effected
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–8 kHz (full vocal range)
- **Processing**: Reverb, delay throws, harmonizer, de-essing, compression
- **Role in mix**: Lead element in verses; counter-melody in drops

#### Build Riser
- **Character**: Massive, escalating, tension — white noise + pitch ascending synth
- **Synthesis approach**: Layered noise sweep + ascending pitch synth + snare roll
- **Frequency range**: Full spectrum sweep (100 Hz → 15 kHz over 8–16 bars)
- **Processing**: Increasing reverb/delay, widening stereo, automating volume up
- **Role in mix**: Critical transition device — defines the build-to-drop moment

## Brostep

### Signature Sounds

#### FM Growl Bass
- **Character**: Aggressive, evolving, mid-range focused — the "signature dubstep sound"
- **Synthesis approach**: FM synthesis (Serum, Vital) — modulator frequency automated via LFO
- **Frequency range**: 100 Hz–5 kHz (dominant mid-range presence)
- **Processing**: OTT, distortion, multiband compression, formant filter
- **Role in mix**: PRIMARY drop element — this IS brostep

#### Wobble Bass
- **Character**: Rhythmic, pulsing, LFO-driven — "wub-wub-wub"
- **Synthesis approach**: Saw/square oscillator with LFO on filter cutoff (rate = 1/4 to 1/16)
- **Frequency range**: 60–3 kHz (full bass to mid)
- **Processing**: Distortion, OTT, sidechain, formant filter automation
- **Role in mix**: Rhythmic bass layer — the LFO rate IS the groove

#### Scream/Yoi Bass
- **Character**: Vocal-like, screaming, formant-rich — sounds like a robot screaming
- **Synthesis approach**: Wavetable synthesis with formant tables; or FM with vocal formant processing
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–6 kHz (vocal formant range)
- **Processing**: Heavy distortion, formant filter (vowel shapes: A-E-I-O-U), OTT
- **Role in mix**: Lead bass variation — used for 8–16 bar sections within drops

#### Impact/Sub Drop
- **Character**: Massive low-end impact at drop entry — the "thud" that signals the drop
- **Synthesis approach**: Sine wave with fast downward pitch envelope (starts at 200 Hz, drops to 30 Hz)
- **Frequency range**: 30–200 Hz (sweeps down)
- **Processing**: Reverb, saturation, mono, limiter
- **Role in mix**: Transition marker — marks the exact moment the drop hits

#### Snare (Layered)
- **Character**: Enormous, cracking, reverberant — the half-time anchor
- **Synthesis approach**: Layered: acoustic snare + clap + white noise burst + tonal body
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–10 kHz (wide, impactful)
- **Processing**: Parallel compression, saturation, plate reverb, transient enhancement
- **Role in mix**: Beat 3 anchor — the single most important drum hit

#### Laser/Zap FX
- **Character**: Quick, pitched, sci-fi — accents between bass notes
- **Synthesis approach**: Fast pitch envelope on sine/saw (high → low in <50ms)
- **Frequency range**: 500 Hz–8 kHz
- **Processing**: Distortion, short delay, stereo ping-pong
- **Role in mix**: Accent — fills gaps between bass hits, adds energy

## Tearout

### Signature Sounds

#### Granular Noise Bass
- **Character**: Chaotic, unpredictable, granular — like machinery breaking apart
- **Synthesis approach**: Granular synthesis (sample fragmentation), or heavy granular processing of existing bass
- **Frequency range**: 60 Hz–8 kHz (full spectrum chaos)
- **Processing**: Extreme distortion, bit reduction, granular delay, multiband saturation
- **Role in mix**: PRIMARY element — replaces traditional bass with textural chaos

#### Industrial Slam
- **Character**: Massive impact, metallic, mechanical — like a hydraulic press
- **Synthesis approach**: Layered impacts: metal slam sample + sine sub hit + noise burst
- **Frequency range**: 30 Hz–6 kHz (full weight)
- **Processing**: Extreme limiting, saturation, short reverb for metallic character
- **Role in mix**: Rhythmic punctuation — replaces traditional kick/snare in some sections

#### Distorted Sub
- **Character**: Overwhelming low-end — distorted, clipping, physical
- **Synthesis approach**: Sine wave into heavy waveshaping/clipping distortion
- **Frequency range**: 30–100 Hz (pure sub, distortion adds harmonics up to 500 Hz)
- **Processing**: Waveshaper, clipper, mono, sidechain, no filter
- **Role in mix**: Foundation — the physical pressure that makes tearout feel dangerous

#### Glitch Texture
- **Character**: Digital artifacts, buffer glitches, stutters — broken electronics aesthetic
- **Synthesis approach**: Buffer override effects, stutter edits, spectral freezing
- **Frequency range**: Full spectrum (varies)
- **Processing**: Bit crush, sample rate reduction, resonant comb filters
- **Role in mix**: Texture layer — fills space between slams with digital chaos

#### Horror FX / Atmosphere
- **Character**: Dark, cinematic, unsettling — horror movie sound design
- **Synthesis approach**: Reversed cymbals, spectral processing of screams, dark ambient textures
- **Frequency range**: 100 Hz–10 kHz (background atmosphere)
- **Processing**: Granular delay, pitch shifting, extreme reverb, slow modulation
- **Role in mix**: Atmosphere — creates emotional context (dread, chaos, horror)

#### Noise Riser
- **Character**: Escalating filtered noise building to explosion
- **Synthesis approach**: White/pink noise through opening band-pass filter with rising resonance
- **Frequency range**: Sweeps from 200 Hz → full spectrum
- **Processing**: Increasing distortion, resonance, volume automation
- **Role in mix**: Transition — signals incoming destruction

## UK Bass / Deep Dubstep

### Signature Sounds

#### Deep Sub-Bass
- **Character**: Massive, physical, meditative — felt in the body, barely audible on headphones
- **Synthesis approach**: Pure sine wave, sometimes with very gentle saturation for system translation
- **Frequency range**: 30–60 Hz (extremely low, physical territory)
- **Processing**: Mono, minimal processing, gentle saturation, sidechain to kick
- **Role in mix**: THE primary element — everything else is decoration around this weight

#### Dubstep Kick
- **Character**: Deep, round, minimal attack — felt more than heard
- **Synthesis approach**: Sine with moderate pitch envelope + light body layer
- **Frequency range**: 40–150 Hz (sub-heavy, minimal click)
- **Processing**: Minimal — maybe gentle saturation, HPF at 25 Hz
- **Role in mix**: Rhythmic anchor — interplays with sub-bass syncopation

#### Dub Echo Stab
- **Character**: Sparse chord or single note that trails into infinite echo
- **Synthesis approach**: Simple synth stab (saw, square) processed through tape delay emulation
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–3 kHz (mid-range, leaves sub clear)
- **Processing**: Tape delay (3–4 repeats, degrading), spring reverb, high-pass filter
- **Role in mix**: Texture and rhythm — the echoes create implied polyrhythm

#### Crackle / Vinyl Texture
- **Character**: Surface noise, crackle, warmth — analog atmosphere
- **Synthesis approach**: Vinyl crackle samples or generated noise with resonant filtering
- **Frequency range**: 2–12 kHz (background texture)
- **Processing**: High-pass filter, very low volume, gentle stereo spread
- **Role in mix**: Atmosphere — adds warmth, analog character, sound system culture reference

#### Sparse Percussion
- **Character**: Minimal, spacious, organic — tribal or processed acoustic sources
- **Synthesis approach**: Sampled percussion (bongo, rimshot, clave) or found sounds
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–6 kHz (mid-range, panned)
- **Processing**: Room reverb, slight pitch variation, velocity humanization
- **Role in mix**: Groove texture — minimal hits that define the 2-step pattern

#### Dark Pad / Atmosphere
- **Character**: Evolving, dark, immersive — creates the emotional landscape
- **Synthesis approach**: Granular or wavetable synth, slow modulation, filtered
- **Frequency range**: 100 Hz–4 kHz (low-mid atmosphere)
- **Processing**: Long reverb (5–8s), slow chorus, bandpass automation
- **Role in mix**: Background — fills space between sub hits without competing

## Future Bass

### Signature Sounds

#### Pitch-Bent Chord Synth
- **Character**: Bright, lush, pitch-wobbling chord stabs — THE future bass sound
- **Synthesis approach**: Supersaw (5–7 voices) with pitch envelope or manual pitch automation
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–8 kHz (wide chord voicings)
- **Processing**: OTT compression, sidechain, stereo widening, light reverb
- **Role in mix**: PRIMARY drop element — carries harmony with rhythmic pitch movement

#### Vocal Chops
- **Character**: Pitched, rhythmic, melodic — chopped vocal fragments as instruments
- **Synthesis approach**: Recorded vocal sliced, pitched to key, rhythmically sequenced
- **Frequency range**: 300 Hz–6 kHz (vocal range)
- **Processing**: Reverb, delay, formant preservation or shifting, tuning
- **Role in mix**: Melodic/rhythmic hook — creates singable moments from fragments

#### 808 Sub-Bass
- **Character**: Warm, sustained, round — trap-influenced low end
- **Synthesis approach**: Sine wave with gentle saturation, sustaining envelope
- **Frequency range**: 30–100 Hz
- **Processing**: Mono, sidechain to kick, soft clipping for system translation
- **Role in mix**: Low-end foundation — warm, supportive, not aggressive

#### Sparkle/Arp Layer
- **Character**: High, tinkling, crystalline — adds shimmer above chords
- **Synthesis approach**: Bell/FM synth, arpeggiated, high-passed
- **Frequency range**: 2–12 kHz (high sparkle)
- **Processing**: Stereo delay (dotted 8th), reverb, high-pass at 2 kHz
- **Role in mix**: Texture — adds brightness and movement above main chords

#### Warm Pad
- **Character**: Soft, evolving, background — emotional foundation
- **Synthesis approach**: Analog-modeled soft saw pad, slow filter movement
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–4 kHz (warm mid-range fill)
- **Processing**: Chorus, long reverb, low-pass at 5 kHz, gentle sidechain
- **Role in mix**: Harmonic fill — creates warmth without competing with lead chord stabs

#### Build Snare Roll
- **Character**: Escalating, tension-building — signals drop incoming
- **Synthesis approach**: Snare sample sequenced: 8ths → 16ths → 32nds with rising pitch
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–8 kHz
- **Processing**: Rising reverb amount, increasing volume, filter opening
- **Role in mix**: Transition device — builds tension into the pitch-bent drop

## Colour Bass

### Signature Sounds

#### Granular Texture Bass
- **Character**: Evolving, spectral, otherworldly — bass as living texture
- **Synthesis approach**: Granular synthesis (Granulator, GranulizR) on bass samples or synth renders
- **Frequency range**: 60 Hz–6 kHz (full range, evolving spectrally)
- **Processing**: Spectral effects, multiband granular, slow modulation, convolution reverb
- **Role in mix**: PRIMARY element — the textural bass IS the genre

#### Spectral Pad
- **Character**: Shimmering, evolving, rich — fills harmonic space with living color
- **Synthesis approach**: Spectral resynthesis, additive synthesis, or wavetable with spectral morph
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–10 kHz (wide, immersive)
- **Processing**: Spectral effects, convolution reverb, slow LFO modulation, stereo spread
- **Role in mix**: Harmonic foundation — provides the "colour" backdrop

#### Glitch Percussion
- **Character**: Organic, irregular, tuned — percussion as melodic texture
- **Synthesis approach**: Granular processing of acoustic samples, spectral slicing
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–8 kHz (tuned, melodic)
- **Processing**: Pitch-tracking, granular delay, resonant filters, reverb
- **Role in mix**: Rhythmic texture — provides groove without traditional drum sounds

#### FM Bell / Tonal Accent
- **Character**: Pure, bright, crystalline — tonal punctuation in textural landscape
- **Synthesis approach**: FM synthesis (2 operators), high ratio for bell-like harmonics
- **Frequency range**: 500 Hz–8 kHz (bright, cutting)
- **Processing**: Long reverb, stereo delay, gentle saturation for warmth
- **Role in mix**: Accent — provides clear tonal reference within textural complexity

#### Evolving Wavetable Lead
- **Character**: Morphing, alive, otherworldly — timbre changes continuously
- **Synthesis approach**: Wavetable synth (Serum, Vital) with automated wavetable position
- **Frequency range**: 150 Hz–6 kHz (full mid-range, continuously evolving)
- **Processing**: Multiband processing, formant filter, chorus, granular delay
- **Role in mix**: Lead — carries melodic content with ever-changing timbre

#### Atmospheric Reverb Wash
- **Character**: Infinite, spacious, immersive — creates depth and dimension
- **Synthesis approach**: Long reverb on short impulses; convolution with unusual IRs (caves, metal tanks)
- **Frequency range**: Full spectrum (reverb tail fills all frequencies)
- **Processing**: Extreme reverb (8–15s), freeze, pitch shifting, granular
- **Role in mix**: Space — creates the three-dimensional sound world colour bass lives in

## Hybrid Trap

### Signature Sounds

#### Distorted 808
- **Character**: Heavy, saturated, aggressive — trap 808 with dubstep weight
- **Synthesis approach**: Sine sub-oscillator with heavy waveshaping/saturation chain
- **Frequency range**: 30–300 Hz (sub fundamental + distortion harmonics)
- **Processing**: Camel Crusher, Decapitator, OTT, mono below 120 Hz
- **Role in mix**: Foundation — carries both bass and rhythmic kick function

#### Dubstep Mid-Bass (Growl)
- **Character**: Aggressive, modulated, mid-range — dubstep sound design on trap rhythm
- **Synthesis approach**: FM/wavetable synthesis, LFO on filter/wavetable position
- **Frequency range**: 150 Hz–4 kHz (mid-range dominance)
- **Processing**: OTT, multiband distortion, formant filter, sidechain to 808
- **Role in mix**: Drop accent — heavy bass hits between 808 notes

#### Trap Hi-Hat (Processed)
- **Character**: Crisp, rapid-fire, detailed — 16th/32nd rolls with variation
- **Synthesis approach**: Sample-based (808 hat or custom metallic sample)
- **Frequency range**: 4–14 kHz
- **Processing**: Pitch automation on rolls, volume velocity, saturation for grit
- **Role in mix**: Groove driver — trap hi-hat patterns carry the rhythmic energy

#### Impact / Riser
- **Character**: Massive, building, explosive — hybrid of trap and dubstep FX approaches
- **Synthesis approach**: Layered: noise sweep + sub hit + reverse cymbal + pitch riser
- **Frequency range**: Full spectrum
- **Processing**: Increasing distortion/volume, reverb automation, stereo widening
- **Role in mix**: Transition — marks section changes with maximum impact

#### Brass / Horn Stab
- **Character**: Aggressive, short, punchy — festival trap signature
- **Synthesis approach**: Sampled brass with saturation, or synthesized (detuned saws, formant shaped)
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–4 kHz (punchy mid-range)
- **Processing**: Saturation, transient shaping, short reverb, sidechain
- **Role in mix**: Accent — rhythmic stabs between bass hits, builds hype

#### Vocal Drop
- **Character**: Short vocal sample signaling the drop — "let's go," countdown, etc.
- **Synthesis approach**: Sampled vocal, often pitched/effected
- **Frequency range**: 300 Hz–5 kHz (vocal presence range)
- **Processing**: Distortion, reverb throw, delay, filtering
- **Role in mix**: Transition marker — human element signaling energy shift

## Wave

### Signature Sounds

#### Atmospheric Pad (Primary)
- **Character**: Vast, evolving, melancholic — the emotional core of wave music
- **Synthesis approach**: Analog-modeled synth, multiple detuned oscillators, slow modulation
- **Frequency range**: 100 Hz–6 kHz (wide, immersive, enveloping)
- **Processing**: Long reverb (5–10s), slow chorus, gentle high-pass, stereo spread
- **Role in mix**: PRIMARY element — creates the emotional landscape

#### 808 Bass (Clean)
- **Character**: Clean, deep, sustained — trap 808 without the aggression
- **Synthesis approach**: Sine wave with long sustain, gentle saturation for presence
- **Frequency range**: 30–100 Hz (clean sub territory)
- **Processing**: Mono, light saturation, sidechain to kick, sustain envelope
- **Role in mix**: Low-end anchor — provides weight without dominating the atmosphere

#### Reverbed Clap
- **Character**: Distant, washed, dreamlike — snare that dissolves into space
- **Synthesis approach**: Clap sample with extreme reverb processing
- **Frequency range**: 200 Hz–6 kHz (mid-range, reverb tail extends higher)
- **Processing**: Long reverb (3–5s), pre-delay, high-pass the reverb, compress lightly
- **Role in mix**: Rhythmic anchor (beat 3) — the hit matters less than the reverb tail

#### Pitched Vocal Sample
- **Character**: Ghostly, emotional, ethereal — fragments of humanity in electronic space
- **Synthesis approach**: Vocal sample pitched up/down, stretched, reverbed into atmosphere
- **Frequency range**: 300 Hz–8 kHz (vocal range, pitch-shifted)
- **Processing**: Pitch shift, granular stretch, long reverb, delay, gentle filtering
- **Role in mix**: Emotional texture — brief human moments in the atmospheric landscape

#### Shimmer Delay Texture
- **Character**: Crystalline, infinite, rising — delay that pitch-shifts upward each repeat
- **Synthesis approach**: Any source (pad, bell, vocal) through shimmer reverb/delay
- **Frequency range**: 1–12 kHz (builds upward with each repeat)
- **Processing**: Shimmer reverb (octave-up per repeat), long feedback, stereo spread
- **Role in mix**: Atmosphere enhancement — adds vertical depth and ethereal quality

#### Trap Hi-Hat (Sparse)
- **Character**: Soft, filtered, distant — trap hats stripped of aggression
- **Synthesis approach**: 808 hat sample, filtered and reverbed
- **Frequency range**: 4–10 kHz (softened compared to trap)
- **Processing**: Low-pass filter at 10 kHz, gentle reverb, velocity variation
- **Role in mix**: Subtle groove — provides minimal rhythmic structure without hardness
