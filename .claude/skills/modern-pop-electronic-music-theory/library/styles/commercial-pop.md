# Commercial Pop — progression / melody / voicing library

**Canonical example key:** C major  
**Starter BPM:** 112 [HEUR]  
**Primary function:** Song-first; chorus intelligibility, topline space, smooth voice leading.

## Progression seeds [HEUR]

- **I–V6–vi–IV** → `C – G/B – Am – F` — stable verse/chorus loop; smooth bass motion.
- **vi–IV–I–V** → `Am – F – C – G` — minor-emotional loop with major payoff.
- **I–vi–IV–V** → `C – Am – F – G` — direct functional lift into V.
- **IV–I–V–vi** → `F – C – G – Am` — starts away from tonic; useful for chorus lift.
- **I–iii–IV–iv** → `C – Em – F – Fm` — borrowed iv for bittersweet color.
- **vi–ii–IV–V** → `Am – Dm – F – G` — pre-chorus-like forward motion.

## Canonical MIDI starter

The included clips are original templates, not track transcriptions.

- `chords.mid` — four-bar harmonic frame or vamp.
- `bass.mid` — style-oriented bass rhythm.
- `motif.mid` — intentionally simple motif seed.

## What to vary first

1. Top note and lyric/melody fit.
2. Harmonic rhythm between verse/pre/chorus.
3. Bass inversion/approach into section boundaries.

## Validation

- Can the hook be recognized on a plain piano/sine sound?
- Does each section have a distinct function without requiring a new progression?
- Are bass and chord registers separated?
- Is at least one musical parameter intentionally repeated?
