# Bass House — progression / melody / voicing library

**Canonical example key:** F minor  
**Starter BPM:** 126 [HEUR]  
**Primary function:** Sparse harmony, rhythmic bass call-response, short stabs, strong negative space.

## Progression seeds [HEUR]

- **i–♭VII–♭VI–♭VII** → `Fm – Eb – Db – Eb` — modal loop; leaves room for bass hook.
- **i–♭VI–♭VII–i** → `Fm – Db – Eb – Fm` — direct minor club loop.
- **i–iv–♭VI–V** → `Fm – Bbm – Db – C` — stronger functional tension.
- **i–♭III–♭VII–♭VI** → `Fm – Ab – Eb – Db` — anthemic minor contour.
- **i pedal + upper ♭VI/♭VII** → `Fm – Db/F – Eb/F – Fm` — pedal-root pressure for drop design.
- **i–♭VII** → `Fm – Eb` — two-chord minimal frame for sound-design-led drops.

## Canonical MIDI starter

The included clips are original templates, not track transcriptions.

- `chords.mid` — four-bar harmonic frame or vamp.
- `bass.mid` — style-oriented bass rhythm.
- `motif.mid` — intentionally simple motif seed.

## What to vary first

1. Remove chords before adding more.
2. Bass rhythm/silence pattern.
3. One-note or two-note synth response.

## Validation

- Can the hook be recognized on a plain piano/sine sound?
- Does each section have a distinct function without requiring a new progression?
- Are bass and chord registers separated?
- Is at least one musical parameter intentionally repeated?
