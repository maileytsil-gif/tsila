# Dance Pop / Electronic Pop — progression / melody / voicing library

**Canonical example key:** F# minor  
**Starter BPM:** 124 [HEUR]  
**Primary function:** Emotional loop + strong rhythmic identity; chorus/drop hybrid.

## Progression seeds [HEUR]

- **i–VI–III–VII** → `F#m – D – A – E` — wide-open EDM/pop loop.
- **VI–III–VII–i** → `D – A – E – F#m` — delays tonic; useful for lift.
- **i–III–VII–VI** → `F#m – A – E – D` — descending emotional contour.
- **i–VII–VI–VII** → `F#m – E – D – E` — compact dance loop.
- **III–VII–i–VI** → `A – E – F#m – D` — major opening with minor center.
- **i–iv–VI–V** → `F#m – Bm – D – C#` — stronger dominant pull via harmonic-minor V.

## Canonical MIDI starter

The included clips are original templates, not track transcriptions.

- `chords.mid` — four-bar harmonic frame or vamp.
- `bass.mid` — style-oriented bass rhythm.
- `motif.mid` — intentionally simple motif seed.

## What to vary first

1. Offbeat vs sustained chord articulation.
2. Chorus/drop register lift.
3. Bass octave and note length around the kick.

## Validation

- Can the hook be recognized on a plain piano/sine sound?
- Does each section have a distinct function without requiring a new progression?
- Are bass and chord registers separated?
- Is at least one musical parameter intentionally repeated?
