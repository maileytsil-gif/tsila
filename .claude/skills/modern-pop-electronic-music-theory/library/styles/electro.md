# Electro / Electro House — progression / melody / voicing library

**Canonical example key:** E minor  
**Starter BPM:** 128 [HEUR]  
**Primary function:** Tighter harmonic tension, aggressive rhythmic motifs, synth/bass dialogue.

## Progression seeds [HEUR]

- **i–VI–VII–V** → `Em – C – D – B` — harmonic-minor V creates stronger return.
- **i–VI–III–VII** → `Em – C – G – D` — wide, anthem-friendly loop.
- **i–VII–VI–V** → `Em – D – C – B` — descending tension staircase.
- **i–iv–VI–V** → `Em – Am – C – B` — functional minor motion.
- **i–♭II** → `Em – F` — Phrygian-flavored two-chord tension.
- **i pedal + upper VI/VII** → `Em – C/E – D/E – Em` — pedal-root pressure for synth-driven drops.

## Canonical MIDI starter

The included clips are original templates, not track transcriptions.

- `chords.mid` — four-bar harmonic frame or vamp.
- `bass.mid` — style-oriented bass rhythm.
- `motif.mid` — intentionally simple motif seed.

## What to vary first

1. Tension note and dominant treatment.
2. Synth/bass octave dialogue.
3. Motif rhythm before adding chord complexity.

## Validation

- Can the hook be recognized on a plain piano/sine sound?
- Does each section have a distinct function without requiring a new progression?
- Are bass and chord registers separated?
- Is at least one musical parameter intentionally repeated?
