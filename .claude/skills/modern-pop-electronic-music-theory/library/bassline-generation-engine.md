# Bassline generation engine

Bassline design is the meeting point of **harmony + groove + low-end ownership**.

## Bassline roles
- `FOUNDATION`: roots/fifths establish harmony.
- `COUNTER-RHYTHM`: pitches are simple; rhythm creates identity.
- `HOOK`: bassline is a primary motif.
- `PEDAL`: root remains stable while upper harmony moves.
- `APPROACH`: short scale/chromatic approach notes lead into chord changes.
- `CALL_RESPONSE`: bass phrases alternate with stabs/lead/vocal.

## Style defaults [HEUR]
- Pop: roots + occasional inversion/approach.
- Dance/Future House: offbeat root/fifth patterns; keep space for kick.
- Bass House: fewer harmonic notes, more rhythmic/timbral call-response.
- Minimal/Tech House: one/two-note cells, syncopation and note length are primary.
- Electro: stronger octave/fifth movement and deliberate tension notes.

## Hard constraint
Before generating notes, consult the low-end skill and decide whether **KICK OWNS SUB**, **BASS OWNS SUB**, **ALTERNATING**, or **KICK+RUMBLE**. Composition and mixing must agree.
