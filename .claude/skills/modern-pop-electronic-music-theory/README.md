# Modern Pop & Electronic Music Theory — v11

The v11 extension turns the v10 theory system into a playable **progression / voicing / bassline / melody library** for Ableton Live 12.

New material:
- six style libraries;
- structured progression, voicing, melody-cell and bassline-cell JSON;
- 18 original MIDI starter clips;
- exact MIDI-number convention;
- transposition and variation engines;
- Live 12 Scale Mode / MIDI Tools workflow;
- originality guardrails.

All exact templates are `[HEUR]`; they are original exercises, not transcriptions. Official Ableton/Berklee behavior and theory are `[DOC]`.
