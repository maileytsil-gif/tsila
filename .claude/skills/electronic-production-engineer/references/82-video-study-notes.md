# Video-study notes

Use these as workflow observations cross-checked with manuals. Re-verify version-sensitive UI details.

## Ableton / dance-music mixdown
Recurring lesson: establish drums and bass first, then synth depth/FX, then vocal and final bus. Sidechain is both a space-making and rhythmic device.

## Waves L4 demonstrations
Compare limiter modes at matched loudness. Listen to transient preservation, low-end behavior and distortion instead of selecting a mode from genre name alone. Treat adaptive clipping, upward compression and limiting as interacting stages.

## oeksound soothe3 demonstrations
Use Delta/removed-signal audition to hear what is being suppressed. External sidechain can create frequency-selective space without ducking the entire target.

## Native Instruments Maschine 3
Mixer/group processing and Perform FX are useful creatively, but Ableton remains the preferred final arrangement/mix source of truth. Euclidean/probability workflows are especially valuable for evolving Minimal, Techno and Afro percussion.

## Akai APC64
The useful production loop is hardware experimentation -> probability/mutate/step sequencing -> commit useful material to Ableton clips -> detailed edit/arrangement in Live. Touch faders are strongest when mapped to stable semantic Rack macros.

## Komplete Kontrol / NKS
NKS is valuable for browsing and meaningful parameter pages. For automation/AI control, prefer parameters exposed to Ableton/LOM rather than depending on graphical control.

## FabFilter / Dan Worrall educational material
Mid/Side is not a synonym for widening. Compression, saturation and loudness should be judged by audible function and gain-matched comparison. A good mix may require very little mastering.

## SAE public material
The durable lesson is methodology: critical listening, acoustics, metering, signal flow, synthesis/sampling, advanced processing, multiple mix perspectives and mastering/QC.
