# Genre router

Choose DNA by musical behavior, not only by a label.

- **House**: groove, musical repetition, vocal/chord identity, warmth, return-of-groove drops.
- **Tech House**: compact kick/bass, vocal hook, dry/precise drums, tension-gap-drop contrast.
- **Minimal / Deep Tech**: micro-variation, negative space, elastic bass, ghost percussion, subtle bridges.
- **Techno**: timbral evolution, hypnosis, kick/rumble foundation, long tension arcs, automation-heavy transitions.
- **Bass House**: call-and-response bass design, strong transients, contrast, edits/fills, aggressive but controlled peak density.
- **Melodic House / Techno**: emotional motif, long automation, depth/reverb, break as a composition inside the composition.
- **Afro House / Afro Tech**: polyrhythmic organic percussion, humanized timing/velocity, round low-end, vocal/melodic identity, groove continuity through breaks.
- **Melodic Dubstep**: emotional harmonic arc, wide upper layers, stable sub, vocal/lead identity, contrast-heavy builds and call-response drops.
- **Liquid DnB**: musicality/soul, fluent drums, clean supportive sub, chords/vocal/atmosphere, gradual arrangement and restrained mastering.
- **Minimal / Deep DnB**: drum/bass funk, negative space, rim/snare identity, sparse textures, small-note-count bass and micro-edited transitions.
- **US DnB crossover profile**: optional regional/crossover layer blending DnB with bass-music/dubstep/songwriting; never assume this profile for all US producers.

For hybrids state a weighting, e.g. `70% Afro House groove / 30% Melodic Techno harmonic arc`.
