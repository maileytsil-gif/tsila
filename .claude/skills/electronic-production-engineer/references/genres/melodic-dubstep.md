# Melodic Dubstep DNA

- Emotional chord/melody arc owns the song; heavy bass supports contrast rather than replacing musical writing.
- Breakdowns: vocal/chords/pads/atmosphere with long tails and harmonic development.
- Builds: rhythmic density, risers and automation; preserve a clear pre-drop gap.
- Drops: sub + chord/lead identity + contrasting bass phrases. Use rests and call-response.
- Sound design: wide harmonic layers above a stable center/sub; use resampling and formant movement for robotic accents.
- Second drop should change orchestration, bass response, octave, rhythm or harmony—not only get louder.
