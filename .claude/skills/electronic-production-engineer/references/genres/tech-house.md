# Tech House DNA

## Four references
- FISHER — Losing It: economy, hook, negative space, strong tension/drop contrast.
- Chris Lake — Turn Off The Lights: precise kick/bass interlock and vocal/bass contrast.
- John Summit — Deep End: vocal chop integrated into groove.
- Cloonee — Sun Goes Down: long DJ arrangement, small fills and low-mid discipline.

## Arrangement DNA
Typical macro-flow: groove/drop -> micro-gap/fill -> variation -> breakdown -> build -> pre-drop gap -> drop. Build energy by substitution as well as addition: remove hats/claps when rolls or risers enter. The final beat or half-bar of silence can be more powerful than another layer.

## Mix priorities
Short/controlled kick and bass envelopes, clean low-mid, tight transients, dry center with selective space. Solve kick/bass timing before aggressive EQ.
