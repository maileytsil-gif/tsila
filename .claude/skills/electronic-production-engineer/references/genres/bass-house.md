# Bass House DNA

## Four references
- Skrillex & Habstrakt — Chicken Soup: call/response bass, sub + mid-bass layers, silence as punctuation.
- JOYRYDE — IM GONE: aggressive drum language, short edits and resampling energy.
- Knock2 — dashstar*: modern transient density, compact bass design and rapid fills.
- AC Slater — Bass Inside: groove-first Night Bass root with less maximalism.

## Arrangement DNA
Drops are conversations, not one loop: phrase A -> gap -> phrase B -> fill -> A2 -> B2. Break creates large contrast; build uses pitch/riser/roll/automation; a gap precedes the impact. Drop 2 should change response, rhythm, register, texture or fill language.

## Mix priorities
Separate SUB / MID BASS / UPPER HARMONICS / ATTACK-NOISE. Control peaks progressively. Preserve sub stability while aggressively shaping upper layers. Harshness after distortion is a candidate for soothe3 or dynamic EQ, not a reason to dull the whole bass.

## Numbers
Evidence tags: see `../10-genre-router.md`.
- **Tempo**: Beatport Bass House Top 100: 126 (29 tracks), 128 (27), 125 (17), 127 (13) [DOC WhatBPM 2023]. Chicken Soup, IM GONE and dashstar* all ≈ 126 [DOC, Spotify estimate]. Default **128** festival, **126** when the swung/G-house groove matters [HEUR].
- **Swing**: two schools, 50–53 % (straight festival) vs 55–65 % (Night Bass / UK bassline) [COMM]; ask the sub-style before setting it.
- **Keys/modes**: most frequent roots E, F♯, F, E♭ [DOC WhatBPM, auto-keys]. Natural minor or Phrygian (♭2 → 1 riffs); the drop is mono-tonal, the riff is the harmony, i–VII at most; riff intervals minor 2nd, minor 3rd, 5th, octave; the pattern repeats over 2 bars and varies by LFO rate, not notes [COMM].
- **Sections** (club, 160 bars = 5:00 at 128): intro A 16 · intro B 16 · build 16 · drop 1 32 · breakdown 16 · build 16 (or 8) · drop 2 32 · outro 16 [COMM Fearvox; CALC]. Festival edit, 3–4 min: intro 8 · build 16 · drop A 16–32 · mini-break 8 (JefroB allows 4) · drop B 16–32 · outro 8 [COMM]. In the drop, a 2-bar variation every 8; hard cut 1–2 bars before the drop, silence on beat 4 of the last bar [COMM]. Mean Beatport length 4:08 [DOC].
- **Low end**: dedicated mono sine sub 30–60 Hz (HPF 25, LPF 80); mid bass 80–500 Hz (HPF 80–100), top 500 Hz–3 kHz (HPF 300); kick HPF below 40 Hz, cut 80–100 Hz only if it fights the sub; tight sidechain on all three layers, bass bus 4–6 dB gain reduction [COMM; HEUR]. No percussion competing with the bass [COMM].
