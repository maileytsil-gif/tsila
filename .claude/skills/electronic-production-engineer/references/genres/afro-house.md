# Afro House / Afro Tech DNA

## Four references
- Black Coffee feat. Bucie — Superman: deep organic groove, space, vocal and restrained bass.
- Caiiro — The Akan: denser tribal/Afro-Tech percussion and dark tension.
- Shimza & Mikhaela Faye — Calling Out Your Name: club energy, vocal center and long progression.
- Adam Port / Stryv / Keinemusik — Move: sparse modern arrangement, round bass, carefully selected percussion and hook vocal.

## Groove DNA
Kick 4/4 is only the grid. Groove comes from interlocking shaker/conga/tom/djembe/organic layers with differentiated velocity, timing, probability, pan and depth. Do not quantize every layer identically.

## Arrangement DNA
Break often preserves shaker, light percussion, vocal, pad or piano so the body still feels the pulse. Bridge tension is frequently organic and subtle rather than white-noise-heavy. Drop impact comes from the complete polyrhythmic network returning with kick/bass. Drop 2 may need only one new percussion layer, octave or theme variation.

## Harmony
Use extended/suspended voicings when appropriate: 7ths, 9ths, sus shapes and open voicings. Leave space for rhythm.

## Mix priorities
Round kick, stable bass, separation/depth between percussion layers, vocal clarity, controlled but spacious atmosphere, dynamics that still breathe on a club system.
