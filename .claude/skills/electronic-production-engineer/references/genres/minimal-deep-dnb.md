# Minimal / Deep Drum & Bass DNA

- Drums + bass are the core composition. Negative space is intentional.
- Kick, snare/rim and break groove require high individual quality because few layers hide weaknesses.
- Bass can use very few notes; timbre, sustain and rhythm create development.
- Textures, dub space and sparse stabs provide depth without filling the spectrum.
- Bridges are often 2-8 bar disruptions, gated breaks, sub cuts or sparse atmospheres rather than festival buildups.
- References: Alix Perez, Skeptical, DLR and Break as production-study anchors; do not copy signatures literally. No measured data on these artists exists in the repo (not verified).

## Numbers
Evidence tags: see `../10-genre-router.md`. DnB-wide data only; minimal/deep specifics are not verified in repo.
- **Tempo**: Beatport Drum & Bass Top 100: 174 (58 tracks), 176 (16), 172 (8) [DOC WhatBPM 2023]. Default **174**; half-time passages are felt at 87 [DOC-2].
- **Grid**: two-step, kick steps 1 and 11 (variant 1 and 8), snare 5 and 13 at v100–110, ghost snares 4, 10, 16 at v30–45, 8th hats v70–84; fill every 8 bars on snare steps 14–16; a sliced break layered at ghost level, high-passed at 200 Hz, so the kick/sub own the bottom; swing on the ghosts only [HEUR, studio grid]. DnB swing range 50–60 % [DOC-2].
- **Keys/modes**: 82 % minor, G minor 32 %, F minor 21 % [DOC GiantSteps, n = 38]. Pedal or one-chord vamps; in neurofunk the saturated reese is the harmony, one or two notes per 8 bars [DOC-2].
- **Sections**: DnB scheme intro 32 · build 16 · drop 1 64 · mid 16–32 · breakdown 32 · build 16 · drop 2 64 · outro 32 [COMM]; within it, 2/4/8-bar disruptions as above [HEUR]. 32 bars at 174 ≈ 44 s [CALC].
- **Low end**: one source owns the fundamental — mono sine sub, no detune, everything else high-passed around 100–120 Hz [DOC-2]; a detuned reese cannot hold the sub role, keep it above the sine [HEUR].
