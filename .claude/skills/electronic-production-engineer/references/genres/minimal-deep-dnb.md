# Minimal / Deep Drum & Bass DNA

- Drums + bass are the core composition. Negative space is intentional.
- Kick, snare/rim and break groove require high individual quality because few layers hide weaknesses.
- Bass can use very few notes; timbre, sustain and rhythm create development.
- Textures, dub space and sparse stabs provide depth without filling the spectrum.
- Bridges are often 2-8 bar disruptions, gated breaks, sub cuts or sparse atmospheres rather than festival buildups.
- References: Alix Perez, Skeptical, DLR and Break as production-study anchors; do not copy signatures literally.
