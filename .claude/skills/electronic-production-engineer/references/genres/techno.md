# Techno DNA

## Four references
- Charlotte de Witte — Doppler: kick-dominant foundation, hypnotic motif, filter/resonance evolution.
- Amelie Lens — Stay With Me: sustained energy through repetition and tension events.
- Adam Beyer & Bart Skils — Your Mind: long-form DJ development around a minimal hook.
- Enrico Sangiuliano — Moon Rocks: massive synthesis, saturation and progressive tension.

## Arrangement DNA
Bridges matter as much as breaks. Maintain hypnosis by changing timbre/density rather than composition every 8 bars. Breaks may hold atmosphere, sustained bass or motif; builds reintroduce pulse gradually. Drops often restore kick/rumble and full timbral density rather than introduce a new melody.

## Mix priorities
Kick/rumble/sub stability, transient control, controlled saturation, automation and mono compatibility. Keep distortion intentional and inspect the 100-400 Hz region for buildup.

## Numbers
Evidence tags: see `../10-genre-router.md`.
- **Tempo**: Beatport Techno (Peak Time / Driving): 132, 135, 130, 138, 134; Techno (Raw / Deep / Hypnotic): 140, 138, 136 [DOC WhatBPM 2023]. Charlotte de Witte originals in the repo's Spotify set sit at 134–140, Doppler (Edit) 135 [DOC, Spotify estimate]. Theory table: techno 120–150 (typical 130), Detroit 120–135, industrial 130–145 [DOC-2]. Default **132–135** peak-time, **128–130** Detroit/groovy [HEUR].
- **Swing**: mostly 50 % (straight), some Detroit ≈ 54 % [COMM]; Attack range 50–60 % [DOC-2]; the studio grid is unswung (≤ 0.01) [HEUR].
- **Keys/modes**: 82 % minor; C minor 18 %, A♭ minor 15 % [DOC GiantSteps, n = 34]; raw/hypnotic chart roots G, A♭, D [DOC WhatBPM, auto-keys]. Often no chord at all: drone or pedal, one-pitch bass with an occasional 5th/octave jump, movement by filter; stabs as parallel shapes (e.g. Em9 planed) [DOC-2]; minor or Phrygian when pitched [COMM].
- **Sections**: the unit of change is 16 or 32 bars, not 8 [COMM]. Additive long form: kick + hat 32 · +perc 32 · +bass 32 · +hook 32 · peak 32 · breakdown (kick out) 32 · return +1 element 64 · outro 32 = 288 bars ≈ 8:44 at 132 [COMM; CALC]. Mean Beatport length: peak-time 6:20, raw/hypnotic 5:44 [DOC].
- **Low end**: kick decay fills 40–60 % of a beat, sine sub + body + click, saturated; rumble = kick into a short dark reverb ducked by the kick; bass = the kick's tail, a one-note sub or rolling 16ths (`- x x x` per beat) [COMM]. Studio grid (130): kick v110–118, clap v88–96, straight 16th hats accented 90/60/70/60, clap dropped one bar in eight [HEUR].
