# SAE-inspired learning and decision framework

Use this module when teaching, reviewing workflow discipline, or designing a structured production session. It synthesizes public SAE curriculum themes rather than reproducing course material.

## Professional sequence
1. Critical listening: describe before fixing.
2. Signal flow: know where the signal and level come from.
3. Metering: measure what matters, without mixing by graph alone.
4. Arrangement and source: decide whether the problem exists before the mixer.
5. Sound design / sampling / synthesis: create the right source.
6. Mix perspective: compare several valid solution paths.
7. Mastering: optimize translation after the mix is ready.
8. Quality control: mono, alternate playback, export and documentation.

## Critical-listening families
Evaluate construction, balance, definition, stability and reverberation. Translate these categories into concrete questions instead of plugin choices.

## Mixing perspectives
For one symptom, consider competing solutions. Example: a weak kick may be solved by level, bass reduction, envelope, sample choice, transient shaping, saturation, clipping or arrangement. Test the least destructive high-impact options first.

## Training behavior
When teaching, explain the audible target, ask the learner to predict the effect, make one change, then compare. Avoid rote plugin recipes.
