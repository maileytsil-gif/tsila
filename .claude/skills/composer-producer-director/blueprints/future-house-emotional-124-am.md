# Blueprint — Emotional Future House / 124 BPM / A minor

## North Star
Bright/dark contrast: emotional chords and memorable melodic contour in the break, then a compact offbeat/bouncy bass-led payoff.

## Core
- Theory: A minor, simple degree loop with smooth inversions.
- Melody: reserve highest register for the payoff/refrain moment.
- Low end: kick short, bass/sub alternates around kick; verify ownership in the actual pattern.
- Sound: pluck/stab as core, airy pad as support, simple riser and impact.
- Mix: preserve transient and chord clarity; do not widen the sub.

## Variation
Second payoff changes one major axis: response melody, upper-layer rhythm or timbral macro — not the entire progression.
