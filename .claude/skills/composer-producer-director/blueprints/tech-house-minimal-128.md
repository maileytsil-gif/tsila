# Blueprint — Minimal / Tech House / 128 BPM

## North Star
Groove-first track with very few elements, a short vocal/rhythmic hook, rolling bass and deliberate negative space.

## Routing
- Theory: minimal harmonic motion or pedal logic.
- Composition: kick/bass/percussion rhythm before melodic layers.
- Kick/Bass: choose explicit ownership; keep envelopes compact.
- Sound design: one stab/chop or small motif only if it increases identity.
- Arrangement: variation by subtraction, fills, mute events, register and texture.
- Mix: prioritize timing, envelope and selection before corrective EQ.
