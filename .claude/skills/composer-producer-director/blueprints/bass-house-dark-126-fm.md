# Blueprint — Dark Bass House / 126 BPM / F minor

This is an original planning example, not a song transcription.

## North Star
Dark, dry Bass House with a syncopated call-response bass hook, short punch kick, restrained verse/break and an emotional harmonic lift before a hard return.

## Routing
1. Theory: choose F minor degrees and a compact 2–4 chord break loop.
2. Composition: build a 4–8 bar drop skeleton around kick/bass/silence.
3. Kick: short club/Bass House kick; do not let a long tail own the sub.
4. Bass: `BASS OWNS SUB`; clean sub + distorted/moving mid-bass layers.
5. Sound design: metallic or vocal-like answer, short stab, minimal pad in break, riser/impact only where needed.
6. Arrangement: emotional break → subtraction → pre-drop silence → dry payoff.
7. Mix: mono low band, sidechain/ducking only as much as required, width in upper layers.
8. Bridge: map `MOTION/BITE/TONE/SPACE` and automate only after the musical curve is approved.

## Gates
Do not proceed to full FX polish until the neutral-sound groove and hook work at low volume.
