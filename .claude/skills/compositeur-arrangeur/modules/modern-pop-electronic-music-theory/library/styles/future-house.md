# Future House — progression / melody / voicing library

**Canonical example key:** A minor  
**Starter BPM:** 124 [HEUR]  
**Primary function:** Bright/bouncy harmonic stabs with emotional minor center and clear drop hook.

## Progression seeds [HEUR]

- **i–VI–III–VII** → `Am – F – C – G` — classic emotional/bouncy loop.
- **i–III–VII–VI** → `Am – C – G – F` — topline-friendly descending energy.
- **VI–VII–i–III** → `F – G – Am – C` — builds toward tonic rather than starting on it.
- **i–VII–VI–VII** → `Am – G – F – G` — compact hook loop.
- **i–iv–VI–VII** → `Am – Dm – F – G` — more functional movement.
- **III–VII–i–VI** → `C – G – Am – F` — major-fronted crossover.

## Canonical MIDI starter

The included clips are original templates, not track transcriptions.

- `chords.mid` — four-bar harmonic frame or vamp.
- `bass.mid` — style-oriented bass rhythm.
- `motif.mid` — intentionally simple motif seed.

## What to vary first

1. Chord-stab inversion and top note.
2. Offbeat bass pattern.
3. Call/response between stab and lead/pluck.

## Validation

- Can the hook be recognized on a plain piano/sine sound?
- Does each section have a distinct function without requiring a new progression?
- Are bass and chord registers separated?
- Is at least one musical parameter intentionally repeated?
