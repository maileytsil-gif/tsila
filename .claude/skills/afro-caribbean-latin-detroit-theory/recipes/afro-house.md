# Recipe — Afro House foundation

1. Lock 4/4 pulse or chosen Afro-House variant.
2. Add a sparse high clock.
3. Add one mid-percussion answer voice.
4. Add a second voice with a contrasting cycle/accent pattern.
5. Bass enters in gaps rather than doubling kick.
6. Use 1–2 chord centers, sus/add9/modal colors `[HEUR]`.
7. Vocal call/response or atmospheric phrase.
8. Arrange by subtract/add and timbral evolution.

Do not label the result authentic to a specific African tradition unless that tradition was actually studied for the project.
